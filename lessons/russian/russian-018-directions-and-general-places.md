# Russian 018 — Directions and General Place Phrases

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–017 and the immediately preceding lessons
**Author:** Manus AI

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to use the general direction words **налево**, **направо**, and **прямо**, then ask and answer a short, fictional question about a nearby general place.

## Retrieval practice

1. Translate **Я не учусь дома.**
2. Ask informally, “Are you at the library?”
3. Produce one short future sentence with **буду**.
4. From the previous C lesson, name one checked boundary that makes binary data processing safer.

## Core pattern

Direction words are often used as fixed chunks. Learn **налево** “to the left,” **направо** “to the right,” and **прямо** “straight ahead.” They describe direction; they do not require you to disclose or request a real location.

| Russian | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Налево. | To the left. |
| Направо. | To the right. |
| Прямо. | Straight ahead. |
| Библиотека направо. | The library is to the right. |

## Concise explanation

Start from the complete model phrase. Keep previously learned words in their established forms and change only the new element. This reduces errors and makes later review easier. Russian questions can keep statement word order; use a question mark and question intonation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Где библиотека?
> *Where is the library?*
>
> **Б:** Библиотека направо.
> *The library is to the right.*
>
> **А:** А парк?
> *And the park?*
>
> **Б:** Парк налево.
> *The park is to the left.*
