## Core pattern
Use although/expectation + contrasting result. In Russian, contrast is often marked by хотя (although), но (but), or however/однако (however). Sentences can be combined into a compound sentence or separated.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я хотел прийти, хотя погода была хорошая. | I wanted to come, although the weather was good. |
| Я хотел прийти, но опоздал на поезд. | I wanted to come, but I missed the train. |
| Я хотел прийти; однако поезд опоздал. | I wanted to come; however, the train was delayed. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use хотя to introduce the expectation or concession ("although"), and use но or однако to introduce the contrast ("but"/"however"). Meaning: хотя sets up an idea that might make the second clause surprising; но/однако connect the actual contrasting outcome. Limitation/common confusion: хотя can appear at the start or middle of a sentence and is followed by a clause in finite form (no special case changes). Однако is slightly more formal than но and often appears after a semicolon or at clause start. Do not confuse хотя with потому что ("because") — хотя expresses contrast, not cause.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я думал, встреча будет короткой.
*I thought the meeting would be short.*

Б: Хотя время было ограничено, обсуждение затянулось.
*Although time was limited, the discussion dragged on.*

А: Но все успели высказать своё мнение.
*But everyone managed to express their opinion.*

Б: Однако решение приняли не сразу.
*However, they did not make a decision immediately.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite this pair using хотя: "Она ожидала найти книгу. Книги не было." Provide one Russian sentence and its English translation.
2. Combine these two sentences with но: "Мы купили билеты. Самолёт не взлетел." Produce one Russian sentence and translation.
3. Use однако at the start of the second clause: "Он приготовился к презентации. Результаты были другие." Provide the combined Russian sentence and translation.
4. Translate into Russian: "Although the test was easy, some students were nervous." Keep wording neutral.
5. Replace хотя with но in your answer to task 4 and note any change in emphasis (one short English sentence).

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph describing a planned trip that did not go as expected. Use хотя at least once and use either но or однако at least once to show the contrast between expectation and outcome. Keep tone neutral and factual. Provide an English translation below each Russian sentence.

Example requirements:
- 6–10 sentences in Russian (Cyrillic).
- Every sentence must have an adjacent English translation.
- Use хотя once and но or однако once.
