# Russian 016 — Wants with `хочу` and Infinitives

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–007 and the immediately preceding lessons in this expanded sequence

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to express simple wants with `хочу + infinitive`, ask an informal yes/no question with `хочешь`, and form clear negative responses.

## Core pattern

Use **я хочу + infinitive** for a simple current wish or preference: **Я хочу читать.** Use **ты хочешь?** for informal “do you want?” The infinitive remains in its dictionary form.

| Russian | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Я хочу читать. | I want to read. |
| Я хочу учиться. | I want to study. |
| Ты хочешь работать? | Do you want to work? — informal |
| Я не хочу работать. | I do not want to work. |

Read each Russian example aloud twice. Then cover the English and produce the meaning from the Russian. Keep the whole phrase together rather than translating one word at a time.

## Concise explanation

Start with the model sentence in the table. Identify the word that carries the new grammar pattern, then keep familiar words such as **я**, **не**, **читать**, **учиться**, **дома**, and **вечером** in their established forms. This reduces the chance of changing several grammatical features at once.

Russian word order can change for context and emphasis, but the neutral beginner order remains dependable: time or subject, then the verb or grammar phrase, then the remaining information. Use a question mark and Russian question intonation for a yes/no question. Do not invent an English-style auxiliary.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Ты хочешь читать книгу?
> *Do you want to read a book?*
>
> **Б:** Да, я хочу читать книгу.
> *Yes, I want to read a book.*
>
> **А:** Ты хочешь учиться вечером?
> *Do you want to study in the evening?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, я не хочу учиться вечером.
> *No, I do not want to study in the evening.*

Cover the English translation. Read both roles aloud, then replace one ordinary noun or activity while retaining the new pattern exactly.

## Independent production exercise

Write and say three general wishes with **я хочу** plus an infinitive. Use a fictional context if needed. Do not state sensitive plans, financial intentions, or personal commitments.

After writing, cover the English meanings and read the Russian twice. On the second reading, underline the new pattern in every sentence. If a sentence has more than one unfamiliar change, shorten it and rebuild it from a familiar model.

## Spoiler: answers and model response

### Retrieval answers

1. **Я читаю в библиотеке.**
2. **Ты дома?**
3. **Я не учусь дома.**
4. A valid answer names an explicit boundary check, such as a non-null pointer, an explicit `size_t` count, a checked file result, a field-range check, or a safe shift-count check.

### Guided-practice answers

1. **учиться**.
2. **Я не хочу работать.** means “I do not want to work.”
3. **Ты хочешь читать?**
4. The pattern is **хочу + infinitive**; the action verb is not conjugated in this position.
5. A failed `realloc` leaves the old allocation valid; overwriting that pointer first would lose the only handle needed to free or preserve it.
