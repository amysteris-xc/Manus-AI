You will recognize and produce simple sentences where the relative pronoun который agrees in gender and number (nominative case) with its antecedent noun: masculine, feminine, neuter (singular). You will complete short sentences and read a short dialogue that models agreement.

## Core pattern
A relative pronoun который agrees in gender and number with its antecedent. Below are basic nominative-singular examples.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
| --- | --- |
| Я вижу человека, который идёт. | I see the person (masculine), who is walking. |
| Это женщина, которая поёт. | This is a woman (feminine), who is singing. |
| Это окно, которое открыто. | This is a window (neuter), which is open. |
| Вот студент, который готовит ответ. | Here is the student (masculine), who is preparing an answer. |

## Concise explanation
Form: In these examples the relative pronoun который takes the nominative singular endings which match the gender of the noun it refers to: который (masculine), которая (feminine), которое (neuter). Meaning: который = "who/which/that" and links a clause to a specific noun. Limitation/common confusion: this lesson shows only nominative singular agreement; в других падежах (in other cases) и во множественном числе (and in plural) the form changes (e.g., которых, которым). Also, который agrees with the antecedent, not with the verb.

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты видишь стол, который новый?
*Do you see the table that is new?*

Б: Да, я вижу стол, который стоит у окна.
*Yes, I see the table that stands by the window.*

А: А ты знаешь девушку, которая читает книгу?
*And do you know the girl who is reading a book?*

Б: Да, это моя соседка, которая учится в университете.
*Yes, that's my neighbor who studies at the university.*

А: Что за окно? Это окно, которое было закрыто.
*What about that window? It's the window which was closed.*

Б: Точно, это то окно, которое нам нужно починить.
*Exactly, it's the window that we need to repair.*

## Guided practice
1. Supply the correct relative pronoun (masculine/feminine/neuter) in each blank: "Я вижу дом, ___ красивый." (write Russian)
2. Supply the correct relative pronoun: "Это актриса, ___ играет главную роль."
3. Supply the correct relative pronoun: "Оно — письмо, ___ пришло вчера." (choose the correct neuter pronoun)
4. Rewrite in Russian: "Here is the book that I bought." Use книга + который form.
5. Identify the antecedent and underline it (mentally) in this Russian sentence and then state the relative pronoun's gender: "Он показал фото, которое было старое." — what is the antecedent and which form of который is used?

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph (in Russian) about a small room and three objects in it. Each sentence should include one relative clause using который/которая/которое in nominative singular that agrees with its antecedent. Use a mix of masculine, feminine, and neuter nouns. Provide an English translation after the paragraph (concise, sentence-by-sentence).

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Я вижу дом, который красивый. — model: "Я вижу дом, который красивый." (дом — masculine → который)
2. Это актриса, которая играет главную роль. — model: "Это актриса, которая играет главную роль." (актриса — feminine → которая)
3. Оно — письмо, которое пришло вчера. — model: "Это письмо, которое пришло вчера." (письмо — neuter → которое)
4. Here is the book that I bought. — model Russian: "Вот книга, которую я купил(а)." Note: This sentence uses the accusative relative pronoun because "I bought the book"; this highlights a limitation: relative pronoun case can change with its role in the relative clause.
5. Он показал фото, которое было старое. — antecedent: фото (neuter), relative pronoun: которое (neuter nominative singular used as the subject of "было").
