# Russian 133 — Letters and Email Register

Learning objective: write a short fictional email opening, request, and closing in an appropriate register.

## What you will be able to do
Write a short fictional email (opening line, one-sentence request or purpose, and a polite closing) using either a neutral/informal register or a formal register; include a brief subject-like purpose statement in Russian.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall the instrumental case for instruments and means from Russian 096–101: form the instrumental of "ручка" and translate.
2. Recall the past-tense agreement rule from Russian 096–101: make past-tense masculine singular of "писать" (I wrote, masculine narrator) and translate.
3. Recall the accusative for animate masculine nouns from Russian 096–101: give the accusative of "коллега" and translate.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: state why validating a message length in bytes matters when sending UTF-8 Russian text (brief).

## Core pattern
You will contrast two common openings plus a concise purpose sentence (subject-like), then a polite closing.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Здравствуйте, Анна. | Hello (formal/neutral), Anna. |
| Привет, Олег! | Hi (neutral/informal), Oleg! |
| Тема: Запрос по расписанию встречи. | Subject: Request regarding the meeting schedule. |
| Прошу подтвердить дату и время. | I ask you to confirm the date and time. |
| С уважением, Мария Петрова. | Respectfully, Maria Petrova. |

## Concise explanation
Use "Здравствуйте" for formal or polite neutral greetings, especially when you do not know the addressee well or when addressing a professional contact. Use "Привет" for informal contexts with friends or close colleagues. A concise subject-like sentence often begins with "Тема:" or simply acts as a short one-line purpose (e.g., "Запрос по..."). Limitation: tone and word choice depend on context—some workplaces accept "Здравствуйте" as neutral while others prefer more formal phrasing such as "Уважаемый/Уважаемая" for official letters; do not assume "Привет" is acceptable in formal contexts.

## Worked dialogue
А: Здравствуйте, Ирина Ивановна.
*Hello, Irina Ivanovna.*

Б: Здравствуйте. Чем могу помочь?
*Hello. How can I help?*

А: Тема: Запрос о переносе встречи. Прошу сообщить, можно ли перенести на пятницу.
*Subject: Request to reschedule the meeting. Please let me know if it is possible to move it to Friday.*

Б: Спасибо, проверю календарь и сообщу до конца дня. С уважением, П. Сидоров.
*Thank you, I will check the calendar and inform you by the end of the day. Respectfully, P. Sidorov.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the neutral opening "Здравствуйте, Марк." into an informal opening suitable for a friend.
2. Create a one-line subject in Russian for asking about documents (use "Тема:" or a short sentence).
3. Convert "Прошу прислать отчёт." into a polite request by adding "пожалуйста".
4. Write a closing that is slightly less formal than "С уважением," but still polite.
5. Replace the name "Анна" in a greeting with a patronymic-style polite address for "Иван Петрович".

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional email in Russian suitable for a work-related but friendly request. Include:
- A greeting (choose either neutral/formal or informal, be consistent).
- A concise subject-like line or one-line purpose.
- One or two brief sentences explaining the request.
- One sentence offering a time preference or asking for confirmation.
- A polite closing and a name line.
Each Russian sentence must be followed by an exact English translation on the next line.

## Final self-check
You are ready to proceed if you can: 1) produce a clear greeting appropriate to the relationship (formal vs informal), 2) write a one-line purpose that a Russian reader would immediately understand, 3) make a polite, grammatically correct single-sentence request, and 4) close with an appropriate sign-off. If any of these feel uncertain, repeat the guided practice focusing on tone and verb forms.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model rewrite (informal greeting): "Привет, Марк!" — "Hi, Mark!"
2. Example subject for documents: "Тема: Запрос документов по проекту." — "Subject: Request for project documents."
3. Polite request with пожалуйста: "Прошу прислать отчёт, пожалуйста." — "Please send the report."
4. Model full 6-sentence email (formal-neutral register):
   1) "Здравствуйте, Ольга Сергеевна." — "Hello, Olga Sergeevna."
   2) "Тема: Согласование времени презентации." — "Subject: Scheduling the presentation time."
   3) "Прошу подтвердить, удобно ли вам провести презентацию в следующий вторник в 14:00." — "Please confirm whether it is convenient for you to hold the presentation next Tuesday at 14:00."
   4) "Если неудобно, предложите, пожалуйста, другие варианты." — "If that is inconvenient, please propose other options."
   5) "Заранее благодарю за ответ." — "Thank you in advance for the reply."
   6) "С уважением, Алексей Дмитриев." — "Respectfully, Alexey Dmitriev."
