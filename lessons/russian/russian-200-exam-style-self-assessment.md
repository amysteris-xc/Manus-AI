Learning objective: evaluate personal Russian progress honestly using examples, uncertainty, and a next study target.
Teaching focus: Treat the task as reflection, not certification. Ask for concrete evidence from prior exercises and one realistic goal.

## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Summarize what you can reliably do in Russian with concrete examples.
- Identify one clear area of uncertainty using specific errors or sentence attempts.
- Set one realistic next study target linked to prior practice.
- Use short Russian phrases accurately to demonstrate level.

## Retrieval practice
1. Write a short sentence in Russian using the past tense (perfective) of a motion verb you learned in Russian 096–101.
2. Conjugate the present tense of the verb читать for я, ты, он/она, мы.
3. Provide the accusative singular form of a masculine animate noun you practiced in Russian 096–101 (e.g., студент).
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: name one simple check you would use to validate that a received byte string length matches an expected message size (one short sentence in English is fine).

## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on expressing ability, uncertainty, and a next goal using short Russian sentences.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я могу читать простые тексты. | I can read simple texts. |
| Я не уверен(а) в согласовании падежей. | I am not sure about case agreement. |
| Моя следующая цель — практика разговорных вопросов. | My next goal is practice with conversational questions. |

## Concise explanation
Use "Я могу ..." to state abilities (ability + infinitive). Use "Я не уверен(а) ..." to express uncertainty; include parenthetical gender ending as needed (unisex note: add -а for feminine speakers). State a specific next goal with "Моя следующая цель — ...". Limitation: this pattern states attitudes and goals but does not itself teach how to fix grammatical gaps — you must supply concrete examples from your practice to make the reflection useful.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я могу понимать короткие диалоги.
*I can understand short dialogues.*

Б: Я не уверен в прошедшем времени глаголов движения.
*I am not sure about past tense of motion verbs.*

А: Моя следующая цель — пересматривать упражнения по прошедшему времени.
*My next goal is to review exercises on the past tense.*

Б: Покажите один пример, и мы оценим точность.
*Show one example, and we will assess the accuracy.*

## Guided practice
1. Write three self-evaluation sentences in Russian: one ability, one uncertainty, one next goal. Each must be 6–10 words.
2. From a past written exercise, copy one sentence you produced and rewrite it correcting any case or verb errors. Provide both versions.
3. Translate into Russian: "I was able to finish the exercise, but I made grammar mistakes." Keep the sentence concise.
4. Record (or write) one short Russian question you would ask a tutor about case usage.
5. Choose one concrete practice activity for the week (e.g., "15 minutes of declension drills daily") and write it in Russian.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence paragraph in Russian describing:
- two things you can do now (abilities),
- one thing that still confuses you (uncertainty),
- one concrete, realistic study goal for the next two weeks.
Each Russian sentence must be grammatically correct. After the paragraph, add one brief English sentence stating how you will measure progress.

Example constraints: use past and present tenses at least once, and include one animate accusative noun.

## Spaced-repetition plan
| When | Task |
|---|---|
| About one hour later | Re-read your three self-evaluation sentences and correct any typos; say them aloud. |
| Next day | Complete a 10-minute targeted exercise on the grammar point you reported as uncertain. |
| Three days later | Produce a new paragraph (4–6 sentences) describing progress and compare to the first paragraph. |
| One week later | Do a timed 10-minute mixed exercise: 5 minutes of declensions, 5 minutes of conjugations; record errors. |

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) produce three clear Russian self-evaluation sentences without major grammar errors, (2) point to one concrete sentence from past work and correct it, and (3) set a realistic two-week study target and describe one measurable way to check it. If you cannot satisfy these three items, review targeted exercises from Russian 096–101 before proceeding.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Example past-tense motion verb sentence (model): Я пришёл домой вчера пешком. — I came home yesterday on foot. (If speaker is female: Я пришла домой вчера пешком.)
2. Conjugation of читать (present): я читаю, ты читаешь, он/она читает, мы читаем.
3. Accusative singular animate example: студент → студента.
4. Simple byte-length validation concept: Verify that len(received_bytes) == expected_length before parsing.
