## Core pattern

To express an opinion, introduce your clause with **я думаю, что...** “I think that...”. To agree, a male speaker says **я согласен** and a female speaker says **я согласна**.

| Phrase | Gender | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| Я думаю, что это так. | neutral | I think that it is so. |
| Я согласен. | masculine | I agree. |
| Я согласна. | feminine | I agree. |
| Я не согласен / согласна. | m / f | I disagree. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Я думаю, что этот урок полезный.
> *I think that this lesson is useful.*
>
> **Б:** Да, я согласен. Он понятный.
> *Yes, I agree. It is clear.*
>
> **А:** А задание трудное?
> *And is the exercise hard?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, я думаю, что оно простое.
> *No, I think that it is simple.*
