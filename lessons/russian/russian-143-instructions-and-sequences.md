## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Give short, clear imperative instructions for everyday safe tasks (e.g., "open the window", "turn off the light").
- Use polite alternatives to imperatives (e.g., requests with пожалуйста or modal constructions).
- Follow a short sequence of 3–5 steps and perform a final check to confirm completion.

## Retrieval practice
1. Produce the singular informal imperative of "читать" (to read) and the singular formal imperative (Вы).
2. Conjugate and give the past tense masculine form of "делать" (to do).
3. Give the short adjective forms for "готов" (ready) for masculine and feminine.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: what does it mean for data to be "byte-aligned" (one short English phrase is enough)?

## Core pattern
A concise pattern for giving short sequences:
- Use the imperative (singular or plural/formal) for direct commands.
- Add пожалуйста for politeness, or use the modal construction "не могли бы вы..." for formal requests.
- Finish with a confirmation question: "Готово?" or "Вы сделали это?"

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Открой окно. | Open the window. |
| Пожалуйста, закрой дверь. | Please, close the door. |
| Не могли бы вы включить свет? | Could you (formal) turn on the light? |
| Сделай это и скажи мне. | Do this and tell me. |
| Готово? | Done? / Is it finished? |

## Concise explanation
Imperatives in Russian are formed from the verb stem: for most verbs, the singular informal imperative is the 3rd-person plural form minus -ут/-ют or the 2nd-person singular form with specific endings (this varies by verb class). The plural or formal (Вы) form adds -те to the singular imperative. For politeness, add пожалуйста or use modal polite forms such as "Не могли бы вы..." followed by an infinitive. Limitation: some verbs are irregular in the imperative (e.g., идти → иди, идти → идите) and reflexive verbs keep -ся (e.g., садиться → сядьтесь is irregular), so check individual verbs when in doubt.

## Worked dialogue
А: Открой окно, пожалуйста.
*Open the window, please.*

Б: Открыл, вам удобно?
*I opened it, is it comfortable for you?*

А: Пожалуйста, выключи свет, когда уйдёшь.
*Please turn off the light when you leave.*

Б: Хорошо, выключу. Готово?
*Okay, I will turn it off. Done?*

А: Да, всё в порядке, спасибо.
*Yes, everything is fine, thank you.*

## Guided practice
1. Write three short imperatives (informal singular) to tell a friend how to make tea (verbs only, in Russian and then translate).
2. Convert those three imperatives into polite requests using пожалуйста.
3. Rewrite the three as formal (Вы) polite requests using "Не могли бы вы..." and infinitives.
4. Read aloud a 3-step sequence: "Open the window — sit down — say 'готово'." Record or say the Russian sentence(s).
5. Practice a final check question: write and say three variations of "Are you done?" (e.g., "Готово?", "Вы сделали это?", "Всё готово?") with translations.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional 6–10 sentence scenario in Russian where you give a sequence of safe household instructions to a roommate (use Cyrillic). Include:
- At least three imperatives (mix informal and polite forms).
- One polite modal request ("Не могли бы вы..." or "Можете ли вы...").
- A final explicit check asking if the task is completed (e.g., "Готово?" or "Вы сделали всё?").
After writing the Russian paragraph, provide an exact English translation sentence-by-sentence.

Example constraints: keep actions safe and everyday (cleaning, opening windows, turning off lights, watering plants).

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model imperatives for making tea (three verbs): "Кипяти воду." / "Засыпь чай." / "Дай чайнику постоять." — Exact translations: "Boil the water." / "Put the tea (leaves) in." / "Let the kettle stand." Note: other valid phrasings exist.
2. Polite with пожалуйста: "Кипяти воду, пожалуйста." / "Пожалуйста, засыпь чай." / "Пожалуйста, дай чайнику постоять." — Accept these as correct.
3. Formal/modal: "Не могли бы вы вскипятить воду?" / "Не могли бы вы засыпать чай?" / "Не могли бы вы дать чайнику постоять?" — Accept minor verb aspect differences; prefer perfective for single completed actions when natural.
4. Example independent production (model criteria): 6–10 sentences, uses at least three imperatives and one "Не могли бы вы..." modal request, ends with a final check like "Готово?" — full credit if all elements present, grammar mostly correct, and English translations match.
