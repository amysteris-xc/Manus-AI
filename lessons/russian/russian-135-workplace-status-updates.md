## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to deliver a concise status update in Russian consisting of: (1) one completed action, (2) a current issue or blocker, and (3) a next step. The update will be appropriate for neutral workplace or study situations and will avoid making sensitive claims.

## Core pattern
Use a three-part structure: Completed work (one sentence, past tense), Current issue/blocker (one sentence, present), Next action (one sentence, near future). Each Russian sentence below has a precise English translation.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я подготовил(а) отчёт по проекту. | I prepared the project report. |
| Сейчас я проверяю данные, и возник вопрос по формату. | Right now I am checking the data, and a question about the format has come up. |
| Завтра я уточню формат и отправлю исправленный файл. | Tomorrow I will clarify the format and send the corrected file. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Completed actions typically use the past tense (perfective or imperfective depending on meaning) like подготовил(а), сделал(а). Current issues use the present tense with verbs like проверяю, есть вопрос. Next steps use a near-future form (future tense or present tense with time marker) like завтра уточню or буду отправлять. Limitation/common confusion: Russian past-tense verbs show gender (e.g., подготовил — masculine, подготовила — feminine) and agreement must match the speaker; ensure you use the correct ending. Also, Russian has two types of future (perfective single-word future vs. compound future for imperfective), but for short status updates, the perfective future (e.g., уточню, отправлю) is simplest and most natural.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я подготовил презентацию.
*I prepared the presentation.*

Б: Отлично. Сейчас есть ли какие‑то проблемы?
*Great. Are there any problems right now?*

А: Да, при проверке данных я заметил(а) несоответствие формата.
*Yes, while checking the data I noticed a format mismatch.*

Б: Какой следующий шаг?
*What is the next step?*

А: Завтра уточню формат у заказчика и отправлю исправленную версию.
*Tomorrow I will clarify the format with the client and send the corrected version.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the core pattern sentences using the feminine past form (for a female speaker).
2. Change the "current issue" sentence to mention a deadline problem instead of a format problem.
3. Replace "завтра" with a specific time phrase "к концу дня" and adjust the next-action sentence.
4. Translate to Russian: "I completed the test; I found a bug; I will fix it today."
5. Record yourself speaking the three sentences aloud and time yourself — keep the whole update under 25 seconds.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional status update (6–10 sentences) about a study or workplace mini-project. Use the three-part structure repeatedly: for each mini-task mention one completed action (past tense), one current issue (present), and one next action (future). Keep scenarios neutral and fictional (e.g., preparing slides, running data checks, drafting a short summary). Use correct gender agreement for the speaker and include time markers (завтра, сегодня, к концу дня).

You are ready to move on if you can: (1) produce a three-sentence status update with past, present, and future elements; (2) maintain correct past-tense gender agreement; (3) substitute different neutral issues (format, deadline, missing info) while keeping clear next steps; and (4) speak the update clearly in under 25 seconds.
