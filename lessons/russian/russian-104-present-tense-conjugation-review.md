- Recognize and produce present-tense forms for common verbs across 1st person singular, 2nd person singular, and 1st/3rd person plural.
- Use those forms in short, accurate sentences that distinguish who is doing the action.
- Avoid a common confusion between Russian 2nd-person singular and plural formal forms by context.

## Core pattern
Practice verbs: читать (to read), говорить (to speak), писать (to write). Use present tense, contrasting 1st person singular, 2nd person singular, and plural (мы/они).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я читаю книгу. | I am reading a book. |
| Ты читаешь газету. | You (sing., informal) are reading the newspaper. |
| Мы читаем вечером. | We read / We are reading in the evening. |
| Я говорю правду. | I speak the truth. |
| Ты говоришь медленно. | You (sing., informal) speak slowly. |
| Они говорят по-русски. | They speak Russian. |
| Я пишу письмо. | I am writing a letter. |
| Ты пишешь список. | You (sing., informal) are writing a list. |
| Мы пишем заметки. | We are writing notes. |

## Concise explanation
In present tense, Russian verbs change their endings to agree with person and number. Common patterns: 1st person singular often ends -ю or -у (я читаю, я пишу, я говорю), 2nd person singular usually ends -ешь, -ишь, or -ишь (ты читаешь, ты пишешь, ты говоришь), and plural forms (мы, вы, они) use endings like -ем/-ём, -ите, -ут/-ют depending on conjugation group. Limitation: irregular verbs and verbs with stem changes (e.g., дать, быть, eсть) do not follow these simple endings and must be learned individually; this lesson focuses on high-frequency regular verbs.

## Worked dialogue
А: Привет! Ты читаешь эту книгу?
*Hi! Are you reading this book?*

Б: Да, я читаю её сейчас. А ты что читаешь?
*Yes, I'm reading it now. And what are you reading?*

А: Я пишу заметки, а потом мы говорим о сюжете.
*I'm writing notes, and then we will talk about the plot.*

Б: Хорошо. Мы говорим по-русски, когда обсуждаем книгу.
*Good. We speak Russian when we discuss the book.*

## Guided practice
1. Conjugate читать for я, ты, мы and write one full sentence for each (three sentences).
2. Conjugate говорить for я, ты, они and write one full sentence for each (three sentences).
3. Change the subject in "Я пишу письмо." to ты and мы; write both resulting full sentences.
4. Read aloud the nine example sentences from "Core pattern." Record yourself (audio or mental) and note any pronunciation difficulty with -ю/-у endings.
5. Identify one sentence from the Core pattern and replace the object with something else (e.g., "газету" → "письмо") keeping the verb form correct.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a small reading group. Include:
- at least two sentences using я + present-tense verb,
- at least two sentences using ты + present-tense verb,
- at least two sentences using мы or они + present-tense verb.
Use different verbs from the Core pattern and change at least one object. Keep the scenario harmless and simple (e.g., friends sharing books and notes). Provide English translations directly under each Russian sentence.
