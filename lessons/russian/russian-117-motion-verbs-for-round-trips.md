## Core pattern
We focus on three verbs to build a simple timeline: поехать (perfective — go by vehicle), приехать (perfective — arrive), вернуться (perfective — return). For habitual or ongoing trips, use the imperfective counterpart ездить or приходить/возвращаться as appropriate.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я поехал(а) в город. | I went to the city (by vehicle; action completed). |
| Мы приехали на вокзал. | We arrived at the station. |
| Он вернулся домой вечером. | He returned home in the evening. |
| Я обычно езжу в парк по выходным. | I usually go to the park (habitual, by vehicle). |

## Concise explanation
Use perfective motion verbs (поехать, приехать, вернуться) to describe completed legs of a trip: starting the journey, arriving, and coming back. Use corresponding imperfective verbs (ездить, приезжать, возвращаться) to express habitual or repeated trips. A common confusion: поехать and ехать differ by aspect and directionality; this lesson focuses on поехать (perfective start by vehicle) and its relation to arrival/return verbs. Limitation: some contexts require choosing walking verbs (идти/пойти) vs. vehicle verbs (ехать/поехать) — pick the appropriate verb based on mode of travel.

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты поедешь в деревню завтра?
*Are you going to go to the village tomorrow?*

Б: Да, я поеду утром и приеду вечером.
*Yes, I will go in the morning and arrive in the evening.*

А: А ты вернёшься в тот же день?
*Will you return the same day?*

Б: Нет, я вернусь в воскресенье; обычно я езжу туда каждое лето.
*No, I'll return on Sunday; usually I go there every summer.*

## Guided practice
1. Convert the sentence "Я поехал(а) в магазин." into a habitual version using ездить. Write the Russian and its English translation.
2. Write one sentence describing arrival: use приехать + место (place) in past tense (masculine). Provide Russian and English.
3. Make a sentence with вернуться in the future perfective: "They will return at night." Provide Russian and English.
4. Change the verb in sentence 2 to its imperfective counterpart приезжать and explain in one sentence (English) how the meaning changes.
5. Create a two-sentence timeline: start (поехать), arrival (приехать), and return (вернуться) using appropriate tenses for a single completed trip.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) describing a weekend round trip: leaving by vehicle, arriving, doing an activity there, and returning. Use at least three of these verbs: поехать, приехать, вернуться, ездить, приезжать, возвращаться. Include times or days (e.g., утром, вечером, в субботу) and one habitual remark (e.g., обычно). Provide the paragraph in Russian and then an English translation immediately below it. Aim for grammatical accuracy and clear timeline markers.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Habitual version of "Я поехал(а) в магазин.": "Я езжу в магазин." — "I go to the store (habitually/by vehicle)."
2. Arrival sentence (past masculine): "Он приехал на вокзал." — "He arrived at the station."
3. Future return with вернуться: "Они вернутся ночью." — "They will return at night."
4. Imperfective change and explanation: "Он приезжал на вокзал." — "He used to/was arriving at the station (habitual or repeated action, not a single completed arrival)."
