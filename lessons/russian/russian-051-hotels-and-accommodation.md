**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes

## Core pattern

Learn accommodation terms: **гостиница** / **отель** “hotel”, **номер** “room”, and **на одного / на двоих** “for one / for two”. Ask **У вас есть свободные номера?** “Do you have vacant rooms?”.

| Hotel phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| свободный номер | vacant room |
| номер на одного | single room |
| номер на двоих | double room |
| на две ночи | for two nights |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Здравствуйте. У вас есть свободные номера?
> *Hello. Do you have vacant rooms?*
>
> **Б:** Да, есть номер на одного.
> *Yes, there is a single room.*
>
> **А:** Сколько он стоит за ночь?
> *How much is it per night?*
>
> **Б:** Тридцать рублей.
> *Thirty rubles.*
