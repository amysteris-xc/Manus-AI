## Core pattern
The basic negative imperative in Russian places не before the verb form: не + (imperative). Use the familiar singular or plural (or polite) imperative forms you already know. Below are common, concrete boundary phrases.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Не спеши. | Don't rush. (familiar singular) |
| Не открывайте дверь. | Don't open the door. (polite or plural) |
| Не забудь документ. | Don't forget the document. (familiar singular) |
| Не шумите, пожалуйста. | Don't make noise, please. (polite/plural with politeness marker) |

## Concise explanation
Form: Put не directly before the imperative form of the verb: не + [imperative]. For familiar singular use the short imperative (e.g., "спеши" → "не спеши"), for polite or plural use the plural/polite imperative (e.g., "откройте" → "не открывайте"). Meaning: expresses prohibition or a direct caution (do not do X). Limitation/common confusion: Russian also uses the particle не with infinitives and other moods; here we focus on imperatives. Do not confuse не + imperative (command not to do something) with не + infinitive in constructions like "можно не..." which has a different structure and nuance (permission or option). Also watch tone: adding пожалуйста softens a prohibition into a polite request.

## Worked dialogue
А: Не спеши, пожалуйста.
*Don't rush, please.*

Б: Хорошо, не буду спешить.
*Okay, I won't rush.*

А: Не открывайте дверь; кто-то стучит.
*Don't open the door; someone is knocking.*

Б: Я не открою, подожду.
*I won't open it, I'll wait.*

А: Не забудь документ для встречи.
*Don't forget the document for the meeting.*

Б: Не забуду — положу в сумку сейчас.
*I won't forget — I'll put it in my bag now.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian (familiar singular): "Don't rush."
2. Translate into Russian (polite/plural): "Don't open the door, please."
3. Produce a Russian negative imperative for: "Don't forget the keys." (familiar singular)
4. Change the familiar singular "Не жди." to a polite/plural form.
5. Add "пожалуйста" to soften: convert "Не шумите" into a polite-sounding request.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional dialogue between a teacher and a student preparing for an exam. Use at least four negative imperatives (mix familiar and polite forms) and include one softened request with пожалуйста. Keep content non-medical and non-legal; focus on practical study boundaries (e.g., don't forget papers, don't rush, don't use your phone, don't open answers early). Provide an English translation under each Russian sentence.
