## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to write and say a short project brief in Russian that includes: цель проекта (project goal), целевая аудитория (target audience), ограничения (constraints), and следующий шаг (next step).

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall a Russian verb for "to decide" you studied in Russian 096–101. Write it in Russian and give an English gloss.
2. Recall a Russian phrase for "in order to" or purpose you learned in Russian 096–101. Write it in Russian and give an English gloss.
3. Recall a Russian noun for "audience" or "reader" from Russian 096–101. Write it in Russian and give an English gloss.
4. Recall a concept about byte-boundary or validation: in many file formats, ensure a checksum or validation byte is included to detect corruption. Write one short English phrase describing this concept.

## Core pattern
Use simple present-tense statements and short nominal phrases to state a project's goal, audience, constraints, and next step.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Цель проекта — создать учебный подкаст. | The project's goal is to create an educational podcast. |
| Целевая аудитория — студенты начального уровня. | The target audience is beginning-level students. |
| Ограничения — три 10-минутных эпизода и бюджет на хостинг. | Constraints — three 10-minute episodes and a hosting budget. |
| Следующий шаг — записать первый эпизод на этой неделе. | Next step — record the first episode this week. |

## Concise explanation
The pattern uses a short topic label (Цель проекта, Целевая аудитория, Ограничения, Следующий шаг) followed by a dash and a compact descriptive phrase. This keeps statements clear and easy to parse in both spoken and written Russian. Limitation: in formal Russian documents you might need more precise grammatical structures (e.g., full subordinate clauses or passive constructions); this lesson focuses on concise, conversationally natural brief statements rather than full legal wording.

## Worked dialogue
А: Цель проекта — создать небольшой учебный блог.
*The project's goal is to create a small educational blog.*

Б: Целевая аудитория — взрослые, изучающие русский язык.
*The target audience is adults learning Russian.*

А: Какие ограничения у нас есть?
*What constraints do we have?*

Б: Ограничения — два часа в неделю на подготовку и ограниченный бюджет.
*Constraints — two hours per week for preparation and a limited budget.*

А: Следующий шаг — подготовить план первого текста.
*Next step — prepare an outline for the first post.*

Б: Хорошо, сделаю это к концу недели.
*Okay, I'll do that by the end of the week.*

## Guided practice
1. Write one sentence stating a fictional project's Цель проекта (goal) in Russian (5–10 words).
2. Write one sentence stating the Целевая аудитория (audience) in Russian (3–6 words).
3. Write one sentence listing two Ограничения (constraints) separated by "и" in Russian.
4. Write one sentence with Следующий шаг beginning with "Следующий шаг —".
5. Read your four sentences aloud and record yourself, then compare pronunciation to a reliable source.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence project brief in Russian for a harmless creative project (for example, a short illustrated storybook, a study group, or a podcast). Include:
- One sentence starting with "Цель проекта —"
- One sentence starting with "Целевая аудитория —"
- One sentence listing at least two "Ограничения —"
- One sentence starting with "Следующий шаг —"
Use simple vocabulary and avoid specialized professional terms. Keep each sentence concise.

## Spaced-repetition plan
| When | Task |
|---|---|
| About one hour later | Read your brief aloud and correct any pronunciation or grammar issues. |
| Next day | Rewrite the brief from memory, then compare to the original. |
| Three days later | Replace one element (goal, audience, constraint, or step) with a new idea and speak the new brief. |
| One week later | Produce a new 6–10 sentence brief for a different fictional project without looking at previous drafts. |

## Final self-check
You are ready to proceed if you can: (1) produce clear single-sentence statements for Цель проекта, Целевая аудитория, Ограничения, and Следующий шаг in Russian; (2) read them aloud with intelligible pronunciation; and (3) rewrite them from memory with only minor mistakes. If any of these fail, repeat the guided practice and spaced-repetition tasks.
