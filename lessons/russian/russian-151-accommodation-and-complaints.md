# Russian 151 — Accommodation and Polite Complaints

## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to write and speak a brief, polite complaint in Russian about an accommodation issue (hotel, apartment, short-term rental), stating what happened, when it happened, and requesting a reasonable remedy.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall and write three Russian words for common places learned in Russian 096–101 (e.g., "гостиница", "квартира", "комната") and give their English meanings.
2. Conjugate the verb "быть" in the past tense for masculine and feminine singular (from Russian 097–099 patterns).
3. Produce the instrumental form of "ремонт" and use it in a short phrase (recalling instrumental endings from Russian 100–101).
4. Recall a C-level concept: explain briefly what a byte-boundary or validation check is in one sentence (e.g., "a validation ensures fields meet expected lengths").

## Core pattern
Use this factual three-part pattern: 1) what happened (concise statement), 2) when it happened (time phrase), 3) desired next step (polite request).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| В номере протекает кран. | A faucet is leaking in the room. |
| Это началось вчера вечером. | This started yesterday evening. |
| Пожалуйста, пришлите техника. | Please send a maintenance worker. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use a short declarative sentence for the problem (subject + verb + object or location), a time phrase (e.g., "вчера", "сегодня утром", "с первого этажа"), then a polite request using "пожалуйста" and an infinitive or imperative softened with "не могли бы вы" / "не могли бы прислать". Meaning: the structure communicates facts and a preferred remedy without accusations or threats. Limitation/common confusion: do not use legal or aggressive language; phrases like "я требую" (I demand) are not appropriate for this polite pattern. Also, the past tense agreement in Russian depends on the speaker’s gender when the verb is perfective past — be careful when describing who experienced the issue.

## Worked dialogue
А: В комнате шумит кондиционер.
*There is noise from the air conditioner in the room.*

Б: Когда это началось?
*When did that start?*

А: Вчера ночью, я не мог(ла) уснуть.
*Last night, I could not fall asleep.*

Б: Пожалуйста, не могли бы вы описать шум и указать номер?
*Please, could you describe the noise and give the room number?*

А: Шум постоянный, номер 214. Пожалуйста, пришлите техника сегодня утром.
*The noise is constant, room 214. Please send a technician this morning.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the core pattern for this scenario: "The heater doesn't work; this morning; please fix it" as one Russian paragraph (3 short sentences).
2. Transform the sentence "Кран протекает" into a polite request asking for help: use "не могли бы вы".
3. Replace the time phrase in the sentence "Это началось вчера вечером" with "сегодня утром" and adjust the full short complaint accordingly.
4. Create two alternative polite closing sentences to end a complaint message (e.g., "Спасибо за помощь" and a variant with availability).
5. Role-play aloud: read the worked dialogue substituting the problem with "интернет не работает" and change the time phrase.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional complaint message in Russian to a front desk or host about a non-urgent accommodation problem (e.g., noisy plumbing, broken lamp, no hot water). Follow the factual three-part structure: what happened, when it happened, desired next step. Include:
- an opening greeting (short),
- one sentence stating the problem,
- one sentence with a time reference,
- one sentence with location (room or apartment number),
- one polite request for a remedy using "пожалуйста" or "не могли бы вы",
- a closing sentence with thanks or availability times.

All sentences must be factual and avoid threats or legal language.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: 1) state an accommodation problem in one clear Russian sentence, 2) add a correct time phrase, 3) politely request a remedy using "пожалуйста" or "не могли бы вы", and 4) keep the tone factual and non-threatening. If you hesitate about past-tense agreement or polite formulas, review the Guided practice items.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model short paragraph for guided practice task 1:
   - Здравствуйте. Отопление не работает. Это началось сегодня утром. Номер 305. Пожалуйста, пришлите техника. Спасибо.
   (Criteria: greeting, problem, time, location, polite request, thanks.)
2. Model transformation for task 2 ("Кран протекает" → polite request):
   - Не могли бы вы прислать кого-нибудь? Кран протекает.
   (Criteria: use "не могли бы вы" + infinitive; keep sentences factual.)
3. Model alternative polite closing sentences for task 4:
   - Спасибо за помощь. Я буду на месте с 9 до 12.
   (Criteria: one expresses thanks, the other gives availability.)
4. Example full independent production (6 sentences):
   - Здравствуйте, меня зовут Иван. В ванной протекает кран. Это началось вчера вечером. Я проживаю в квартире 7B. Пожалуйста, пришлите, если возможно, техника сегодня днём. Заранее спасибо за оперативность.
   (Criteria: greeting, problem, time, location, polite remedy request, thanks; factual and non-threatening.)
