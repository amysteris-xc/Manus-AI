## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to ask simple questions at a city information desk (e.g., hours, location, required documents) and understand short, factual responses. You will practice common service vocabulary and polite question forms.

## Core pattern
Concise teaching paragraph: Many city-service interactions use short questions about location, hours, and documents. Common verbs: находиться (to be located), работать (to work / be open), требоваться (to be required — reflexive: требоваться), and можно (is it possible / may I). Use polite forms: можно + infinitive and пожалуйста.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Где находится справочная? | Where is the information desk located? |
| Во сколько открывается отделение? | At what time does the branch open? |
| Какие документы требуются для справки? | Which documents are required for the certificate? |
| Можно сюда подойти позже? | May I come here later? |
| Справочная работает до шести? | Does the information desk work (is it open) until six? |

## Concise explanation
Form: Questions often start with Где, Во сколько, Какие, or use можно + infinitive for requests. Short replies often use prepositional phrases for location (в центре, на первом этаже), time expressions (до шести, с девяти до шести), and lists of nouns in the nominative or genitive depending on the verb. Limitation/common confusion: verbs of necessity or requirement can be expressed with требоваться (impersonal) or надо/нужно + Infinitive; choose требуются for "are required" when focusing on documents (e.g., "Документы требуются" vs "Нужно принести документы").

## Worked dialogue
А: Где находится справочная?
*Where is the information desk located?*

Б: Справочная на первом этаже, рядом с лифтом.
*The information desk is on the first floor, next to the elevator.*

А: Во сколько она открывается?
*What time does it open?*

Б: Она открывается в девять утра и закрывается в шесть вечера.
*It opens at nine in the morning and closes at six in the evening.*

А: Какие документы требуются для оформления пропуска?
*Which documents are required to obtain a pass?*

Б: Нужны паспорт и заполненная заявка. Цена — условная, уточняйте у оператора.
*A passport and a completed application are needed. The fee is conditional; check with the operator.*

## Guided practice
1. Say aloud and translate: "Где находится отдел городских услуг?" Then answer in Russian: "Отдел находится на третьем этаже."
2. Write the question "Во сколько открывается центр?" and reply with a time range: "С десяти до семи."
3. Replace "паспорт" in the reply above with another document (e.g., "свидетельство") and adjust the sentence if needed.
4. Form a polite request using можно: ask "Можно задать вопрос?" and write two possible short answers.
5. Role-play: imagine an information desk that closes at 18:30. Write the exchange: you ask closing time; the clerk replies. Use exact Russian sentences.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional exchange you could have at a city services information desk. Include:
- One question asking location (Где...),
- One question about opening hours (Во сколько... or Когда...),
- One question about required documents (Какие...),
- One polite request using можно,
- Two short factual replies from the clerk (location/time/documents).
Each Russian sentence must be followed by its precise English translation (italicized or regular — maintain clarity). Use clear, simple vocabulary learned here; keep the scenario fictional (e.g., "муниципальный центр" in a made-up city).

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for the location question: "Где находится отдел городских услуг?" — "Отдел городских услуг находится на третьем этаже, кабинет 305." (The municipal services office is on the third floor, office 305.)
2. Model answer for opening hours: "Во сколько открывается центр?" — "Центр открывается в десять утра и работает до семи вечера." (The center opens at ten AM and operates until seven PM.)
3. Model answer for documents: "Какие документы требуются?" — "Требуются паспорт и заявление." (A passport and an application are required.)
4. Model polite request and response: "Можно подойти завтра?" — "Да, можно; приходите с девяти до двенадцати." (May I come tomorrow? — Yes, you may; come between nine and twelve.)
