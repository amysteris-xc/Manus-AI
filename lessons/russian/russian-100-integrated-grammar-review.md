# Russian 100 — Integrated Grammar Review

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–099
**Author:** Manus AI

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to review core cases, tense, aspect, motion, and connectors through controlled sentence repair and production.

## Retrieval practice

1. Say one full model sentence from Russian 057 without looking.
2. Recall the difference between a current journey and a habitual journey.
3. State why a C byte buffer needs an explicit `size_t` length.
4. Translate one earlier polite request into English.

## Core pattern

This review prioritizes choosing a known reliable form: possession with **у**, companion with **с**, topic with **о**, process versus result, and movement versus habit.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| У меня нет времени, но я буду учиться. | I do not have time, but I will study. |
| Я иду с другом в парк. | I am going to the park with a friend. |
| Мы говорим о новой книге. | We are talking about a new book. |

## Concise explanation

Build each statement from a reliable chunk before adding detail. Preserve the case, verb form, or connector shown in the model rather than translating word-for-word from English. Russian word order is flexible, but this lesson uses a neutral order so that the grammar pattern remains visible.
