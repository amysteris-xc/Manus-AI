## Core pattern

Proverbs reinforce culture and rhythm: **Лучше поздно, чем никогда** “Better late than never” and **Век живи — век учись** “Live a century, learn a century (you live and learn)”.

| Russian proverb | Literal meaning | English equivalent |
|---|---|---|
| Лучше поздно, чем никогда. | Better late than never. | Better late than never. |
| Век живи — век учись. | Live a century — learn a century. | You live and learn. |
| Повторение — мать учения. | Repetition is mother of learning. | Practice makes perfect. |
| Делу время, потехе час. | Business a time, fun an hour. | Business before pleasure. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Я учу русский язык каждый день.
> *I learn Russian every day.*
>
> **Б:** Молодец! Повторение — мать учения.
> *Well done! Repetition is the mother of learning.*
>
> **А:** Иногда это трудно, но интересно.
> *Sometimes it is hard, but interesting.*
>
> **Б:** Век живи — век учись!
> *You live and learn!*
