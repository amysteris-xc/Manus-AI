## Core pattern

The compound future **буду + infinitive** commonly expresses an ongoing or repeated future activity. A perfective present-looking form can express a completed future result: **я прочитаю**.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я буду читать вечером. | I will be reading in the evening. |
| Я прочитаю статью завтра. | I will finish reading the article tomorrow. |
| Мы будем работать дома. | We will work at home. |

## Concise explanation

Build each statement from a reliable chunk before adding detail. Preserve the case, verb form, or connector shown in the model rather than translating word-for-word from English. Russian word order is flexible, but this lesson uses a neutral order so that the grammar pattern remains visible.
