## Core pattern
This pattern helps you say who can access something, how to ask for permission, and how to refuse or request safer handling.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| У меня есть пароль. | I have a password. |
| Можно посмотреть настройки приватности? | May I check the privacy settings? |
| Пожалуйста, не делитесь моей информацией. | Please do not share my information. |

## Concise explanation
Forms: simple present statements and polite modal questions. Use "Можно..." to ask permission or possibility, "Пожалуйста" to soften requests, and "не" to negate. Limitation: "Можно..." is impersonal—if you need to specify a person, add "вы можете..." or "ты можешь..." for clarity (formal vs. informal). Avoid giving technical instructions; this lesson focuses on phrasing and boundaries.

## Worked dialogue
А: У меня проблема с паролем.
*I have a problem with my password.*

Б: Можно ли вам помочь через официальный сайт?
*May I help you through the official website?*

А: Да, пожалуйста, подскажите, где настройки приватности.
*Yes, please tell me where the privacy settings are.*

Б: Откройте «Настройки», затем «Безопасность и конфиденциальность».
*Open "Settings", then "Security and Privacy".*

## Guided practice
1. Say in Russian: "I forgot my password." (one sentence).
2. Ask politely: "Can you check my account settings?" using formal "you."
3. Refuse a request: Say "Please do not share my password" in Russian.
4. Ask where to find privacy settings: Form a question using "где."
5. Tell a friend politely: "I will change my permissions later" (one sentence).

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional message in Russian to a support agent describing a benign account concern and asking for official help. Include:
- One sentence stating the issue (e.g., forgotten password).
- One polite request using "Можно..." or "Можете ли вы...".
- One sentence asking where privacy settings are.
- One sentence asking the agent not to share your personal information.
- Sign off with a short thank-you sentence.
Each Russian sentence must be clear and use Cyrillic.
