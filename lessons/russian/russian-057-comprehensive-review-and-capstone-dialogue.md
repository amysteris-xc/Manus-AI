# Russian 057 — Comprehensive Review and Capstone Dialogue

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–056 and the immediately preceding lessons
**Author:** Manus AI

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to participate in an extended ten-line fictional conversation integrating greetings, preferences, time, places, occupations, and future plans.

## Retrieval practice

1. Translate one model phrase from the previous Russian lesson.
2. Recall one grammatical contrast or question pattern studied recently.
3. State one key safety boundary or bounds-check rule from the previous C lesson.
4. Formulate one short sentence describing an activity or routine.

## Core pattern

This capstone lesson reviews everything learned across the first 57 lessons. Construct clear sentences, maintain gender agreement, and express complex thoughts by linking simple, reliable clauses.

| Concept | Sample phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | Меня зовут Алексей, я инженер. | My name is Aleksey, I am an engineer. |
| Habit | Каждое утро я пью чай и читаю. | Every morning I drink tea and read. |
| Travel | Завтра я еду на поезде в город. | Tomorrow I am going by train to the city. |
| Reflection | Я думаю, что этот курс полезен. | I think that this course is useful. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Здравствуйте! Как ваши успехи в изучении языка?
> *Hello! How is your progress in learning the language?*
>
> **Б:** Здравствуйте. Я занимаюсь каждый день и уже многое понимаю.
> *Hello. I practice every day and already understand a lot.*
>
> **А:** Что вы планируете делать дальше?
> *What do you plan to do next?*
>
> **Б:** Я планирую продолжать практиковаться и читать книги по-русски.
> *I plan to continue practicing and reading books in Russian.*
>
> **А:** Желаю вам успехов!
> *I wish you success!*
>
> **Б:** Большое спасибо! До свидания.
> *Thank you very much! Goodbye.*
