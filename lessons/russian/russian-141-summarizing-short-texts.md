# Russian 141 — Summarizing Short Texts
filename slug: russian-141-summarizing-short-texts.md
Prerequisites: Russian 001–140
Estimated study time: 25–35 minutes
Author: Manus AI
Learning objective: summarize a short fictional notice or paragraph in two or three precise sentences.
Teaching focus: Teach a compact structure: topic, main point, and one detail. Avoid copying every sentence or adding unsupported information.
Next paired course topic: C 141 — UTF-8 Structural Byte Validation

## What you will be able to do
You will be able to read a short fictional notice or paragraph in Russian and produce a concise summary in Russian of two or three sentences that clearly states: (1) the topic, (2) the main point, and (3) one supporting detail. Summaries should not copy full sentences from the source nor add facts not present in the text.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall and write the Russian verb for "to go" in the past tense (from Russian 096–101): write one masculine and one feminine form.
2. Recall the Russian word for "because" and use it in a short Russian phrase.
3. Recall the instrumental case ending for feminine nouns ending in -а (from Russian 096–101): give one example with translation.
4. Recall a UTF-8 concept: what is a byte boundary and why is it important when splitting a multi-byte UTF-8 sequence? Answer in one sentence.

## Core pattern
Teaching paragraph: To make a compact summary, use three short statements in sequence: topic (one short noun phrase), main point (one concise clause with a verb), and one relevant detail (a phrase or clause). Keep Russian grammar simple: prefer present or past tense, and use clear connectors like потому что / но / с (instrumental) only when they directly reflect the source.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Тема: объявления в библиотеке. | Topic: announcements in the library. |
| Главное: библиотека закрыта сегодня. | Main point: the library is closed today. |
| Деталь: закрытие продлится до пяти часов вечера. | Detail: the closure will last until 5 p.m. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Put a short noun phrase first (topic), then a single clause with the main verb (main point), then a short phrase giving one concrete detail. Meaning: This structure forces focus and prevents unnecessary repetition. Limitation/common confusion: do not try to include every minor fact; if the source contains multiple unrelated details, choose the one most clearly connected to the main point. Also, avoid inventing causes or outcomes not explicitly stated in the text.

## Worked dialogue
А: Увидел объявление у входа в музей?
*Did you see the notice at the museum entrance?*

Б: Да, там написано, что музей закрыт на реставрацию.
*Yes, it says the museum is closed for restoration.*

А: На сколько времени закрытие?
*For how long is it closed?*

Б: По объявлению, закрытие продлится до конца месяца.
*According to the notice, the closure will last until the end of the month.*

## Guided practice
1. Read the short notice: "Кафе временно не работает из-за ремонта. Открытие запланировано через две недели." Write a two-sentence Russian summary following topic → main point → one detail.
2. Summarize in Russian the fictional paragraph: "Урна на площади переполнена. Муниципальная служба обещала убрать мусор сегодня." Two sentences.
3. Take this sentence and compress: "Вечером на стадионе будет концерт, билеты продаются у входа, ожидается до трёх тысяч зрителей." Create a two-sentence Russian summary.
4. Rewrite your summary from task 1 replacing the main verb tense (present ↔ past) while preserving meaning and grammar.
5. Peer-check: exchange summaries with a classmate (or compare to a model answer below) and underline any added information not present in the original.

## Independent production exercise
Read this fictional paragraph and produce a two- or three-sentence Russian summary (topic → main point → one detail). Do not add information.

Paragraph:
"На остановке появилось новое расписание. Автобусы №5 и №7 изменили время отправления: первый рейс стал на полчаса раньше. В расписании также отмечено, что ночные маршруты работают по будним дням."

Write 6–10 sentences in Russian that describe the situation, then compress them into a two- or three-sentence summary following the taught pattern. (First part: produce 6–10 sentences retelling the paragraph in your own words; second part: produce the compact summary.)

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can consistently produce a two- or three-sentence Russian summary that: (1) begins with a clear topic noun phrase, (2) states the main point in one grammatically correct clause, (3) includes exactly one supporting detail drawn only from the source, and (4) does not copy full, consecutive sentences verbatim from the source. If you meet these criteria in three practice items, you are ready for the next lesson.
