## Core pattern
This lesson centers on a polite rescheduling pattern combining a request, a neutral reason, an alternative suggestion, and confirmation.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Не могли бы вы перенести нашу встречу на другой день? | Could you (polite) move our meeting to another day? |
| К сожалению, я не смогу прийти в назначенное время. | Unfortunately, I will not be able to come at the scheduled time. |
| Могу ли я предложить перенести на пятницу в 14:00? | May I suggest moving it to Friday at 14:00? |
| Подтвердите, пожалуйста, новое время. | Please confirm the new time. |

## Concise explanation
Form: The polite conditional "Не могли бы вы..." + infinitive is a common, neutral way to request rescheduling. To explain inability, use "К сожалению, я не смогу..." + infinitive. To propose an alternative, use "Могу ли я предложить..." or "Можем перенести на..." followed by the new date/time in accusative where relevant (dates usually in accusative or prepositional with "на"). Limitation: Russian allows multiple polite forms (e.g., "Не могли бы вы..." vs "Могли бы вы..."), and word order can vary for emphasis; do not assume direct word-for-word equivalence with English modals in all contexts.

## Worked dialogue
А: Не могли бы вы перенести нашу встречу с понедельника на пятницу?
*Could you move our meeting from Monday to Friday?*

Б: К сожалению, в пятницу у меня уже есть другая запись. Могу ли я предложить пятницу в 16:00?
*Unfortunately, I already have another appointment on Friday. May I suggest Friday at 16:00?*

А: Пятница в 16:00 мне подходит. Подтвердите, пожалуйста, место встречи — ваш офис или видеозвонок?
*Friday at 16:00 suits me. Please confirm the meeting place — your office or a video call?*

Б: Давайте видеозвонок. Я отправлю приглашение на 16:00 и пришлю ссылку.
*Let's do a video call. I will send an invitation for 16:00 and send the link.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "Could you reschedule our appointment to Wednesday at 10:30?"
2. Write a one-sentence neutral reason in Russian starting with "К сожалению,..." explaining you cannot make the original time.
3. Formulate a polite alternative suggestion in Russian beginning with "Могу ли я предложить..." and include a time.
4. Write a short confirmation request in Russian: ask the other person to confirm the new time.
5. Combine items 1–4 into a two-turn exchange (you request and propose; the partner confirms).

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional exchange (all sentences in Russian) in which you:
- Politely ask to reschedule a planned meeting,
- Give a neutral reason (work conflict, schedule overlap, etc.),
- Offer two alternative times (one preferable, one backup),
- Ask the other person to confirm which option works,
- Close politely thanking them.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model translation for Guided practice 1: "Не могли бы вы перенести нашу встречу на среду в 10:30?" — accept this or a close polite variant.
2. Model neutral reason (Guided practice 2): "К сожалению, у меня конфликт в расписании." / "К сожалению, у меня входящая встреча в это время." — either is acceptable.
3. Model alternative suggestion (Guided practice 3): "Могу ли я предложить перенести на среду в 10:30 или на четверг в 11:00?" — include one primary and one backup.
4. Model confirmation request (Guided practice 4): "Подтвердите, пожалуйста, какое время вам подходит." — precise, polite confirmation request.
