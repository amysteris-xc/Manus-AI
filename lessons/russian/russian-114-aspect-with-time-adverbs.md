## Core pattern
When a time adverb like уже, ещё, обычно, or вчера appears, it often shifts which aspect is natural: уже + perfective to mark completed result; ещё + imperfective to mark continuation; обычно + imperfective to mark habit; вчера + usually perfective to mark a completed past event (but context can allow imperfective for background).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я уже написал письмо. | I have already written the letter. (completed result; perfective) |
| Я ещё пишу письмо. | I am still writing the letter. (ongoing action; imperfective) |
| Обычно я пишу письма по утрам. | I usually write letters in the mornings. (habit; imperfective) |
| Вчера я написал отчёт. | Yesterday I wrote (completed) the report. (completed past; perfective) |

## Concise explanation
Form: pair time adverbs with either imperfective or perfective verb forms. Meaning: уже typically pairs with a perfective verb to indicate the action has reached a result (e.g., сделал, написал). Ещё commonly pairs with an imperfective to indicate continuation or non-completion (e.g., делаю, пишу). Обычно selects imperfective to express repeated or habitual actions. Вчера often signals a bounded past event and so commonly appears with perfective; however, imperfective can appear with вчера to describe background or repeated events (limitation/common confusion: time adverbs guide aspectual choice but do not force it — context and speaker intent determine whether completion or ongoingness is meant).

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты уже сделал домашнее задание?
*Have you already done the homework?*

Б: Ещё не сделал, я ещё читаю текст и записываю важные фразы.
*I haven't done it yet; I'm still reading the text and writing down important phrases.*

А: Обычно ты делаешь задания вечером?
*Do you usually do your assignments in the evening?*

Б: Да, обычно я делаю их после ужина, но вчера я сделал всё утром.
*Yes, I usually do them after dinner, but yesterday I did everything in the morning.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Convert the imperfective sentence "Я пишу письмо." into a sentence with уже + perfective that shows the letter is finished.
2. Take "Она читала книгу." and add a time adverb to show continuation (use ещё) and provide the translation.
3. Create a sentence about your habit using обычно and an imperfective verb (e.g., гулять, читать, есть).
4. Rewrite "Вчера я готовил ужин." as a completed event with a perfective verb and translate both versions, stating why the aspect changed.
5. Explain in one sentence why "Я уже читаю книгу" and "Я уже прочитал книгу" communicate different expected meanings.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a student’s day using at least four of these time words: уже, ещё, обычно, вчера, and optionally сегодня or потом. Include a mix of imperfective and perfective verbs. Make sure each Russian sentence is followed by a precise English translation. Aim to show: a habitual routine, one ongoing action, and one completed past event.
