## Core pattern

In Russian, you travel “on” a vehicle: **на автобусе** “by bus”, **на поезде** “by train”, **на метро** “by metro”. The transport noun takes the prepositional ending **-е** (except indeclinable **метро**).

| Russian phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| на автобусе | by bus |
| на поезде | by train |
| на метро | by metro |
| пешком | on foot (adverb) |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Как ты едешь в библиотеку?
> *How do you go to the library?*
>
> **Б:** Я еду на автобусе.
> *I go by bus.*
>
> **А:** А твой друг?
> *And your friend?*
>
> **Б:** Он идёт пешком.
> *He goes on foot.*
