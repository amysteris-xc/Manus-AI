## What you will be able to do
You will be able to:
- Recognize and use common verb aspect pairs to contrast ongoing/habitual actions with completed results.
- Produce simple sentences that clearly mark whether an action is viewed as a process/habit or as completed.
- Avoid the common mistake of treating the perfective form as appropriate for habitual contexts.

## Core pattern
When contrasting process/habit (imperfective) with completed result (perfective), use an aspect pair. Common pairs: читать / прочитать, писать / написать, готовить / приготовить.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я читаю книгу. | I am reading a book. (process or habitual) |
| Я прочитал книгу. | I read the book. (I finished reading it — completed result) |
| Она пишет письмо. | She is writing a letter. (process or habitual) |
| Она написала письмо. | She wrote the letter. (completed result) |
| Мы готовим ужин. | We are preparing dinner. (process) |
| Мы приготовили ужин. | We prepared / have prepared dinner. (completed result) |

## Concise explanation
Imperfective verbs (читать, писать, готовить) describe actions as processes, repeated/habitual events, or ongoing activities. Perfective verbs (прочитать, написать, приготовить) indicate the action reached completion or a single completed event. Limitation: not every perfective is formed by a single predictable prefix; some aspect pairs use suppletive roots or different derivations, so learn common pairs individually rather than assuming any prefix will work.

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты читаешь эту книгу?
*Are you reading this book?*

Б: Да, я читаю её каждую ночь.
*Yes, I read it every night.*

А: Ты прочитал её уже?
*Have you finished reading it already?*

Б: Нет, ещё не прочитал — я только на странице сто.
*No, I haven't finished it yet — I'm only on page one hundred.*

А: Напишешь сообщение, когда закончишь?
*Will you write the message when you finish?*

Б: Да, я напишу его вечером.
*Yes, I will write it in the evening.*

## Guided practice
1. Transform the imperfective sentence "Я читаю газету." into a sentence that says the reading is completed.
2. Change "Она пишет отчёт." to show that she finished the report.
3. Create a pair: write one sentence with готовить that shows a process and one with приготовить that shows completion.
4. Correct this error: A learner wrote "Я написал книгу каждый день." Explain and write the correct habitual version.
5. Translate into Russian: "They were preparing dinner when I arrived." (Use imperfective for the ongoing action.)

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph about a friend who spends a week on a small project. In your paragraph:
- Use at least three imperfective verbs to describe habitual actions or ongoing work.
- Use at least two perfective verbs to state completed results.
- Be explicit about one deadline or completed milestone.
Example elements you might include: reading, writing, preparing, finishing. Keep sentences simple and clear.
