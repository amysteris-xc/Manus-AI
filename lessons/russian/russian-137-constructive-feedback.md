## What you will be able to do
You will be able to offer short, respectful constructive feedback in Russian that (1) begins with a positive observation about the work, (2) separates the person from the work, and (3) gives one specific suggestion for improvement using softening language and evidence phrases.

## Core pattern
Brief teaching: Use a three-part structure for concise feedback: (1) Positive observation, (2) Separation phrase that avoids blaming, (3) One specific suggestion with a softener and evidence phrase.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Мне понравилось, как вы объяснили идею. | I liked how you explained the idea. |
| Это не связано с вами лично, но текст иногда непоследователен. | This is not about you personally, but the text is sometimes inconsistent. |
| Возможно, стоит добавить пример, потому что я заметил(а) разрыв в аргументации. | Maybe you should add an example, because I noticed a gap in the reasoning. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Combine a positive observation with a separation phrase (e.g., "это не связано с вами лично" or "не как человек, а как работа") and follow with a suggestion softened by words like "возможно", "может быть", or "я бы предложил(а)". Use evidence phrases such as "я заметил(а), что..." or "по моему опыту..." to anchor the suggestion in observable facts. Limitation: Russian often drops subjects and uses short forms; be careful to keep enough context so the listener clearly knows whether you refer to the person or the work to avoid misunderstanding.

## Worked dialogue
А: Мне понравилась структура вашего отчёта.
*I liked the structure of your report.*

Б: Спасибо, я старался. Что вы ещё заметили?
*Thanks, I tried. What else did you notice?*

А: Я заметил(а), что в разделе с данными есть несколько несоответствий. Это не связано с вами лично, но, возможно, стоит перепроверить таблицы.
*I noticed that there are a few inconsistencies in the data section. This is not about you personally, but maybe it's worth double-checking the tables.*

Б: Хорошая идея. Я могу пересмотреть таблицы и добавить примеры для ясности.
*Good idea. I can review the tables and add examples for clarity.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite this feedback making it softer: "Ваше предложение неправильно." Provide a positive start and an evidence phrase.
2. Translate into Russian: "I liked your presentation, but maybe add one clear example because I noticed a jump between ideas."
3. Replace the blaming sentence "Вы сделали ошибку" with two sentences that separate person and work and include a suggestion.
4. Read a short paragraph (fictional) and write one-sentence feedback: one positive observation + one specific improvement using "возможно" and "я заметил(а), что...".
5. Role-play: write a 4-turn mini-dialogue where you give feedback about a slide deck using the core pattern.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional reply to a colleague's draft email (6–10 sentences). Use the three-part feedback structure at least twice in the text: (1) start each feedback with a genuine positive observation, (2) include a separation phrase so the feedback is about the draft not the person, and (3) finish with a specific suggestion using a softener ("возможно", "может быть", "я бы предложил(а)") and an evidence phrase ("я заметил(а), что...", "по моему опыту..."). Aim for clear, polite language and vary vocabulary (e.g., "мне понравилось", "хорошая идея", "это не о вас").
