## Core pattern
Short teaching paragraph:
Reflexive verbs in Russian attach -ся or -сь to the infinitive or conjugated form; this ending is part of the verb and often indicates the action is directed back at the subject (reflexive) or shared between participants (reciprocal). Below are common daily-action patterns in present tense.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я учусь в университете. | I study (am studying) at the university. |
| Мы встречаемся вечером. | We meet (are meeting) in the evening. |
| Она готовится к экзамену. | She is preparing (herself) for the exam. |
| Ты одеваешься быстро. | You dress (yourself) quickly. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Attach -ся (after a vowel) or -сь (after a consonant at the end of the verb form) to the verb; the reflexive ending follows conjugation (e.g., готовлюсь, готовишься, готовятся). Meaning: -ся/-сь commonly marks that the subject undergoes or participates in the action (reflexive) or that the action is mutual (reciprocal), and in many verbs it changes the basic transitive meaning to an intransitive one. Limitation/common confusion: Not every verb can take -ся productively, and for some verbs -ся produces an idiomatic sense that must be learned (e.g., слышать vs. слышаться have different meanings); do not assume a simple one-to-one mapping of transitive ↔ reflexive.

## Worked dialogue
А: Привет! Во сколько ты обычно встаёшь?
*Hi! What time do you usually get up?*

Б: Я встаю в семь утра и потом быстро одеваюсь.
*I get up at seven in the morning and then I dress quickly.*

А: Ты готовишься к экзамену сейчас?
*Are you preparing for the exam now?*

Б: Да, я готовлюсь каждый вечер и учусь два часа.
*Yes, I prepare every evening and study for two hours.*

А: Вы часто встречаетесь с одноклассниками?
*Do you often meet with classmates?*

Б: Мы встречаемся раз в неделю после занятий.
*We meet once a week after classes.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Conjugate учиться in present tense for: я, ты, он/она, мы, вы, они. Write each full Russian form with an English gloss.
2. Convert the transitive sentence Она готовит суп. into a reflexive/appropriate non-transitive sentence if natural, and explain if conversion is not possible. Provide both Russian and English.
3. Translate into Russian: "They meet at the café on Saturdays." Use a reflexive verb if appropriate.
4. Change the sentence Я встречаю друга вечером. to a reflexive or reciprocal version if you want to say "We meet each other in the evening." Provide Russian and English.
5. Write three short Russian sentences (one about study, one about meeting, one about preparing) using -ся/-сь in present tense about your fictional daily routine; translate each into English.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) describing a week in the life of a student who balances classes, study, and social meetings. Use at least four different reflexive verbs from this lesson or clearly related verbs (for example, учиться, готовиться, встречаться, собираться, умываться, одеваться). Each sentence must be in present tense and include an English translation immediately after the Russian sentence. Keep the paragraph coherent: show routine, a planned meeting, and study preparation.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Conjugation model for учиться: я учусь — I study; ты учишься — you (singular) study; он/она учится — he/she studies; мы учимся — we study; вы учитесь — you (plural/formal) study; они учатся — they study.
2. Она готовит суп. → Conversion: This direct transitive sentence lacks a natural reflexive counterpart meaning "she prepares herself soup." You can say Она готовится к обеду. — She is preparing for lunch. (Explanation: готовить (to cook for someone) vs. готовиться (to prepare oneself/for something).)
3. "They meet at the café on Saturdays." → Они встречаются в кафе по субботам.
4. "We meet each other in the evening." modeling: Я встречаю друга вечером. → Мы встречаемся друг с другом вечером. — We meet each other in the evening.
