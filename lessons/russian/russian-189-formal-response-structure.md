## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to compose a short, formal Russian response that (1) acknowledges a reported problem without admitting fault, (2) expresses understanding and concern, and (3) states a specific next step you will take or recommend — while avoiding promises you cannot guarantee.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall the polite address forms from Russian 096–101: write the formal "you" in the dative case for a single addressee.
2. Recall use of the genitive after "нет" from Russian 096–101: form a negation of possession.
3. Recall the standard polite modal "могли бы" for requests from Russian 096–101: create a brief request to a colleague.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: explain why you should not promise delivery times when network latency can introduce a 1–3 second variability (state the concept of uncertainty/variance in time-sensitive systems).

## Core pattern
Use this concise pattern: Acknowledge + Express concern + State next step (conditional or with limited commitment language).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Спасибо за ваше сообщение, мы это приняли к сведению. | Thank you for your message; we have taken note of it. |
| Мы сожалеем о причинённых неудобствах и изучим ситуацию. | We regret the inconvenience caused and will investigate the situation. |
| На следующем этапе мы соберём дополнительную информацию и свяжемся с вами. | As a next step we will collect additional information and contact you. |

## Concise explanation
The form here is formal and neutral. Begin with a polite acknowledgement (Спасибо за..., Благодарим за...), then state concern with мягкое выражение (e.g., сожалеем о...), and finish with a next step using verbs that limit commitment (e.g., изучим, постараемся, планируем) or conditional phrasing (если подтвердится, мы...). Limitation: avoid absolute promises like "обеспечим" or "гарантируем" unless you truly control the outcome; prefer verbs that indicate intent or planned action.

## Worked dialogue
А: Добрый день. Мы получили жалобу на задержку доставки заказа №1234.
*Good afternoon. We received a complaint about a delayed delivery for order No. 1234.*

Б: Спасибо за сообщение, мы это приняли к сведению.
*Thank you for the message; we have taken note of it.*

А: Клиент утверждает, что посылка не пришла вовремя и он недоволен.
*The customer claims the package did not arrive on time and is dissatisfied.*

Б: Мы сожалеем о причинённых неудобствах и изучим ситуацию; если подтвердится задержка, мы предложим варианты решения.
*We regret the inconvenience caused and will investigate the situation; if the delay is confirmed, we will propose resolution options.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite "Спасибо за ваше сообщение" in a slightly firmer tone appropriate for a formal department reply.
2. Change "мы изучим ситуацию" to a conditional phrase that depends on verification.
3. Replace "сожалеем о причинённых неудобствах" with another polite expression of concern (keep formal register).
4. Compose a one-sentence next step that uses "планируем" and avoids guarantees.
5. Convert a promise "мы вернём деньги" into a limited alternative "мы рассмотрим возможность возврата средств" (maintain politeness and non-commitment).

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional formal response from a customer support manager to a client who reports intermittent login failures. Your response must:
- Begin with a polite acknowledgment of the client’s report.
- Include one sentence expressing concern without admitting fault.
- Include one conditional sentence with "если" or a verb in future conditional meaning ("если подтвердится", "в случае если").
- State one concrete next step you will take that does not guarantee an outcome (use verbs like "исследуем", "планируем", "рассмотрим").
- Offer a limited tentative timeline phrased cautiously (e.g., "в течение нескольких рабочих дней" but avoid an exact guaranteed time).
- End with a formal closing sentence offering further contact.

All Russian sentences must use Cyrillic and be accompanied by precise English translations on the next line.

## Final self-check
You are ready to proceed if you can: (1) produce a 6–10 sentence formal reply that acknowledges an issue without admitting fault; (2) state one next step using cautious or conditional language; and (3) avoid absolute guarantees or promises outside your control. If you cannot meet these three criteria consistently, repeat the Guided practice and Independent production.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model sentence for polite acknowledgement: "Благодарим вас за сообщение о проблеме с входом." — "We thank you for the message about the login issue."
2. Model conditional concern: "Мы сожалеем о причинённых неудобствах и изучим это; если подтвердится техническая ошибка, мы предложим возможные решения." — "We regret the inconvenience caused and will investigate this; if a technical error is confirmed, we will propose possible solutions."
3. Model cautious next step with timeline: "На следующем этапе мы соберём логи и проанализируем их в течение нескольких рабочих дней." — "As a next step we will collect logs and analyze them within several business days."
4. Model formal closing: "Пожалуйста, свяжитесь с нами, если у вас появятся дополнительные детали." — "Please contact us if you have additional details."
