## Core pattern
The core patterns in this lesson are short phrases that express regret and a follow-up suggestion.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Жаль. | It's a pity. / That's a shame. |
| К сожалению, не получилось. | Unfortunately, it didn't work out. |
| Жаль, но это нормально. | It's a pity, but that's okay. |
| К сожалению, сейчас нет. | Unfortunately, it's not available now. |
| Жаль, давайте попробуем позже. | That's a pity, let's try later. |

## Concise explanation
"Жаль" is a short impersonal exclamation meaning "it's a pity" or "what a shame"; it does not change for gender, number, or case and is commonly followed by a clause or standalone. "К сожалению" is a fixed introductory phrase meaning "unfortunately" and is used before a statement. A common confusion is treating "жаль" like an adjective that must agree with a noun — it does not; it functions like an impersonal comment. Use neutral, non-accusatory language with these phrases to keep statements respectful. When suggesting next steps, simple verbs in infinitive or imperative are natural (e.g., "давайте попробуем", "попробуйте позже").

## Worked dialogue
А: Жаль, билетов на концерт сейчас нет.
*It's a pity, there are no concert tickets now.*

Б: К сожалению, да. Можно подождать и проверить снова завтра?
*Unfortunately, yes. Can we wait and check again tomorrow?*

А: Жаль, я попробую позвонить организатору.
*It's a pity, I'll try to call the organizer.*

Б: Хорошо, давайте попробуем позже.
*Okay, let's try later.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Translate to Russian: "It's a pity, the book is sold out." Use either "жаль" or "к сожалению".
2. Rewrite this sentence using "к сожалению": "Я опоздал, и не попал на встречу."
3. Create a short two-sentence exchange where one person says "Жаль" about a cancelled plan and the other proposes "давайте перенесём".
4. Replace the phrase "Очень жаль" with a neutral "жаль" in a polite sentence and explain (one short sentence) why the neutral form may be preferable.
5. Compose a single Russian sentence expressing "Unfortunately, I can't join today; let's try next week." Use "к сожалению" and a suggested next step.

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences in Russian describing a fictional minor disappointment in a respectful context (for example: a missed package delivery, a canceled workshop, or a booked table that's unavailable). Include:
- At least two uses of "жаль" and/or "к сожалению".
- One polite proposed next step using "давайте" or an infinitive suggestion (e.g., "попробуем позже", "созвониться завтра").
Provide the English translation for each Russian sentence immediately after it.

Example structure to follow (do not copy): Russian sentence, then its precise English translation.
