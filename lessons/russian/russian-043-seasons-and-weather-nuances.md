## Core pattern

Precipitation is expressed with the verb **идёт**: **идёт дождь** “it is raining” and **идёт снег** “it is snowing”. For sunny or windy conditions, use adverbs like **солнечно** and **ветрено**.

| Phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Идёт дождь. | It is raining. |
| Идёт снег. | It is snowing. |
| Сегодня ветрено. | Today it is windy. |
| На улице солнечно. | It is sunny outside. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Какая сегодня погода?
> *What is the weather like today?*
>
> **Б:** На улице идёт дождь и ветрено.
> *Outside it is raining and windy.*
>
> **А:** Значит, ты дома?
> *So you are at home?*
>
> **Б:** Да, сегодня я читаю дома.
> *Yes, today I am reading at home.*
