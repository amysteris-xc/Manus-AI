## 1. Use `Где?` to ask about location

**Где?** means “Where?” Use it when you ask about the place where a person or action is located.[1] This lesson uses **Где?** for location only. Do not use it yet for “Where are you going?”; motion and destination use different patterns that will come later.

```text
Где ты?
Where are you? — informal singular

Где вы?
Where are you? — polite singular or plural
```

Russian often omits a present-tense word corresponding to English “am,” “is,” or “are.” Therefore, a location phrase can be a complete answer.

```text
Я дома.
I am at home.

Я в парке.
I am in the park.
```

The subject **я** may be omitted when the situation makes it clear:

```text
Дома.
At home.
```

Keep **я** in your early practice. It makes the sentence shape easier to see and hear.

## 2. Learn place words with their location forms

Russian place nouns often change after a location preposition. The form used with **Где?** is commonly called the **prepositional case**.[1] [2] Do not try to master every ending today. Learn each high-frequency phrase as a complete location package.

| Dictionary word | Location package | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| дом — house, home | до́ма | at home |
| шко́ла — school | в шко́ле | in/at school |
| парк — park | в па́рке | in the park |
| библиоте́ка — library | в библиоте́ке | in/at the library |
| рабо́та — work | на рабо́те | at work |

Two useful observations are visible in the table:

1. Nouns ending in **-а** often use **-е** in these location packages: **школа → в школе**, **библиотека → в библиотеке**, and **работа → на работе**.
2. Some masculine nouns add **-е**: **парк → в парке**.

These observations are only a starting pattern, not a complete rule for every Russian noun. The safest beginner method is to memorize the whole phrase, including **в** or **на** and the changed noun ending.

> **Location-package rule:** Learn **в школе**, not only **школа**; learn **на работе**, not only **работа**. The preposition and noun form work together.

## 3. `в`, `на`, and `дома` in today’s phrases

In basic location statements, **в** often corresponds to English “in” or “at” for enclosed places or institutions, such as a school or library.[1] **На** appears in several frequent location phrases, including **на работе** “at work.” Learn the choice with the phrase rather than translating one English preposition word-for-word.

| Russian phrase | Natural English translation | Use today |
|---|---|---|
| в школе | at school / in school | a location where someone studies or works |
| в парке | in the park | a location in a park |
| в библиотеке | at the library / in the library | a location where someone reads or studies |
| на работе | at work | a location connected with work |
| дома | at home | a special complete location word |

**Дома** is a high-value phrase for “at home.” For this lesson, use **Я дома** as one unit. Do not replace it with `*Я в дома`; that is not the correct location phrase.

## 4. Combine places with familiar present-tense verbs

Russian 005 gave you **работаю**, **читаю**, and **учусь**. Add a location package to say where the action happens.

```text
Я работаю в школе.
I work at a school.

Я читаю в библиотеке.
I read in the library.

Я учусь в школе.
I study at school.
```

You can place the location first for emphasis or context:

```text
В библиотеке я читаю.
In the library, I read.

Дома я учусь.
At home, I study.
```

Both orders are useful. Start with **subject + verb + location** when you are building a new sentence. Use **location + subject + verb** after you can recognize the phrase as a unit.

## 5. Ask about a person or an activity

Here are three safe patterns to memorize. The answer uses the same location packages.

| Question | Answer | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| Где ты? | Я дома. | Where are you? — I am at home. |
| Где ты читаешь? | Я читаю в библиотеке. | Where do you read? — I read at the library. |
| Где вы учитесь? | Я учусь в школе. | Where do you study? — I study at school. |

Notice the subject form in the questions:

```text
Где ты читаешь?
Where do you read? — informal singular

Где вы учитесь?
Where do you study? — polite singular or plural
```

The answer may keep **я** even when the question already makes the subject obvious. This is helpful at the beginner stage and remains grammatically normal.

## 6. Worked dialogue: locate a reading routine

Read the dialogue slowly with study stress marks. Cover the English and say both roles aloud. The dialogue reuses greetings and routine verbs from earlier lessons.

> **А:** Здра́вствуйте! Где вы?
> *Hello! Where are you?*
>
> **Б:** Я в библиоте́ке.
> *I am at the library.*
>
> **А:** Вы чита́ете в библиоте́ке?
> *Do you read at the library?*
>
> **Б:** Да, я чита́ю в библиоте́ке. А вы?
> *Yes, I read at the library. And you?*
>
> **А:** Я до́ма. Ве́чером я учу́сь до́ма.
> *I am at home. In the evening, I study at home.*
>
> **Б:** О́чень хорошо́!
> *Very good!*

Read the same dialogue without study accents:

```text
Здравствуйте! Где вы?
Я в библиотеке.
Вы читаете в библиотеке?
Да, я читаю в библиотеке. А вы?
Я дома. Вечером я учусь дома.
Очень хорошо!
```

The phrase **учу́сь до́ма** means “I study at home.” The location word does not need a separate preposition in this phrase.

## 8. Independent production exercise

Write and say a short four-line location check-in. Use a real or invented routine; do not include an address, travel plan, workplace, school, or any other sensitive location detail. General categories are enough.

Use this model and replace at least two location packages:


Read all five lines aloud twice. On the second pass, cover the English and identify every complete location package: **дома**, **в школе**, **в парке**, **в библиотеке**, or **на работе**.

## Spoiler: answers and model response

1. **я работаю**, **я читаю**, **я учусь**.
2. **вы читаете** is polite singular or plural.
3. **новое**.
4. The soft sign **ь** softens the preceding consonant. It does not independently determine noun gender.
5. An array parameter is treated as a pointer and does not retain the caller’s array length. The helper needs the explicit count to know its valid traversal boundary.
6. **Где?** means “Where?” for a location.
7. **Я дома.**
8. **Я читаю в библиотеке.**
9. **Я учусь в школе.**
10. **В парке я читаю.** means “In the park, I read.”
11. **на работе** means “at work.”
12. **Где вы учитесь?**
13. The safe forward-loop condition is `index < count`, for example:

```c
for (size_t index = 0; index < count; ++index) {
    use(values[index]);
}
```

14. A model safe location check-in is:

> Где вы?
> Я дома.
> Где вы читаете?
> Я читаю в библиотеке.
> Вечером я учусь дома.
