# Russian 123 — Describing People with Simple Clauses
Filename slug: russian-123-describing-people-with-clauses.md
Prerequisites: Russian 001–122
Estimated study time: 25–35 minutes
Author: Manus AI
Learning objective: describe a person using a familiar noun plus a short descriptive clause.
Teaching focus: Use the controlled nominative patterns with `который` and common present-tense verbs. Avoid complex relative-pronoun case changes.

## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to combine a simple noun (e.g., "человек", "мужчина", "женщина", "ребёнок", "студент") with a short descriptive clause introduced by который to say who someone is and what they are doing in the present tense. You will form clear, controlled sentences like "Человек, который улыбается, мой друг." Focus is on nominative subjects and present-tense verbs.

## Core pattern
Use a simple noun in nominative + comma + который + present-tense clause. Keep который in nominative (который) because it refers to the subject noun in nominative.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Человек, который улыбается, мой друг. | The person who is smiling is my friend. |
| Студент, который читает книгу, очень внимательный. | The student who is reading a book is very attentive. |
| Женщина, которая говорит по-русски, из Москвы. | The woman who speaks Russian is from Moscow. |

## Concise explanation
Form: [Noun in nominative], который + [verb in present 3rd-person singular] + (rest). Meaning: you name a person and immediately add a short clause describing what that person does or is like, using который to link the noun and the clause. Limitation/common confusion: this lesson keeps который in nominative because the noun it refers to is the subject of the clause. Do not change which case который takes here — that requires different sentence structures (object, indirect object, or prepositional usage) and is beyond this lesson.

## Worked dialogue
А: Привет! Ты знаешь мужчину, который работает в библиотеке?
*Hi! Do you know the man who works in the library?*

Б: Да. Мужчина, который работает в библиотеке, живёт рядом.
*Yes. The man who works in the library lives nearby.*

А: А кто женщина, которая читает газету у входа?
*And who is the woman who reads the newspaper at the entrance?*

Б: Это преподаватель, которая преподаёт историю.
*That is the teacher who teaches history.*

## Guided practice
1. Convert the noun "ребёнок" into a full sentence: "The child who is playing is outside." Use который + играть (present).
2. Make a sentence: "The woman who is smiling is my neighbour." Use женщина + улыбаться.
3. Describe: "The man who is talking is a doctor." Use мужчина + говорить + врач.
4. Replace "студент" with "студентка" and write: "The female student who reads is quiet." Use читать.
5. Create a negative descriptive clause: "The person who is not working is at home." Use человек + не работать.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about people in a small café. Each sentence must use the core pattern: a noun in nominative + который + a present-tense verb clause (you may repeat nouns). Vary the verbs (e.g., читать, пить, говорить, улыбаться, ждать). Keep sentences simple and present-tense. Example start (do not copy exactly): "Мужчина, который читает газету, пьёт кофе." Continue to create five more similar sentences to make a 6–10 sentence paragraph.

You are ready to move on if you can: (1) produce at least five correct sentences using a nominative noun + который + present-tense 3rd-person verb without case errors; (2) explain why который stays in nominative in these sentences (because it refers to the subject noun); and (3) spot and correct one common mistake: using который but then putting the verb in a non-3rd-person form or mismatching singular/plural.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model for Guided practice 1: "Ребёнок, который играет, на улице." — noun "ребёнок" (singular), который in nominative, играет = 3rd-person singular.
2. Model for Guided practice 2: "Женщина, которая улыбается, моя соседка." — улыбается is 3rd-person singular reflexive form of улыбаться.
3. Model for Guided practice 3: "Мужчина, который говорит, — врач." — говориT? Use говорит (3rd-person), and dash/comma as needed; врач as predicate noun.
4. Example independent paragraph criteria: six sentences, each in the form [Noun], который + [3rd-person present verb], varied verbs (читать, пить, говорить, улыбаться, ждать, смотреть).
