# Russian 053 — Asking for Help and Clarification

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–052 and the immediately preceding lessons

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to ask someone to slow down, repeat, or clarify using polite phrases like **повторите медленнее** and **я не понимаю**.

## Core pattern

Vital conversational management tools: **Я не понимаю** “I do not understand”, **Говорите медленнее, пожалуйста** “Speak slower, please”, and **Что это значит?** “What does this mean?”.

| Clarification phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Я не понимаю. | I do not understand. |
| Повторите, пожалуйста. | Please repeat. |
| Говорите чуть медленнее. | Speak a little slower. |
| Что значит это слово? | What does this word mean? |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Извините, я не понимаю это слово.
> *Excuse me, I do not understand this word.*
>
> **Б:** Хорошо, я объясню медленно.
> *Alright, I will explain slowly.*
>
> **А:** Спасибо за помощь.
> *Thank you for the help.*
>
> **Б:** Пожалуйста, обращайтесь.
> *You are welcome, feel free to ask.*
