## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Ask "Where to?" and "Where from?" using куда and откуда.
- Ask and answer "How do I get to...?" using как добраться and simple transport verbs.
- Say basic transport means: автобус, трамвай, метро, такси, поезд, пешком.
- Describe a simple route plan with a known destination phrase (e.g., "в аэропорт", "на вокзал", "в центр").

## Core pattern
Short teaching paragraph:
We will combine the question phrase "Как добраться до + destination?" with simple transport words and the particles куда / откуда to ask and answer about routes. Keep destinations in prepositional or accusative fixed phrases you already know (e.g., "в магазин", "на вокзал", "в аэропорт").

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Как добраться до вокзала? | How do I get to the train station? |
| Куда вы едете? | Where are you going? |
| Откуда отправляется автобус? | Where does the bus depart from? |
| Я поеду на метро в центр. | I will go by metro to the center. |

## Concise explanation
Form and meaning: "Как добраться до X?" literally asks "How to reach X?" and expects answers that name transport and brief route steps, for example "На метро" or "На автобусе, затем пешком." Use куда to ask destination ("Куда вы идёте?") and откуда to ask origin ("Откуда вы?"). Limitation / common confusion: do not try to apply complex case alternations here—use the fixed short destination phrases you already know (e.g., "в центр", "на вокзал", "в аэропорт"). For beginners, it's simpler to answer with a transport word + destination phrase than to give full case-based multi-step directions.

## Worked dialogue
А: Как добраться до аэропорта?
*How do I get to the airport?*

Б: Отсюда? На автобусе №10 или на такси.
*From here? By bus number 10 or by taxi.*

А: А куда отправляется автобус?
*And where does the bus depart from?*

Б: Автобус отправляется с остановки возле метро.
*The bus departs from the stop near the metro.*

А: Сколько времени до центра на метро?
*How long is it to the center by metro?*

Б: Около двадцати минут, если без пробок.
*About twenty minutes if there is no traffic.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "How do I get to the train station?" and write both question and a short answer "By tram, then walk."
2. Ask in Russian "Where are you going?" and answer "I am going to the museum by bus."
3. Write a short Russian question asking "Where from does the taxi leave?" and answer "From the main entrance."
4. Create two short Russian sentences: one using куда and one using откуда, each about a market or park.
5. Replace the transport word in "Я поеду на метро в центр." with at least three other transport words (one per line) keeping the same destination.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph in Russian describing how you (a traveler) will reach a hotel from an airport. Include:
- One sentence asking "Как добраться до гостиницы?" or similar,
- One sentence using куда or откуда,
- At least two different transport words from this lesson (e.g., автобус, такси, метро, поезд, пешком),
- One sentence giving a time estimate (e.g., "около тридцати минут"),
- Conclude with a sentence stating your chosen plan (one transport option).
Keep sentences simple and use destination phrases like "в гостиницу", "в центр", or "на вокзал."

Example requirement: produce 6–10 sentences, each sentence in Russian; include an English translation in your notes if you want to check yourself (not required for submission).

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for Guided practice #1: "Как добраться до вокзала? — На трамвае, затем пешком." (How do I get to the train station? — By tram, then on foot.)
2. Model answer for Guided practice #2: "Куда вы идёте? — Я еду в музей на автобусе." (Where are you going? — I'm going to the museum by bus.)
3. Model answer for Guided practice #3: "Откуда отправляется такси? — Оно отправляется от главного входа." (Where does the taxi leave from? — It leaves from the main entrance.)
4. Model independent paragraph (6 sentences, example):
- "Как добраться до гостиницы?"
  *How can I get to the hotel?*
- "Я прилетел в аэропорт и не знаю дорогу."
  *I arrived at the airport and don't know the way.*
- "Откуда отправляется автобус до города?"
  *From where does the bus to the city depart?*
- "Автобус отправляется с терминала, но я могу взять такси."
  *The bus departs from the terminal, but I can take a taxi.*
- "На такси дорога займёт около тридцати минут."
  *By taxi the trip will take about thirty minutes.*
- "Я поеду на такси прямо в гостиницу."
  *I will go by taxi directly to the hotel.*
