## Core pattern

Use **рядом с** “next to” and **около** “near / by”. Note that **около** is followed by a genitive singular ending (e.g. **около парка** “near the park”).

| Preposition | Example | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| около (+ Gen) | Дом около парка. | The house is near the park. |
| около (+ Gen) | Библиотека около метро. | The library is near the metro. |
| рядом с | Стол рядом с окном. | The table is next to the window. |
| далеко | Парк далеко. | The park is far away. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Где находится библиотека?
> *Where is the library located?*
>
> **Б:** Библиотека около парка.
> *The library is near the park.*
>
> **А:** А метро далеко?
> *And is the metro far?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, метро рядом с библиотекой.
> *No, the metro is next to the library.*
