## Core pattern

To say a body part hurts, say **у меня болит** plus a singular body part: **у меня болит голова** “I have a headache / my head hurts”. For plural body parts, Russian uses **болят**.

| Phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| голова | head |
| рука | arm / hand |
| нога | leg / foot |
| У меня болит голова. | My head hurts. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Как ты себя чувствуешь?
> *How do you feel?*
>
> **Б:** Не очень хорошо. У меня болит голова.
> *Not very well. My head hurts.*
>
> **А:** Тебе нужен отдых?
> *Do you need rest?*
>
> **Б:** Да, я буду отдыхать.
> *Yes, I will rest.*
