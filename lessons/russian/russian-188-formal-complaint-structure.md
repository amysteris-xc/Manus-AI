## Core pattern
A concise neutral complaint typically follows this short template: context sentence, factual issue sentence, requested response sentence, polite close.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Уведомляю вас о следующем. | I notify you of the following. |
| Произошла ошибка в счёте от 12 марта 2026 года. | An error occurred in the invoice dated 12 March 2026. |
| Прошу откорректировать счёт и предоставить подтверждение исправления в течение 10 рабочих дней. | I request that you correct the invoice and provide confirmation of the correction within 10 business days. |
| С уважением, Иванова А. | Sincerely, Ivanova A. |

## Concise explanation
Formal complaints in Russian are typically concise and use neutral, impersonal wording. Start with a contextual opener (e.g., Уведомляю вас о следующем), state the factual problem with specific data (date, reference number), clearly state the requested action with a polite verb (прошу, прошу Вас), and end with a polite closing (С уважением or С уважением, [Name]). Limitation: avoid overly emotional language in formal Russian letters — such exaggeration weakens the formality and can be culturally inappropriate.

## Worked dialogue
А: Уведомляю вас о следующем: счёт № 452 от 12.03.2026 содержит неверную сумму.
*I notify you of the following: invoice No. 452 dated 12.03.2026 contains an incorrect amount.*

Б: Можете уточнить, какая именно сумма указана неверно?
*Can you clarify which exact amount is incorrect?*

А: В графе «Итого» указана сумма 15 000 руб., тогда как должно быть 5 000 руб. по приложенному акту.
*In the "Total" column the amount 15,000 RUB is indicated, whereas it should be 5,000 RUB according to the attached act.*

Б: Понял(а). Просим прощения; просим корректировать счёт и прислать исправленный документ на этот адрес электронной почты.
*Understood. We apologize; we ask you to correct the invoice and send the amended document to this email address.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "I notify you that the delivery on April 2 was incomplete." Provide a concise single-sentence version.
2. Rewrite the sentence above to include a requested response: ask for a corrected delivery confirmation within 5 business days.
3. Replace the word "прошу" with a more formal variant "настаиваю" and explain briefly (one sentence) how the tone changes.
4. Create a one-line polite closing using a female name and surname initial.
5. Identify and correct the error in this Russian sentence: "Прошу вас, отправьте мне исправление." (Hint: formal written style prefers avoiding a comma after "Прошу вас".)

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional formal complaint of 6–10 sentences (keep it neutral and concise). Use this structure:
- 1 sentence: context (who you are or a reference number);
- 2 sentences: factual issue with at least one specific date or number;
- 2 sentences: requested response (what you want) including a reasonable deadline;
- 1–2 sentences: an offer to provide documents or contact information;
- 1 sentence: polite closing with your name.
All Russian sentences must be grammatically correct and use Cyrillic.

## Spoiler: answers and model response

2. Model answer for Retrieval practice 2: "прошу" or "прошу Вас" as the common formal verb for request.
3. Model polite closing for Retrieval practice 3: "С уважением, [Имя Фамилия]" (e.g., "С уважением, Иванов И.").
4. Example model independent production (6 sentences):
   1) "Уведомляю вас, что счёт-фактура № 78 от 02.04.2026 получен."
   *I notify you that invoice No. 78 dated 02.04.2026 was received.*
   2) "В нём указана неверная сумма в графе «Итого»: 20 000 руб., вместо 2 000 руб."
   *It shows an incorrect amount in the "Total" column: 20,000 RUB instead of 2,000 RUB.*
   3) "Прошу корректировать счёт-фактуру и выслать исправленную версию."
   *I request that you correct the invoice and send the amended version.*
   4) "Прошу сделать это в течение 10 рабочих дней с момента получения этого письма."
   *Please do this within 10 business days of receiving this letter.*
   5) "Готов(а) предоставить копии платёжных документов по запросу."
   *I am ready to provide copies of payment documents upon request.*
   6) "С уважением, Петрова А."
   *Sincerely, Petrova A.*
