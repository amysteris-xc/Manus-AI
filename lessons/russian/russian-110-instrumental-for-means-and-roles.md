## Core pattern
The instrumental case often marks the means (with what), a companion (with whom), or a role (as what). Below are concise, high-utility patterns.

Russian | Precise English translation
--- | ---
пишу ручкой | I write (using) a pen
езжу на автобусе | I travel (by) bus
работаю учителем | I work as a teacher
иду с другом | I go (walking) with a friend

## Concise explanation
Form: The instrumental case changes the noun ending depending on gender and number (e.g., ручка → ручкой, автобус → автобусом, друг → другом, учитель → учителем). Meaning: use it to indicate the instrument or means (what you use), a companion (with whom), or a role/occupation (as what). Limitation/common confusion: not every "with" in English uses the instrumental in Russian—prepositions like "о" (about) use the prepositional case, and movement prepositions (e.g., "с" for "with" when meaning accompaniment) use the instrumental, but "со" may be required before certain consonant clusters. Do not assume a single ending works for all nouns — endings depend on gender, number, and stem.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я пишу ручкой.
*I write with a pen.*

Б: А я пишу карандашом.
*I write with a pencil.*

А: Ты работаешь учителем или студентом?
*Do you work as a teacher or a student?*

Б: Я работаю учителем и иногда работаю с коллегой.
*I work as a teacher and sometimes work with a colleague.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "I travel by bus." Use the pattern from the table.
2. Translate into Russian: "She writes with a pen."
3. Make a Russian sentence saying: "We go with (our) friend." Use a possessive if you know it; otherwise use "с другом".
4. Change this sentence to say role instead of means: "Он работает поваром." — write an English translation and then make a similar sentence for "She works as a doctor."
5. Identify the instrumental noun in this sentence and explain its function: "Они говорят со студентом."

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a day in the life of a person who uses several means and works in a role. Include:
- at least two sentences with instruments (e.g., "пишу ручкой", "готовлю ножом"),
- one sentence stating the person's job using "работать + [instrumental role]" (e.g., "работаю врачом"),
- one sentence with a companion using "с + [instrumental]" (e.g., "иду с другом").
All sentences should be in Russian; after each Russian sentence, put an exact English translation on the next line. Aim for clear, concrete vocabulary rather than idioms.
