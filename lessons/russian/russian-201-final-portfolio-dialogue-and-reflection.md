## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to write a short fictional dialogue and a reflective paragraph in Russian that clearly states a goal, gives evidence, acknowledges a limitation, makes a request, records a response, and proposes a next action — all in polite, accurate Russian suitable for a portfolio or capstone exchange.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall the Russian instrumental case use with means: write the Russian phrase for "I travel by train" remembering Russian 096–101 patterns.
2. Recall the formation of past tense for verbs of motion: write "She completed the project" in Russian, using correct past tense agreement.
3. Recall use of modal necessity with нужно/надо: translate "We need to prepare the presentation" into Russian.
4. Recall a C-level byte-boundary or validation concept: state in English one short rule about byte-boundary alignment or why validating byte-size matters in data exchange.

## Core pattern
This lesson centers on clear, short statements that sequence: goal → evidence → limitation → request → response → next action. Use simple grammatical structures (infinitives, present/past tense, modal constructions, genitive for negation) and polite request forms (пожалуйста, можно ли).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Моя цель — подготовить итоговое портфолио. | My goal is to prepare the final portfolio. |
| У меня есть примеры работ и отзывы. | I have work samples and feedback. |
| Ограничение: у меня мало времени на доработку. | Limitation: I have little time for revisions. |
| Можно ли получить комментарии по структуре? | May I get comments on the structure? |
| Спасибо, я учту ваши замечания и сделаю правки. | Thank you, I will take your comments into account and make edits. |
| Следующее действие — отправить финальную версию через неделю. | Next action — send the final version in one week. |

## Concise explanation
The pattern uses a simple nominal sentence for stating goals (Моя цель — ...), perfective or past verbs for evidence of completed work (e.g., сделал/сделала, есть + noun), short noun phrases to state limitations (Ограничение: ...), modal constructions or questions with можно/пожалуйста for polite requests, and future or imperative forms for next actions. Limitation: polite register in Russian can vary by context; for formal academic feedback, use full forms (Можно ли ...? Будьте добры ...) rather than overly colloquial reductions.

## Worked dialogue
А: Моя цель — завершить портфолио к концу недели.
*My goal is to finish the portfolio by the end of the week.*

Б: У вас уже есть примеры работ и краткая аннотация?
*Do you already have work samples and a short annotation?*

А: Да, у меня есть три проекта и отзывы от двух преподавателей; но ограничение — мало времени на финальную редактуру.
*Yes, I have three projects and feedback from two instructors; but the limitation is little time for final editing.*

Б: Можно ли прислать вам замечания по структуре сейчас, и вы внесёте правки?
*May I send you comments on the structure now, and will you make edits?*

А: Пожалуйста, пришлите. Я учту замечания и отправлю обновлённую версию через пять дней.
*Please send them. I will take the comments into account and send the updated version in five days.*

## Guided practice
1. Write the sentence "My goal is to improve the abstract" in Russian using the core pattern.
2. Convert "I have two sample essays" into Russian, using correct accusative/genitive where needed.
3. Compose a one-sentence limitation beginning with "Ограничение:" about time, budget, or access to resources.
4. Formulate a polite request asking a reviewer to give feedback on organization (use Можно ли or Не могли бы вы).
5. Write a one-sentence next action using the future tense (e.g., Я отправлю / Я сделаю).

## Independent production exercise
Produce a 6–10 sentence fictional portfolio dialogue and short reflection (combine both in one paragraph or as short turns) that includes:
- a clear goal (Моя цель — ...),
- at least one piece of evidence that something is done (e.g., у меня есть, я сделал/сделала),
- a stated limitation (Ограничение: ...),
- a polite request (Можно ли..., Не могли бы вы...),
- a response from the reviewer (e.g., Спасибо, замечания полезны),
- a next action (Следующее действие — ...).
Write the whole production in Russian (Cyrillic). After each Russian sentence, include an immediate precise English translation in italics. Keep sentences grammatically correct and use the registers modeled above.

## Final self-check
Before submitting your portfolio dialogue and reflection, ensure you have: stated a clear goal (Моя цель — ...), provided concrete evidence of work, acknowledged a realistic limitation, made a polite request, recorded the reviewer's response, and proposed a specific next action with timeframe. Check verb agreement in past tense (gender/number), correct case after verbs and prepositions, and that politeness markers (пожалуйста, можно ли, не могли бы вы) match the intended formality.
