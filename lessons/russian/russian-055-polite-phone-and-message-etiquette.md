## Core pattern

Written communication etiquette: start formal notes with **Уважаемый господин / Уважаемая госпожа** “Dear Sir / Madam” and close with **С уважением** “Sincerely / With respect”.

| Etiquette phrase | Function | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| Здравствуйте. | opening | Hello / Good day. |
| Сообщаю вам, что... | body opening | I inform you that... |
| Спасибо за внимание. | pre-closing | Thank you for your attention. |
| С уважением. | closing | With respect / Sincerely. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> *Hello. I inform you that the materials are ready.*

>
> **А:** С уважением, автор курса.
> *With respect, course author.*
>
> **Б:** Всего доброго.
> *All the best.*
