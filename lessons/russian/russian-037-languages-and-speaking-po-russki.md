## Core pattern

To say you speak a language, use the verb **говорить** plus an adverb with the **по-...-ски** pattern: **по-русски** “in Russian”, **по-английски** “in English”, **по-фински** “in Finnish”.

| Phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Я говорю по-русски. | I speak Russian. |
| Ты говоришь по-английски? | Do you speak English? |
| Он говорит по-фински. | He speaks Finnish. |
| Я немного говорю по-русски. | I speak a little Russian. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Вы говорите по-русски?
> *Do you speak Russian?*
>
> **Б:** Да, я немного говорю по-русски.
> *Yes, I speak a little Russian.*
>
> **А:** А по-английски?
> *And in English?*
>
> **Б:** По-английски я говорю хорошо.
> *In English I speak well.*
