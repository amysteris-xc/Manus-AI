- Say that you or someone else likes or dislikes something using the fixed pattern with the dative: e.g., "мне нравится музыка".
- Say whom you help using a simple verb + dative pattern: e.g., "я помогаю другу".
- Produce short sentences combining these patterns with clear noun forms (singular dative for animate persons and correct subject forms for the liked items).

## Core pattern
Learn two simple complete patterns: "кому нравится что" and "я помогаю кому".

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Мне нравится музыка. | I like music. (Literally: Music is pleasing to me.) |
| Тебе нравится фильм. | You (informal) like the movie. (Literally: The movie is pleasing to you.) |
| Я помогаю другу. | I help (my) friend (male). |

## Concise explanation
"Мне нравится..." uses the dative pronoun (мне, тебе, ему, ей, нам, вам, им) to indicate the person who experiences liking; the thing that is liked is the grammatical subject and usually remains in nominative (or can be an infinitive: "мне нравится читать"). "Я помогаю другу" uses the verb "помогать" (imperfective) and takes the person who receives help in the dative case (друг → другу). A common confusion: English speakers often expect the liker to be the subject; in Russian the person who likes is in dative, not nominative. Limitation: "нравиться" agrees with the thing that is liked (singular/plural) for verb form, e.g., "Мне нравится фильм" vs "Мне нравятся фильмы." For this lesson we focus on the singular patterns.

## Worked dialogue
А: Мне нравится новый ресторан.
*I like the new restaurant.*

Б: Кому он нравится? Тебе?
*Who likes it? You?*

А: Нет, ему нравится больше. Я помогаю ему с выбором.
*No, he likes it more. I help him with the choice.*

Б: Ты часто помогаешь другу выбирать еду?
*Do you often help your friend choose food?*

## Guided practice
1. Change the sentence "Мне нравится музыка" to say "He likes music" using the correct dative and verb agreement.
2. Translate into Russian: "She helps her sister." Use an explicit noun for "sister".
3. Make a negative sentence: "I don't like coffee." Provide the Russian sentence.
4. Replace "другу" with a feminine dative form for "colleague (female)" — write "I help my female colleague" in Russian.
5. Convert "Тебе нравится фильм" into a plural-liked object: "You like the books." (Use plural noun and correct verb form.)

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph about a small group of friends planning a dinner. Include:
- At least two sentences using the "кому нравится..." pattern with different people (use different dative pronouns or names in dative).
- At least one sentence using "я помогаю..." with an explicit dative noun (e.g., другу, сестре, коллеге).
- At least one negative "не нравится" sentence.
Keep each sentence simple (one clause) and use accurate dative forms.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) reliably form sentences "Мне/Тебе/Ему/Ей нравится ..." about singular items; (2) produce "Я помогаю + dative" with at least three different dative nouns; (3) correctly make one negative "не нравится" sentence. If you hesitate about which case to use for the person who likes or receives help, review the Core pattern and Concise explanation again.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Dative of "брат": брат → брату. Model sentence: "Мне нравится брат моей подруги." (Literal: The brother of my friend is pleasing to me.)
2. Dative of "сестра": сестра → сестре. Model sentence for helping: "Она помогает сестре." (She helps her sister.)
3. Conjugation of "помогать" for "я": "я помогаю". Example: "Я помогаю маме."
4. Byte-boundary validation: For UTF-8 input you must check byte counts (exact bytes) when the system requires byte-level limits; character counts can differ because some characters use multiple bytes.
