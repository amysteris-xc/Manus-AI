## Core pattern

Use **слушать музыку** “to listen to music” and destinations like **в музей** “to the museum” and **в театр** “to the theater” to build rich interest-based sentences.

| Interest phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| слушать музыку | to listen to music |
| ходить в музей | to go to a museum |
| спектакль в театре | performance in a theater |
| классическая музыка | classical music |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Какую музыку ты любишь слушать?
> *What kind of music do you like to listen to?*
>
> **Б:** Я люблю слушать классическую музыку.
> *I like to listen to classical music.*
>
> **А:** Ты часто ходишь в театр?
> *Do you often go to the theater?*
>
> **Б:** Иногда, когда есть время.
> *Sometimes, when there is time.*
