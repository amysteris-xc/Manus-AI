## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on the short negative existential `нет` + noun in the genitive and the possessive-negative `у X нет ...`. These are common, fixed, and useful patterns.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Нет времени. | There is no time. / I don't have time. |
| У меня нет билета. | I don't have a ticket. |
| У неё нет вопросов. | She doesn't have any questions. |
| Нет воды. | There is no water. |

## Concise explanation
Form: `нет` is a short existential negator used with nouns in the genitive case (singular or plural as required), and `у + [person] + нет + [noun in genitive]` expresses lack or absence for that person. Meaning: use `нет` when you want to say that something does not exist, is not present, or someone does not possess something. Limitation/common confusion: not every negative sentence uses the genitive—verbs and other constructions often use не + verb (e.g., `я не знаю` — "I do not know"). Do not assume genitive-after-negation applies to all verbs or cases; it is primarily for existential/possession negation.

## Worked dialogue
А: У тебя есть билет на концерт?
*Do you have a ticket for the concert?*

Б: Нет, у меня нет билета.
*No, I don't have a ticket.*

А: Почему нет? Ты купил билеты онлайн?
*Why not? Did you buy the tickets online?*

Б: Нет времени покупать сейчас.
*No time to buy them now.*

А: Может, мы найдем билеты у входа?
*Maybe we'll find tickets at the entrance?*

Б: Вряд ли — обычно нет свободных мест.
*Hardly — usually there are no free seats.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "I don't have money." (Use `у меня нет ...`.)
2. Change "У меня нет билета." to ask a question: "Do I have a ticket?" (Use `у меня` and inversion/intonation.)
3. Complete the sentence: "Нет ____." Choose the correct genitive form for "time" (время).
4. Make a short Russian answer to "Есть вода?" meaning "No, there is no water."
5. Correct or explain this attempt: "Я нет денег." — Identify the error and provide the correct sentence.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about preparing for a weekend trip. Include:
- At least two sentences using `у меня нет ...` with different nouns (e.g., билет, паспорта, денег).
- At least one sentence using `нет` as an existential negator (e.g., `нет времени`, `нет свободных мест`).
- One sentence that uses a non-genitive negation with `не` + verb (e.g., `я не успел купить ...`) to show contrast.
Focus on clear, simple vocabulary; each sentence should be one line.
