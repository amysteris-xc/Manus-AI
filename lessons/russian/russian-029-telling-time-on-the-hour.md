## Core pattern

To say what hour it is now, combine **сейчас** “now” with a number and the appropriate hour noun form. Use **сейчас один час**, **два / три / четыре часа**, and **пять / шесть часов** as fixed time phrases.

| Phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Сколько сейчас времени? | What time is it now? |
| Сейчас два часа. | It is two o’clock now. |
| Сейчас пять часов. | It is five o’clock now. |
| Сейчас один час. | It is one o’clock now. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Сколько сейчас времени?
> *What time is it now?*
>
> **Б:** Сейчас три часа.
> *It is three o’clock now.*
>
> **А:** Ты работаешь сейчас?
> *Are you working now?*
>
> **Б:** Да, сейчас я работаю.
> *Yes, now I am working.*
