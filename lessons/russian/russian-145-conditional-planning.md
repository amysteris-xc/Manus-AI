## Core pattern
If you want to express conditional planning, the basic patterns are:
- Если + present (condition) → future / imperative / present (result) for likely conditions.
- Если + past (hypothetical) → conditional with бы (requires past form + бы) for unreal or unlikely situations. (We focus here on likely conditions using present + future.)

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Если завтра будет дождь, я буду учиться дома. | If it rains tomorrow, I will study at home. |
| Если экзамен перенесут, мы поедем на выходные. | If they postpone the exam, we will go away for the weekend. |
| Если к шести не закончу, позвоню тебе. | If I don't finish by six, I'll call you. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Start with если (if), then a condition clause (often present tense for real/likely conditions), followed by a result clause that can use future tense (буду + infinitive or perfective future). Meaning: these sentences describe plans that change depending on a stated situation. Limitation/common confusion: using the past + бы makes an unreal or counterfactual condition (unlikely now) and is not interchangeable with present → future patterns. For beginner–intermediate planning, prefer если + present → буду/поеду/сделаю for clarity.

## Worked dialogue
А: Если завтра будет тепло, пойдем в парк.
*If it is warm tomorrow, we will go to the park.*

Б: А если пойдёт дождь, что сделаем?
*And if it rains, what will we do?*

А: Я возьму зонтик, но лучше останемся в кафе рядом.
*I'll take an umbrella, but better we'll stay in the café nearby.*

Б: Хорошо, тогда я возьму книгу и буду учиться там.
*Good, then I'll take a book and study there.*

## Guided practice
1. Convert this plan into a conditional sentence: "I will study at the library if the trains run on time." Use если + present → future.
2. Make a travel plan: "If the weather is good, we will go to the beach; if it is cloudy, we will visit a museum." Write two sentences connected by a comma.
3. Create a study contingency: "If I finish the homework before eight, I will call my friend." Use negative timing: до восьми / к восьми.
4. Rewrite this with a perfective future in the result: "If they announce the schedule, I will sign up." Use the appropriate perfective verb.
5. Change a likely condition into an unlikely (counterfactual) one: "If I had more free time, I would travel." Use past + бы.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about planning a weekend trip with a friend, using at least three different conditional sentences with если. Use concrete details (dates, transport, weather, places). Include at least one sentence with a negative condition (e.g., если не будет места) and at least one sentence using the perfective future for the result (e.g., поеду, куплю, позвоню).
