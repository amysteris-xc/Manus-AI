## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to ask whether an explanation is clear, respond politely if it is not, and suggest one specific way to make it clearer. You will also practice distinguishing clarity (understanding) from agreement (accepting content) and request an example or rephrasing.

## Core pattern
Concise teaching paragraph: To ask about clarity, use polite question frames such as "Это понятно?" or "Мне всё ясно?" and to suggest a clarification use "Может, вы могли бы..." or "Можно ещё раз объяснить...". Below are common concise phrases you can combine to ask about clarity and propose one concrete clarification.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Это понятно? | Is this clear? |
| Мне всё ясно? | Is everything clear to me? / Am I clear? |
| Не могли бы вы объяснить ещё раз? | Could you explain one more time, please? |
| Можно пример? | Could I have an example? |
| Может, вы могли бы уточнить пункт о... | Maybe you could clarify the point about... |
| Я понимаю идею, но не согласен с выводом. | I understand the idea, but I don't agree with the conclusion. |

## Concise explanation
Forms and meaning: "Это понятно?" and "Мне всё ясно?" are short, neutral ways to ask whether information was understood. Use "Не могли бы вы..." or "Можно..." to request a repetition, example, or specific clarification. Limitation/common confusion: asking "Я понимаю?" is unnatural — prefer "Это понятно?" or "Мне ясно?" Also, clarity (понятно/непонятно) concerns comprehension, not agreement; to express disagreement while maintaining clarity separate, say "Я понимаю, но..." followed by your objection.

## Worked dialogue
А: Это понятно?
*Is this clear?*

Б: В целом понятно, но можно пример про последний шаг?
*Generally clear, but could you give an example about the last step?*

А: Конечно. Не могли бы вы сказать, какой именно момент смущает?
*Of course. Could you say which specific point confuses you?*

Б: Меня смущает, как перейти от теории к практике. Можно показать короткий пример?
*I'm confused about how to move from theory to practice. Could you show a short example?*

А: Да: сначала делаем X, затем Y, и в конце Z — вот короткий пример. Это помогло?
*Yes: first do X, then Y, and finally Z — here's a short example. Did that help?*

Б: Теперь понятно, спасибо. Я понимаю идею, хотя с выводом не согласен.
*Now it's clear, thank you. I understand the idea, although I do not agree with the conclusion.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate and write the Russian sentence: "Is the last point clear?" Use "последний пункт".
2. Rewrite the phrase "Can you explain that again?" twice: once using "Не могли бы вы..." and once using "Можно...".
3. Formulate a polite request for an example focusing on "алгоритм" (algorithm).
4. Given this short explanation in Russian, mark it "понятно" or "непонятно" and write one-sentence reason in Russian why: "Сначала проверяем входные данные, затем применяем правило, потом возвращаем результат."
5. Role-play: write a 4-line mini-dialogue where you ask for clarification about "шаг 3" and suggest "можно показать код" as a clarification.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional exchange as a reviewer after reading a short technical explanation. Include:
- One direct question asking "Это понятно?" or similar.
- One request for a concrete clarification using "Не могли бы вы..." or "Можно...".
- One suggestion starting with "Может, вы могли бы..." that proposes exactly one way to clarify (for example: "показать пример", "нарисовать схему", or "дать шаги с номерами").
- One sentence that separates comprehension from agreement, such as "Я понимаю, но не согласен с..." or "Мне понятно, но я бы предложил...".
All sentences in Russian must be grammatically correct; provide precise English translations after each Russian sentence.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) ask "Это понятно?" naturally in conversation; (2) request a specific clarification using "Не могли бы вы..." or "Можно..." without hesitation; (3) propose exactly one measurable clarification like "показать пример" or "дать пошаговую инструкцию"; and (4) state politely whether you understand while separating comprehension from agreement (e.g., "Я понимаю, но..."). If you cannot reliably do all four, repeat the Guided practice and Independent production.
