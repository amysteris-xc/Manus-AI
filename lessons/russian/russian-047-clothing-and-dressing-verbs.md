## Core pattern

Clothing vocabulary includes **куртка** “jacket”, **пальто** “coat (indeclinable neuter)”, and **шапка** “hat / cap”. In cool weather, use **надеть** or describe a state: **он в тёплой куртке** “he is in a warm jacket”.

| Garment | Gender | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| тёплая куртка | f | warm jacket |
| чёрное пальто | n | black coat |
| синяя шапка | f | blue hat |
| шарф | m | scarf |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** На улице холодно?
> *Is it cold outside?*
>
> **Б:** Да, холодно. Надень куртку.
> *Yes, it is cold. Put on a jacket.*
>
> **А:** У тебя есть шапка?
> *Do you have a hat?*
>
> **Б:** Да, у меня есть тёплая шапка и шарф.
> *Yes, I have a warm hat and a scarf.*
