## What you will be able to do
You will be able to propose a meeting time and place, ask for alternatives, offer a compromise, and confirm the final plan in a short conversational exchange.

## Retrieval practice — four prompts
1. Recall Russian 096–101: How do you say "What time?" in Russian? Write the Russian phrase and translate it.
2. Recall Russian 096–101: How do you ask "Are you free on Saturday?" in Russian? Write the Russian question and translate it.
3. Recall Russian 096–101: How do you say "Let's meet at..." in Russian? Provide the phrase and translation.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: What is a byte boundary (8 bits) and why is aligning to it important when parsing binary data? Provide a one-sentence answer.

## Core pattern
Small, practical patterns for negotiating: suggest — ask for alternative — offer compromise — confirm.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Давай встретимся в субботу в три часа. | Let's meet on Saturday at 3 o'clock. |
| А можно в два? | Could it be at two? / Is two okay? |
| Я могу в два, но только если мы встретимся у кафе, а не у кинотеатра. | I can do two, but only if we meet at the café, not at the cinema. |
| Договорились: суббота в два у кафе. | Agreed: Saturday at two at the café. |

## Concise explanation
Use Давай/Давайте + infinitive to propose an action (Давай встретимся). To suggest a time, use a time expression with в + accusative (в три часа). To ask for an alternative, use А можно + time or А можно + infinitive (А можно в два?). To offer a compromise, connect clauses with но (but) and specify the condition (но только если...). A common confusion: in informal speech, people often drop particles or use colloquial word order; stick to clear phrasing when practicing. Note limitation: time expressions with "на" versus "в" depend on fixed phrases (e.g., на выходных vs в понедельник) — this lesson only uses в + time-of-day.

## Worked dialogue
А: Давай встретимся в субботу в три часа.
*Let's meet on Saturday at 3 o'clock.*

Б: А можно в два? У меня в три уже встреча.
*Could it be at two? I already have a meeting at three.*

А: Я могу в два, но только если мы встретимся у кафе, а не у кинотеатра.
*I can do two, but only if we meet at the café, not at the cinema.*

Б: Хорошо, договорились: суббота в два у кафе.
*Okay, agreed: Saturday at two at the café.*

## Guided practice — five tasks
1. Rewrite the proposal phrase replacing "суббота" with "понедельник" and translate both sentences.
2. Change "в два" to "в полдень" (at noon) in the dialogue and say how the sentence form changes, then translate.
3. Create two alternative compromise sentences using но только если and или (or). Translate both.
4. Role-play: write a short 4-line exchange where you suggest a time and the partner asks for an alternative; include final confirmation.
5. Identify in the worked dialogue the verb forms and underline (mentally) the infinitives used for proposing (e.g., встретиться). Write a one-sentence explanation of why infinitive is used after давай.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional dialogue (can be direct reported lines) in which two people negotiate:
- an initial proposal with time and place,
- a request for an alternative time,
- an offered compromise that changes either time or place,
- a short reason for the constraint (one sentence),
- and a final clear confirmation.
Every Russian sentence must be in Cyrillic and followed immediately by an English translation. Aim to use Давай/Давайте, А можно, но только если, and Договорились (or equivalent) at least once.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) propose a meeting with Давай/Давайте + infinitive, (2) ask for an alternative with А можно + time or infinitive, (3) offer a compromise using но только если or или, and (4) confirm the plan clearly with Договорились or Хорошо. If you hesitated translating time phrases or mixing в + time vs на + day, review the Concise explanation and redo the Guided practice.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for Retrieval practice prompt 1: "What time?" — "Во сколько?" — "At what time?"
2. Model answer for Retrieval practice prompt 2: "Are you free on Saturday?" — "Ты свободен в субботу?" / "Вы свободны в субботу?" — "Are you free on Saturday?" (use Ты for informal, Вы for formal).
3. Model answer for Retrieval practice prompt 3: "Let's meet at..." — "Давай встретимся в..." — "Let's meet at..."
4. Model answer for the byte-boundary prompt: "A byte boundary is a group of 8 bits; aligning to it avoids misreading multi-byte fields when parsing binary data." (one-sentence criterion)
