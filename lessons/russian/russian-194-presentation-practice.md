## What you will be able to do
You will be able to deliver a brief, clear fictional presentation in Russian using a simple frame: opening, two main points, closing, and an invitation for a question. The language is suitable for classroom or practice settings and avoids advanced rhetorical devices.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall a way to say "Hello" from Russian 096–101: "Здравствуйте." — What is the tone and register?
2. Recall how to introduce yourself from Russian 096–101: "Меня зовут Анна." — Practice adding a short occupation: "Я студент(ка)."
3. Recall a simple connective from Russian 096–101: "и" or "но" — use it to link two short ideas.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: "aligned to a byte boundary" — in plain terms, think "multiple of 8 bits" for packaging validation.

## Core pattern
A short signposting frame: opening phrase + "Первое" + "Второе" + closing phrase + "Есть ли вопросы?" Use this structure to keep the talk organized.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Здравствуйте, меня зовут Олег. | Hello, my name is Oleg. |
| Во-первых, у нас есть план. | First, we have a plan. |
| Во-вторых, план включает сроки. | Second, the plan includes deadlines. |
| На этом всё. Есть ли у вас вопрос? | That's all. Do you have a question? |

## Concise explanation
The frame uses simple ordinal signposts "Во-первых" and "Во-вторых" to introduce two main points; each is followed by a short statement. The closing "На этом всё" signals the end, and "Есть ли у вас вопрос?" invites one question. Limitation: for formal speeches, Russian offers more elaborate transitions and politeness markers — this lesson focuses on a compact, practiceable frame rather than full formal register.

## Worked dialogue
А: Здравствуйте, меня зовут Мария.
*Hello, my name is Maria.*

Б: Здравствуйте, Мария. Чем вы хотите поделиться?
*Hello, Maria. What would you like to share?*

А: Во-первых, наш проект улучшает интерфейс.
*First, our project improves the interface.*

Б: Во-вторых, какие сроки выполнения?
*Second, what are the deadlines?*

А: Сроки — три месяца, и команда готова начать. На этом всё. Есть ли у вас вопрос?
*The deadline is three months, and the team is ready to start. That's all. Do you have a question?*

## Guided practice
1. Write an opening sentence: "Здравствуйте, меня зовут ..." and add a fictional role.
2. Create two short points beginning with "Во-первых" and "Во-вторых" (one sentence each).
3. Practice a closing sentence: "На этом всё." Then add "Есть ли у вас вопрос?"
4. Replace "есть ли у вас вопрос?" with the informal "У вас есть вопрос?" and note the register difference.
5. Say the full 6–8 sentence presentation aloud and record (or time) it to last about 30–60 seconds.

## Independent production exercise
Prepare a short fictional presentation (6–10 sentences) about a simple project. Include:
- Greeting and name,
- One-sentence context (what the project is),
- "Во-первых" + one benefit,
- "Во-вторых" + one timeline or requirement,
- A brief sentence about resources or team,
- Closing "На этом всё" and an invitation for one question.

Example constraints: keep sentences simple, use present or future tense, avoid long subordinate clauses.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) deliver the full three-part frame smoothly, (2) use "Во-первых" and "Во-вторых" correctly, (3) close with "На этом всё" and invite one question, and (4) answer a simple follow-up question about time or purpose. If you cannot do these, repeat the guided practice and spaced items.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model opening: "Здравствуйте, меня зовут Саша. Я представляю небольшой проект." — This introduces speaker and context.
2. Model first point: "Во-первых, проект улучшит взаимодействие пользователей." — A clear benefit.
3. Model second point: "Во-вторых, реализация займёт два месяца." — A concise timeline.
4. Model closing and invitation: "На этом всё. Есть ли у вас вопрос?" — Signals end and invites one question.
