- Give concrete evidence of progress using past or present perfect-like constructions.
- Propose one specific next action with a modest verb form.
- Keep statements realistic and reflective rather than claiming full mastery.

## Core pattern
Teaching paragraph:
To reflect on learning, combine three elements: a modest qualifier (пока / уже / ещё), a verb for goal/action, and a short evidence clause. Use simple past or present forms and a next-action infinitive or short phrase.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Моя цель — пока улучшить разговорную речь. | My goal is, for now, to improve spoken language. |
| Я уже читаю статьи по теме и ещё делаю упражнения. | I already read articles on the topic and still do exercises. |
| В качестве следующего шага я собираюсь повторять новые слова каждый день. | As the next step I plan to review new words every day. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use a qualifier (пока, уже, ещё) before or after the main clause to show degree or timing. Use simple verbs (учить, читать, писать, повторять, практиковать) and an infinitive to state next actions (например: "собиpaюсь читать", "буду повторять"). Meaning: these constructions clearly communicate a goal, progress, and a next step without claiming full mastery. Limitation/common confusion: Russian does not have a direct present perfect tense like English; use context with уже/ещё or past tense to show completed actions, and be careful—using уже often implies completion, while пока and ещё imply incompleteness.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я хочу рассказать о своей учебной цели.
*I want to tell about my learning goal.*

Б: Какая у тебя цель сейчас?
*What is your goal right now?*

А: Моя цель — пока говорить свободнее по-темам ежедневной жизни.
*My goal is, for now, to speak more freely about everyday topics.*

Б: У меня есть доказательство прогресса: я уже могу поддержать короткий разговор и ещё учу новые слова.
*I have evidence of progress: I can already hold a short conversation and I am still learning new words.*

А: А какой у тебя следующий шаг?
*And what is your next step?*

Б: Я буду слушать подкаст на десять минут каждый день и записывать пять новых предложений.
*I will listen to a ten-minute podcast every day and write down five new sentences.*

## Guided practice
1. Write one sentence stating a current learning goal using пока, ещё, or уже. Provide an English translation.
2. Write one sentence giving evidence of progress (use past tense or уже). Provide translation.
3. Write one sentence stating one concrete next action with an infinitive (e.g., "чтобы...," "собиpaюсь...," "буду..."). Provide translation.
4. Combine the three ideas into a short paragraph (3–4 sentences) using honest qualifiers.
5. Read your paragraph aloud and time yourself; note whether you hesitated on any verb forms and rewrite one sentence to simplify it.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a learner named Ольга who studies Russian. Include:
- One sentence stating Ольга's current goal with пока/ещё/уже.
- One sentence with evidence of progress.
- One sentence admitting a remaining difficulty (use ещё or пока).
- One sentence stating a clear next action (infinitive or short future).
- Two sentences of simple background (how long she studies, what she practices).
- One sentence concluding with a modest tone (avoid claiming mastery).
Provide English translations under each Russian sentence.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) state a clear learning goal using a qualifier (пока/ещё/уже), (2) provide at least one concrete piece of evidence of progress in Russian, and (3) propose a specific next action in an infinitive or short future. ## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model goal sentence: "Моя цель — пока улучшить разговорную речь." — shows modesty with пока.
2. Model evidence sentence: "Я уже могу поддержать короткий разговор на бытовые темы." — uses уже to indicate progress.
3. Model next-action sentence: "Я буду повторять новые слова каждый день по десять минут." — specific frequency and duration.
4. Example short paragraph (6 sentences):
   - "Ольга изучает русский два года." — "Olga has been studying Russian for two years."
   - "Моя цель — пока говорить свободнее." — "My goal is, for now, to speak more freely."
   - "Она уже может читать простые статьи и ещё учится понимать речь на слух." — "She can already read simple articles and is still learning to understand spoken language."
   - "Её следующий шаг — слушать подкасты по десять минут и выписывать новые слова." — "Her next step is to listen to podcasts for ten minutes and write down new words."
   - "Она практикует разговор с партнёром раз в неделю." — "She practices speaking with a partner once a week."
   - "Пока она не чувствует себя мастером, но видит прогресс." — "She does not feel like a master yet, but she sees progress."
