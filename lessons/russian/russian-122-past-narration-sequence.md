## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to narrate a short sequence of past actions (3–4 steps) using the past tense with correct gender/number agreement and the connectors сначала, потом, and наконец.

## Retrieval practice — four numbered prompts
1. Recall the past-tense endings for the masculine singular of regular verbs in Russian 096–101. Write one example with the verb читать.
2. Recall how to form the feminine past tense for verbs learned in Russian 096–101. Give an example with the verb посмотреть.
3. Recall the plural past-tense form for a verb from Russian 096–101. Give an example with the verb приготовить.
4. Recall one concept about byte-boundaries or validation used in C-style APIs: what is a common check to ensure a buffer index is valid? (Answer briefly in English.)

## Core pattern
Use past-tense verb forms (agreeing with subject gender/number) and connect actions with sequence words: сначала (first), потом (then), наконец (finally).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Сначала я встал, потом я позавтракал, наконец я ушёл. | First I got up, then I had breakfast, finally I left. |
| Сначала она открыла книгу, потом она прочитала главу, наконец она выключила свет. | First she opened the book, then she read the chapter, finally she turned off the light. |
| Сначала мы купили билеты, потом мы сели в поезд, наконец мы приехали в город. | First we bought tickets, then we got on the train, finally we arrived in the city. |

## Concise explanation
To narrate past events, use the past tense form of the verb that agrees with the subject: masculine singular typically ends in -л (e.g., сделал), feminine singular in -ла (e.g., сделала), neuter in -ло (e.g., сделало), and plural in -ли (e.g., сделали). Place sequence words (сначала, потом, наконец) before the clause or action they relate to; they do not change form. Limitation: this pattern is for simple past narration of completed actions — for ongoing background actions or simultaneous events you will need imperfective verbs and different constructions, which can change aspect and nuance.

## Worked dialogue
А: Сначала я пришёл в кафе, потом я заказал кофе.
*First I came to the café, then I ordered coffee.*

Б: А что ты делал после?
*And what did you do after that?*

А: Потом я прочитал новости, наконец я написал сообщение другу.
*Then I read the news, finally I wrote a message to a friend.*

Б: Хорошо — всё прошло спокойно?
*Good — did everything go smoothly?*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Transform the sentence "Она читала письмо" into a three-step past narration using сначала, потом, наконец (use different verbs each step).
2. Write three sentences describing what you did yesterday morning using the past tense and sequence words (one sentence per step).
3. Change this masculine narration to feminine: "Сначала он открыл дверь, потом он вышел, наконец он закрыл её." Provide the full feminine version.
4. Convert the following singular narration to plural: "Сначала я приготовил ужин, потом я помыл посуду." Provide the plural version meaning "we."
5. Identify and correct agreement errors: "Сначала они пришёл, потом она сели, наконец он ушла." Rewrite correctly.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph of 6–10 sentences describing a simple past event (e.g., a morning routine, a short trip, or a small party). Use сначала, потом, and наконец at least once each. Ensure all past verbs agree with the intended subjects in gender and number. Keep vocabulary simple and focus on clear sequencing.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) produce a 3–4 step past narration using сначала, потом, наконец without hesitation; (2) form past-tense endings that correctly match masculine, feminine, neuter, and plural subjects in your sentences; (3) convert short narrations between singular/plural and masculine/feminine with correct agreement. If you struggle with any of these, repeat the Guided practice and Spaced-repetition tasks.
