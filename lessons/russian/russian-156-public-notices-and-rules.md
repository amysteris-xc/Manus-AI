## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to read and write short public notices and simple rules in Russian that state access, opening hours, prohibitions, and basic instructions (e.g., «Вход запрещён», «Работает с 9:00 до 18:00»). You will also learn typical vocabulary and sentence patterns used on signs and classroom rules. Note: for any legal or official interpretation, consult an authoritative local source.

## Core pattern
Public notices often use short, nominal sentences or imperatives. They frequently omit subject pronouns and use infinitives, short verbs, or participles. Below are core patterns for access, timing, and rules.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Вход запрещён. | Entry is forbidden. |
| Работает с 9:00 до 18:00. | Open/operates from 9:00 to 18:00. |
| Просьба не шуметь. | Please do not make noise. |
| Вход по пропускам. | Entry by passes only. |
| Не курить. | No smoking. |

## Concise explanation
Short notices often use the passive short participle (e.g., запрещён) or an infinitive/imperative form (e.g., не курить, просим не ставить). Times are given with с ... до ... to indicate a continuous interval. For access control, phrases like «по пропускам» or «для сотрудников» indicate who may enter. Limitation: some nominal signs (e.g., «Осторожно» or «Внимание») lack verbs entirely and rely on context—don't assume every phrase gives a full sentence structure.

## Worked dialogue
А: Где вход?
*Where is the entrance?*

Б: Вход справа, рядом с сервисом.
*The entrance is on the right, next to the service area.*

А: Работает ли касса сейчас?
*Is the ticket office open now?*

Б: Касса работает с 10:00 до 17:00.
*The ticket office is open from 10:00 to 17:00.*

А: Можно фотографировать внутри?
*Can one take photos inside?*

Б: Нет, внутри фотографировать нельзя — знак говорит "Фотосъёмка запрещена".
*No, photographing inside is not allowed — the sign says "Photography prohibited".*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "Opening hours: Monday–Friday 08:30–17:00."
2. Write a short Russian notice for a classroom: "Please silence your phones."
3. Convert the phrase "No entry without helmet" into Russian appropriate for a construction site sign.
4. Change the sentence "Вход запрещён" into a polite request using "просим": make it read like "Please do not enter."
5. Compose a two-line sign indicating restricted access for staff only and the hours when access is allowed.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional notice (6–10 sentences) for a small community library in Russian. Include:
- Opening days and hours with с ... до ...;
- A rule about food and drink;
- A rule about noise level;
- A short instruction for returns (e.g., "Возврат книг в специальный ящик");
- One sentence indicating who can use the reading room (e.g., "Для посетителей библиотеки").
All sentences must be in Cyrillic. After finishing, provide an English translation for each sentence.

You are ready to move on if you can (1) accurately form sentences stating opening hours with с ... до ...; (2) write at least three short prohibitions or requests as you'd see on notices (e.g., «Не курить», «Вход по пропускам», «Просьба не шуметь»); and (3) indicate restricted access using short noun phrases (e.g., «Для сотрудников»). ## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Example translation for "Opening hours: Monday–Friday 08:30–17:00.": «Часы работы: Понедельник—Пятница с 08:30 до 17:00.» — Acceptable variants: «Пн—Пт» for abbreviations.
2. Example for "Please silence your phones.": «Просьба отключить звук на мобильных телефонах.» — Also correct: «Просьба включить беззвучный режим.»
3. Example for "No entry without helmet": «Вход без каски запрещён.» or «Вход без защитной каски воспрещён.» — Both are correct; the first is more common on signs.
4. Model independent production (short library notice, 6 sentences):
   1) «Библиотека работает со вторника по субботу с 10:00 до 18:00.»
   2) «Вход для читателей бесплатный.»
   3) «Просят не приносить еду и напитки в читальный зал.»
   4) «Просьба соблюдать тишину.»
   5) «Возврат книг осуществляется в специальный ящик у выхода.»
   6) «Читальный зал для посетителей библиотеки.»
   These sentences show typical patterns: hours with с ... до ..., prohibitions with просим/просят/не, and return instruction with осуществляется/в.
