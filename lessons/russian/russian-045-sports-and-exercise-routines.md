## Core pattern

To say you play a ball game, use **играть в** plus the accusative game noun: **играть в футбол** “to play football / soccer”. For general exercise, use **заниматься спортом**.

| Activity | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| заниматься спортом | to exercise / engage in sport |
| играть в футбол | to play football |
| бегать утром | to run in the morning |
| Я люблю спорт. | I like sport. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Ты занимаешься спортом?
> *Do you exercise?*
>
> **Б:** Да, по утрам я бегаю в парке.
> *Yes, in the mornings I run in the park.*
>
> **А:** А твой брат играет в футбол?
> *And does your brother play football?*
>
> **Б:** Да, он часто играет в футбол.
> *Yes, he often plays football.*
