## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to: briefly describe a minor problem, politely ask someone for help or clarification, and propose a clear, practical solution or next step in neutral, everyday contexts (e.g., missing page in a handout, a delayed meeting, or a calendar confusion).

2. Recall the accusative singular of a masculine inanimate noun from Russian 096–101 and create a short sentence.
3. Recall a polite request pattern (e.g., "Можно...?" or "Не могли бы вы...?") from Russian 096–101 and make a short question.
4. Recall a basic byte-boundary or validation concept from C: explain in one sentence why validating input length (byte-boundary) matters when receiving text from a network or file.

## Core pattern
We focus on three useful patterns for describing a minor problem, asking for help, and suggesting a solution.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| У меня проблема: в раздатке не хватает страницы. | I have a problem: the handout is missing a page. |
| Не могли бы вы прислать мне недостающую страницу? | Could you send me the missing page? (polite) |
| Давайте назначим новый час встречи или переместим пункт в повестке. | Let's schedule a new meeting time or move the item on the agenda. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Start by stating the problem with "У меня проблема" or "Проблема в том, что..." then give the specific issue in the nominative/accusative as appropriate (e.g., "не хватает страницы" — "a page is missing" uses "страницы" in the genitive after "не хватает"). For requests, use polite formulas: "Можно...?" for neutral requests or "Не могли бы вы...?" for more formal politeness. To propose a solution, use "Давайте..." for a collaborative suggestion or "Я могу..." to offer to take action. Limitation/common confusion: Russian uses cases to show relationships — verbs like "не хватает" take the genitive ("не хватает времени", "не хватает страницы"), which is different from English structure; learners must remember that "не хватает" commonly governs genitive, not accusative.

## Worked dialogue
А: У меня проблема: в документе отсутствует страница три.
*I have a problem: page three is missing in the document.*

Б: Можно увидеть файл, чтобы я проверил?
*May I see the file so I can check?*

А: Да, вот файл. Не могли бы вы прислать недостающую страницу или сказать, что у вас есть?
*Yes, here is the file. Could you send the missing page or tell me what you have?*

Б: Я могу отсканировать страницу и выслать её по почте через час. Подходит?
*I can scan the page and send it by email in an hour. Does that work?*

А: Подойдёт, спасибо. Если не получится, давайте перенесём обсуждение этого пункта на следующую встречу.
*That works, thank you. If it doesn't work out, let's move this item to the next meeting.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the sentence "The meeting is delayed" in Russian using a neutral phrase for "delayed" (use "перенесена" or "задерживается" depending on context).
2. Transform "Не хватает страницы" into a full sentence explaining which page is missing and from what document.
3. Compose a polite request asking a colleague to resend an attachment: use "Не могли бы вы..." or "Можно..." and include the word "вложение".
4. Propose two possible solutions in Russian to a confusing schedule: one using "Давайте..." and one using "Я могу...".
5. Change a direct imperative "Отправьте страницу" to a more polite form suitable for email.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional message (6–10 sentences) in Russian to a colleague about a small problem with a shared document: describe the problem, ask for help politely, propose at least one practical solution, and offer your own assistance. Each sentence should be simple (one clause) and accurate; include dates or times if relevant. Provide an English translation sentence-by-sentence under each Russian sentence (use italicized text for the translation).

Example structure to follow (do not copy verbatim): state the problem, request a resend or clarification, suggest a time to fix it, offer to send a corrected file.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: 1) state a small problem in one or two sentences using correct case forms (e.g., "не хватает страницы", "встреча перенесена"), 2) make a polite request ("Не могли бы вы...?" or "Можно...?" ) and 3) propose a clear next step with "Давайте..." or "Я могу...". If you still hesitate on genitive after "не хватает" or choice between "перенесена" and "задерживается", review the concise explanation and redo the guided practice.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for guided practice 1: "Встреча перенесена на час позже." / "The meeting is moved one hour later." (Alternative: "Встреча задерживается на час." — note subtle difference: "перенесена" implies rescheduling, "задерживается" implies running late.)
2. Model answer for guided practice 2: "В документе 'Инструкции' не хватает страницы три." / "Page three is missing from the 'Instructions' document."
3. Model answer for guided practice 3: "Не могли бы вы прислать вложение ещё раз?" / "Could you please resend the attachment?"
4. Model answer for guided practice 4: "Давайте перенесём встречу на пятницу." / "Let's move the meeting to Friday." and "Я могу составить новый график и разослать его." / "I can make a new schedule and send it out."
