## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on three short, polite patterns you can use during listening: asking to slow down, asking to repeat, and asking for the main point.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Можете говорить помедленнее, пожалуйста? | Can you speak more slowly, please? |
| Не могли бы вы повторить ещё раз? | Could you repeat that once more? |
| В чём основная мысль? | What is the main point? |

## Concise explanation
Forms: Use modal-request constructions with "можете" / "не могли бы вы" + infinitive for polite requests. For asking about speed, use comparative adverbs like "помедленнее". For repetition, "повторить" + "ещё раз" is direct and common. For main point, "основная мысль" is a standard phrase. Limitation: English modal nuance doesn't map perfectly to every Russian context; "не могли бы вы" is more formal and polite than "можете", and in informal situations you can use "можешь" or "повтори, пожалуйста".

## Worked dialogue
А: Можете говорить помедленнее, пожалуйста?
*Can you speak more slowly, please?*

Б: Конечно, я буду говорить чуть медленнее.
*Of course, I will speak a bit more slowly.*

А: Не могли бы вы повторить последний пример?
*Could you repeat the last example?*

Б: Да, повторю ещё раз и объясню проще.
*Yes, I will repeat it once more and explain more simply.*

А: В чём основная мысль этого абзаца?
*What is the main point of this paragraph?*

Б: Главная мысль — что практика важнее теории вначале.
*The main point is that practice is more important than theory at the beginning.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Say aloud: "Можете говорить помедленнее, пожалуйста?" three times, focusing on pronunciation of "помедленнее".
2. Transform the polite plural "Можете говорить помедленнее, пожалуйста?" into informal singular: write and say "Можешь говорить помедленнее, пожалуйста?"
3. Listen to a short Russian paragraph (choose any text you have) and practice saying: "Не могли бы вы повторить последний фрагмент?" before each repetition.
4. After listening to a paragraph, ask aloud: "В чём основная мысль?" and then paraphrase the answer in simple Russian (2–3 short sentences).
5. Role-play with a partner or record yourself: alternate between asking to slow down, asking to repeat, and asking for the main point; each turn should use a different polite form ("можете", "не могли бы вы", "пожалуйста").

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences describing a fictional listening session at a university lecture. Include at least:
- one sentence asking the lecturer to slow down (use "пожалуйста"),
- one sentence asking to repeat a word,
- one sentence asking what the main point of the lecture is,
- one sentence using informal form (e.g., "можешь") as if speaking to a classmate.
Keep all sentences in Russian; after each sentence, provide an English translation on the next line. Aim for clear, simple grammar and vocabulary suitable for a learner who has completed Russian 001–101.
