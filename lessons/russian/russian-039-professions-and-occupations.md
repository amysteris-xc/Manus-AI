## Core pattern

In present-tense statements about profession, Russian does not use a linking verb: **он инженер** “he is an engineer”, **она учительница** “she is a teacher”. Professions provide realistic descriptive vocabulary.

| Word | Gender | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| инженер | m | engineer |
| учитель / учительница | m / f | teacher |
| студент / студентка | m / f | student |
| врач | m (common) | doctor |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Кто твой друг по профессии?
> *What is your friend by profession?*
>
> **Б:** Мой друг — инженер.
> *My friend is an engineer.*
>
> **А:** А его сестра?
> *And his sister?*
>
> **Б:** Она учительница.
> *She is a teacher.*
