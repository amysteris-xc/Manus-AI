## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to carry out a short, polite service-recovery exchange in Russian: apologize, clarify the facts, offer a concrete option (refund, replacement, discount, or expedited fix), and confirm the customer's preference. The dialogue will be brief and realistic for customer-service contexts.

2. Recall Russian 096–101: give the phrase for asking "What happened?" politely (e.g., "Что случилось?") and a more formal alternative.
3. Recall Russian 096–101: write the standard phrase for offering help ("Чем я могу помочь?") and a formal variant.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: what is a common validation check when accepting file uploads — "проверка размера файла" (file size check) — and why it's necessary.

## Core pattern
Use this concise pattern to structure the exchange: Apology + Clarification question + Offer + Confirmation.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Извините за неудобство, мы разберёмся. | Sorry for the inconvenience, we will look into it. |
| Можете, пожалуйста, уточнить, что именно произошло? | Could you please clarify exactly what happened? |
| Мы можем вернуть деньги, заменить товар или предложить скидку. Что вам удобнее? | We can refund the money, replace the item, or offer a discount. Which do you prefer? |
| Хорошо, я оформляю возврат. Подтвердите, пожалуйста, ваши данные. | Okay, I'm processing the refund. Please confirm your details. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use a short apology phrase (Извините / Простите), then a polite clarifying question with "можете... уточнить" or "что именно произошло?" to gather facts. Offer concrete options with verbs like "можем вернуть", "заменить", "предложить" and finish by asking for confirmation with "Что вам удобнее?" or "Подтвердите, пожалуйста." Meaning: this sequence keeps the conversation constructive: acknowledge, understand, offer, confirm. Limitation/common confusion: "извините" and "простите" are both apologies, but "простите" can sound more formal or solicit forgiveness; "извините" is more commonly used in service contexts. Do not use overly colloquial shortcuts in service dialogues.

## Worked dialogue
А: Извините за неудобство, что именно произошло с вашим заказом?
*Sorry for the inconvenience, what exactly happened with your order?*

Б: Товар пришёл повреждённым — упаковка была порвана, и одна деталь сломана.
*The item arrived damaged — the package was torn and one part is broken.*

А: Понимаю. Могу уточнить номер заказа и дату доставки?
*I understand. May I confirm the order number and delivery date?*

Б: Номер заказа 4572, дата доставки 12 сентября.
*Order number 4572, delivery date September 12.*

А: Спасибо. Мы можем выслать замену завтра или оформить возврат денег. Что вам удобнее?
*Thank you. We can send a replacement tomorrow or process a refund. Which is more convenient for you?*

Б: Пожалуй, я выберу замену, если она придёт на следующей неделе.
*I'll choose a replacement, if it arrives next week.*

А: Хорошо, я оформляю замену и пришлю подтверждение на вашу почту. Подтвердите, пожалуйста, адрес электронной почты.
*Okay, I'm arranging the replacement and will send confirmation to your email. Please confirm your email address.*

## Guided practice
1. Role-play as customer A and staff B using the core pattern; record or write both sides in Russian (4–6 lines).
2. Write three different offers to resolve a problem (refund, replacement, discount) in Russian; include precise verbs and request confirmation.
3. Convert the apology "Извините за неудобство" into a more formal version using "приносим извинения" and explain the nuance in one sentence.
4. Change the worked dialogue so the customer requests a discount instead of a replacement; keep the same number of turns.
5. Translate the sentence "We will need a photo of the damage to proceed" into Russian and use it in a two-line exchange.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional dialogue where you are the staff member at a café who needs to apologize for a wrong order, clarify what the customer received, offer two concrete options (refund, replacement, or a free item), and confirm the customer's selection. Use polite forms and include at least one sentence asking for the customer's name or order number. Each sentence must be in Russian and followed immediately by its precise English translation.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) apologize naturally with "Извините" or "Приносим извинения"; (2) ask one clarifying question about facts ("Что именно произошло?" or "Можете уточнить..."); (3) offer at least two concrete resolution options using correct verbs; and (4) confirm the customer's choice politely. If you can do all four in a short spoken or written exchange, you have achieved the lesson objective.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model apology phrase: "Извините за неудобство, мы разберёмся." — A brief, service-appropriate apology acknowledging the problem.
2. Model clarification question: "Можете, пожалуйста, уточнить, что именно произошло?" — Polite request for facts; requires instrumental/accusative accuracy depending on follow-up nouns.
3. Model offer options (three samples): "Мы можем вернуть деньги.", "Мы можем выслать замену.", "Мы можем предложить скидку 20%." — Each uses "можем" + infinitive verb to state options.
4. Model confirmation line: "Что вам удобнее: возврат, замена или скидка?" or "Подтвердите, пожалуйста, ваш выбор." — Clear request for the customer's preference.
