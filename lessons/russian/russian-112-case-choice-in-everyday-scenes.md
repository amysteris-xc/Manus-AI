## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will reliably choose between a few common Russian case patterns when describing: where someone is (location), who they are with (companion), who receives something (recipient), and what the conversation or action is about (topic). You will use familiar prepositions and verb patterns practiced in Russian 096–101 and avoid over-generalizing exceptions.

## Core pattern
This is a compact set of reliable models for four common pieces of information in short scenes: location (where), companion (with whom), recipient (to whom/for whom), and topic (about what). Use these templates as patterns to slot nouns and verbs.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Он в кафе с другом о работе. | He is in the café with (a) friend talking about work. |
| Я даю книгу сестре о музыке. | I give the book to (my) sister about music. |
| Мы стоим у музея с гидом. | We are standing by the museum with (a) guide. |
| Она пишет письмо маме о планах. | She writes a letter to (her) mother about plans. |

## Concise explanation
Form: location commonly uses в/на + prepositional for static location (Он в школе). Companion uses с + instrumental (с другом). Recipient uses dative (to/for): сестре, маме. Topic can be expressed with о/об + prepositional (о музыке) or with accusative when the verb requires a direct object (читать книгу — topic as object). Limitation/common confusion: some verbs require accusative for the "thing" (читать + книгу) while "about" uses о + prepositional — do not substitute them blindly.

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты где сейчас?
*Where are you now?*
Б: Я в парке с коллегой.
*I'm in the park with a colleague.*
А: Кому ты сейчас отправляешь сообщение?
*Who are you sending a message to now?*
Б: Я отправляю сообщение начальнику о встрече.
*I'm sending a message to the boss about the meeting.*

## Guided practice
1. Replace the nouns in "Он в кафе с другом о работе." with "библиотека / одноклассник / урок" keeping grammar correct.
2. Write a Russian sentence: "I give the key to my neighbor about the apartment" using appropriate cases and prepositions (choose whether "about" is needed as о + prepositional).
3. Convert "Она пишет письмо маме о планах." into past tense keeping cases correct.
4. Make a negative sentence: "We are not standing by the museum with a guide."
5. Change the recipient in "Я даю книгу сестре о музыке." to plural ("to the sisters") and adjust the sentence.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional scene (use Cyrillic). Include: one location with в/на + prepositional, one companion with с + instrumental, one recipient in dative, one sentence using о + prepositional to state a topic, and at least one direct object in accusative where required by the verb. Keep vocabulary simple and consistent (no invented words). Provide an English translation for the whole paragraph beneath it.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Replacement model for Guided practice 1: "Он в библиотеке с одноклассником о уроке." — Correct forms: библиотеке (prep.), одноклассником (instr.), о уроке (prep.).
2. Model for Guided practice 2: "Я даю ключ соседу (о квартире)." — Preferable: "Я даю ключ соседу." If you include "about the apartment," use "о квартире" for topic, but it's often unnecessary.
3. Past-tense conversion for Guided practice 3: "Она написала письмо маме о планах." — Past tense verb agrees with subject; cases unchanged.
4. Negative model for Guided practice 4: "Мы не стоим у музея с гидом." — Use у + genitive for "by/near" location.
5. Plural recipient for Guided practice 5: "Я даю книгу сёстрам о музыке." — сёстрам is dative plural.
