## Core pattern

Use **который** “which / who” to join a noun with information about it. Begin with nominative patterns such as **книга, которая лежит...** and **человек, который работает...**.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Это книга, которая лежит на столе. | This is the book that is on the table. |
| Он человек, который много читает. | He is a person who reads a lot. |
| Это город, который мне нравится. | This is a city that I like. |

## Concise explanation

Build each statement from a reliable chunk before adding detail. Preserve the case, verb form, or connector shown in the model rather than translating word-for-word from English. Russian word order is flexible, but this lesson uses a neutral order so that the grammar pattern remains visible.
