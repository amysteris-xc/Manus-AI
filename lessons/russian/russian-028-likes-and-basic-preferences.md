# Russian 028 — Likes, Dislikes, and Basic Preferences

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to express simple likes and dislikes with **мне нравится** and **мне не нравится**, using singular nouns or familiar infinitives.

## Core pattern

Use **мне нравится** for “I like” when the following item is a singular noun or an infinitive verb. For plural objects, Russian changes the verb form; keep today’s practice to singular nouns and infinitives.

| Phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Мне нравится чай. | I like tea. |
| Мне нравится читать. | I like reading / I like to read. |
| Мне не нравится этот парк. | I do not like this park. |
| Тебе нравится учиться? | Do you like studying? |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Тебе нравится чай?
> *Do you like tea?*
>
> **Б:** Да, мне нравится чай.
> *Yes, I like tea.*
>
> **А:** Тебе нравится этот парк?
> *Do you like this park?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, мне не нравится этот парк.
> *No, I do not like this park.*
