## Core pattern
The core pattern: main clause + чтобы + subordinate clause (purpose). Use present or infinitive forms in the subordinate clause as appropriate.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я учу русский, чтобы понимать фильмы. | I study Russian in order to understand movies. |
| Она звонит, чтобы спросить время. | She calls in order to ask the time. |
| Мы идём в парк, чтобы отдохнуть. | We are going to the park in order to rest. |

## Concise explanation
Чтобы introduces a subordinate clause expressing purpose and is followed by a verb in the clause (infinitive or conjugated). The basic structure is: Main clause + чтобы + clause (e.g., Я делаю X, чтобы Y). Limitation: нельзя заменять чтобы на "для того, чтобы" in very informal speech without slight change in emphasis — both are correct but "для того, чтобы" is more formal/explicit. Also, дословный перевод "so that" works often, but context decides tense and politeness.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я читаю книгу, чтобы учить новые слова.
*I am reading a book in order to learn new words.*

Б: Зачем ты читаешь эту статью?
*Why are you reading this article?*

А: Я читаю, чтобы подготовиться к тесту.
*I am reading in order to prepare for the test.*

Б: Хорошо. Я тоже буду читать, чтобы узнать больше.
*Good. I will also read in order to learn more.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate to Russian: "He writes notes in order to remember." Use чтобы.
2. Make a sentence: "We visit the library to study." Use мы and чтобы.
3. Change this statement into a purpose sentence: "Она звонит маме." → add why: "to ask how she is."
4. Create a negative purpose sentence: "They do not call so that they won't wake him." (Use чтобы and не.)
5. Replace the purpose clause with an infinitive where natural: "Я иду в магазин, чтобы купить хлеб." — identify the infinitive.

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences about a weekday routine using чтобы to state simple purposes for at least four actions. Use different subjects (я, ты, он/она, мы). Keep purposes practical and harmless (studying, asking, resting, meeting). Provide an English gloss after each Russian sentence or in a separate line immediately following each Russian sentence.

Example structure to follow:
- Russian sentence 1.
  English translation 1.
- Russian sentence 2.
  English translation 2.
... (continue until 6–10 sentences)
