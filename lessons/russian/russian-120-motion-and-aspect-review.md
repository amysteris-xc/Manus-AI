## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to produce a short three-part statement that contrasts (1) what someone is doing right now (imperfective, motion verb, possibly multi-directional), (2) what they usually do (habitual, imperfective), and (3) what they did yesterday (perfective, completed trip). You will also be able to explain why a different verb form is used in each part.

## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on a three-part pattern: Now (imperfective present progressive or motion-in-progress), Usually (habitual imperfective), Yesterday (perfective past — completed single trip).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я сейчас иду в парк. | I am now walking/going to the park. |
| Обычно я хожу в парк по воскресеньям. | I usually go to the park on Sundays. |
| Вчера я пошёл в парк и вернулся вечером. | Yesterday I went to the park and returned in the evening. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use imperfective motion verbs for actions in progress or habitual actions (e.g., идти/ходить, ехать/ездить). For a single completed trip you normally use the perfective pair (e.g., пойти/поехать) in the past tense. Meaning: "сейчас" + imperfective = ongoing action; "обычно" + imperfective = habitual; past perfective = completed single event. Limitation/common confusion: Some motion verbs are multi-directional and have both unidirectional and multidirectional imperfective stems (идти vs ходить); don't assume you can always swap forms — the choice can change meaning (single journey vs repeated/habitual action). Also note aspect pairs must be learned per verb; there is no universal rule that constructs the perfective from the imperfective in all cases.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я сейчас еду на работу.
*I am going to work now (by vehicle, in progress).*

Б: Ты обычно ездишь на работу на машине?
*Do you usually go to work by car?*

А: Да, обычно я езжу на машине, но вчера я поехал пешком.
*Yes, usually I go by car, but yesterday I went on foot (completed trip).*

Б: Понятно — сейчас ты в пути, обычно ты ездишь, а вчера это было одно завершённое действие.
*I see — now you are in transit, usually you go (habitually), and yesterday that was one completed action.*

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model explanation: "Иду" describes an action in progress now (imperfective, unidirectional in progress). "Пошёл" describes a single, completed start of a trip (perfective, past) — use "пошёл" to report that you went somewhere (and completed the action).
2. Model three-part conversion for "Она обычно ходит в библиотеку.":
   - Она сейчас идёт в библиотеку. — *She is going to the library now.*
   - Обычно она ходит в библиотеку по утрам. — *She usually goes to the library in the mornings.*
   - Вчера она пошла в библиотеку и вернулась днём. — *Yesterday she went to the library and returned in the afternoon.*
3. Model change to ехать/поехать (adapted table row):
   - Я сейчас еду в парк. — *I am now driving/going (by vehicle) to the park.*
   - Обычно я езжу в парк по воскресеньям. — *I usually go to the park on Sundays (by vehicle).*
   - Вчера я поехал в парк и вернулся вечером. — *Yesterday I went to the park (by vehicle) and returned in the evening.*
4. Answer criteria for the independent production: six to ten Russian sentences, each with an adjacent precise English translation; includes now (imperfective in-progress), usually (habitual imperfective), and yesterday (perfective past) with consistent, accurate verb aspect choice.
