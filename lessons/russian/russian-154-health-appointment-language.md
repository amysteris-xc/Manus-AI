## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Say a short, non-urgent reason for a medical or dental appointment in Russian.
- Ask when the earliest available appointment is.
- Provide simple scheduling details (preferred day/time and contact method).
- Understand basic responses from reception or a scheduler.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall the Russian verbs for "to be" (past and present) and form a simple past sentence from Russian 096–101. Write: "I was sick yesterday." in Russian.
2. Recall the accusative case for animate singular masculine from Russian 096–101 and form: "I see the doctor." in Russian.
3. Recall a polite question marker and verb inversion from Russian 096–101 by turning "You can help me" into a polite question: "Can you help me?" in Russian.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: What is a simple explanation of why checking a message length against a maximum byte-size is necessary? (One sentence in English.)

## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on phrases to request an appointment and briefly describe non-urgent symptoms or concerns.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Здравствуйте, я хотел(а) бы записаться на приём. | Hello, I would like to make an appointment. |
| У меня есть небольшая боль в горле уже три дня. | I have a slight sore throat for three days. |
| Когда у вас есть свободное время на следующей неделе? | When do you have availability next week? |
| Я предпочитаю утро, можно в понедельник или вторник. | I prefer mornings, possible Monday or Tuesday. |
| Мой номер телефона такой-то: +7... — можно перезвонить? | My phone number is +7... — can you call me back? |

## Concise explanation
Use the polite conditional form "я хотел(а) бы" to request an appointment. Describe symptoms in short declarative sentences using present perfect-duration expressions like "уже три дня" or simple present for ongoing states. For availability questions, use "Когда у вас есть..." or "Есть ли у вас..." followed by a time frame. Limitation: do not use urgent medical vocabulary here; this lesson avoids emergency expressions (e.g., "срочно", "скорая") because those require different register and actions.

## Worked dialogue
А: Здравствуйте, я хотел(а) бы записаться на приём к терапевту.
*Hello, I would like to make an appointment with a general practitioner.*

Б: Добрый день. На какую дату вы рассчитываете?
*Good afternoon. What date are you hoping for?*

А: У меня небольшая температура и кашель уже четыре дня; можно на следующую неделю утром?
*I have a slight fever and cough for four days; is it possible next week in the morning?*

Б: На следующей неделе есть свободное время в среду в 10:30 и в пятницу в 9:00. Какой вариант вам удобен?
*Next week there is availability on Wednesday at 10:30 and Friday at 9:00. Which option is convenient for you?*

А: Подойдёт среда в 10:30. Можете записать меня на это время и позвонить для подтверждения?
*Wednesday at 10:30 will do. Can you book me for that time and call to confirm?*

Б: Конечно. Какой номер телефона для связи?
*Certainly. What phone number can we reach you at?*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "Hello, I would like to schedule a dental check-up next Tuesday in the afternoon." Use polite phrasing.
2. Formulate in Russian: "I have had a mild headache for two days, I would like to see a doctor on Friday morning."
3. Ask in Russian if there is a same-week appointment available using "Есть ли у вас..." and include "завтра".
4. Respond in Russian to a scheduler who offers "вторник в 11:00" by accepting and giving a phone number.
5. Write a short Russian sentence asking whether you should bring previous medical records: "Should I bring my previous medical records?"

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences in Russian describing a fictional, non-urgent reason for an appointment and asking about availability. Include:
- A polite opening ("Здравствуйте, ...").
- One sentence describing symptoms or the reason (use "уже X дней" or "после ...").
- A preferred time or day.
- A question about next available times.
- A request for confirmation by phone or email.
- Provide a contact method (fictional number or email).
All sentences should be simple and clear; avoid emergency language.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for Retrieval 1: "Вчера я болел(а)." — "Yesterday I was sick." (Use болел for masculine, болела for feminine.)
2. Model answer for Retrieval 2: "Я вижу врача." — "I see the doctor." (Accusative animate masculine: "врача".)
3. Model answer for Retrieval 3: "Вы можете мне помочь?" — "Can you help me?" (Polite question using inversion/word order.)
4. Model response for Independent production (example, 7 sentences):
   1) "Здравствуйте, я хотел(а) бы записаться на приём." — "Hello, I would like to make an appointment."
   2) "У меня уже три дня болит горло и чуть-чуть повышена температура." — "I have had a sore throat for three days and a slightly elevated temperature."
   3) "Не срочно, но хотелось бы на следующую неделю." — "Not urgent, but I would like next week."
   4) "Когда у вас есть свободные утренние окна?" — "When do you have free morning slots?"
   5) "Мне подходит вторник или четверг." — "Tuesday or Thursday works for me."
   6) "Пожалуйста, подтвердите по телефону +7 900 000 0000." — "Please confirm by phone +7 900 000 0000."
   7) "Спасибо большое." — "Thank you very much."
