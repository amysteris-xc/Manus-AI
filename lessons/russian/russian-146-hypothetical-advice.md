## Core pattern
A concise teaching paragraph: Use "я бы" + past-tense verb (or conditional meaning) to express a tentative suggestion: "I would ...". Use "стоит" + infinitive to recommend something more generally: "It is worth ... / You should ...", but it's less personal and still softer than an order. Both are not commands; add "может быть" or "если хочешь" to soften further.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я бы почитал ту книгу. | I would read that book. |
| Тебе стоит отдохнуть. | It's worth your time to rest. / You should rest. |
| Я бы не спешил(а) с решением. | I wouldn't rush to decide. |

## Concise explanation
Form: "я бы" + past-tense verb (e.g., "я бы сделал") expresses a hypothetical or tentative recommendation; it borrows the past-tense form but has conditional meaning. "Стоит" + infinitive (e.g., "стоит попробовать") is impersonally recommending an action. Limitation/common confusion: "я бы" looks like past tense but functions as conditional; do not translate it as a simple past. Also, "стоит" is impersonal and cannot be used with a personal subject like "я стоит"; use "мне стоит" for personal experience.

## Worked dialogue
А: Мне не хватает времени на учёбу, но я хочу успевать.
*I'm short on time for studying, but I want to keep up.*

Б: Я бы планировал занятия по 30 минут вместо нескольких часов подряд.
*I would schedule 30-minute study sessions instead of several hours in a row.*

А: А стоит ли делать перерывы во время таких занятий?
*And is it worth taking breaks during such sessions?*

Б: Да, стоит делать короткие перерывы каждые 25–30 минут, если хочешь быть продуктивнее.
*Yes, it's worth taking short breaks every 25–30 minutes if you want to be more productive.*

А: Спасибо, я попробую так.
*Thanks, I'll try that.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian using "я бы": "I would write notes after each lesson."
2. Rewrite the sentence "Тебе надо отдыхать" (You must rest) using "стоит" to make it a softer suggestion.
3. Create two sentences: one with "я бы" suggesting a study habit, one with "стоит" recommending rest.
4. Convert the suggestion "Я бы не ел много сладкого" into a softened version by adding "может быть".
5. Role-play: write a short 4-line exchange where one person asks about planning a weekend and the other uses "стоит" twice to recommend low-stakes activities.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional message (6–10 sentences) to a friend who says they feel overwhelmed with school tasks. Use at least three instances of "я бы" and at least two instances of "стоит". Keep the tone gentle and avoid giving professional advice; frame suggestions as personal opinions and optional ideas (use phrases like "может быть", "если хочешь", "на мой взгляд"). Use clear past-tense verb forms after "я бы" and infinitives after "стоит".
