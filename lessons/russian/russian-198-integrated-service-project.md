## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to: politely clarify requests, set and communicate reasonable boundaries, propose an alternative, and arrange a follow-up in a multi-step service dialogue (e.g., coordinating a minor repair, scheduling a small delivery, or resolving a simple billing question).

## Core pattern
This lesson centers on a three-step pattern: clarify the request, set a boundary or limitation, and offer a next step (alternative or follow-up).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Можете, пожалуйста, уточнить, что именно требуется? | Could you please clarify exactly what is required? |
| Я могу сделать это, но не раньше чем через два дня. | I can do that, but not earlier than in two days. |
| Вместо этого я могу предложить альтернативу: / Договоримся на... | Instead, I can offer an alternative: / Let's agree on... |

## Concise explanation
The pattern combines a polite question for clarification (using "можете/могли бы" + infinitive), a clear boundary statement with a time or scope limitation (using "могу" or "не могу" + reason or time), and a follow-up proposal (using "вместо этого", "могу предложить", or "договоримся на"). A common confusion: Russian allows several polite forms (Можно/Можете/Могли бы); choose one consistent level of formality — mixing very formal and very informal forms can sound awkward. Also, time expressions with "через" and "в" are not interchangeable (e.g., "через два дня" = in two days; "в два дня" = at two o'clock).

## Worked dialogue
А: Можете, пожалуйста, уточнить, что именно нужно установить в комнате?
*Could you please clarify exactly what needs to be installed in the room?*

Б: Требуется установить простой стеллаж и подключить одну лампу, но у меня мало времени завтра. Можете приехать в пятницу?
*It is required to install a simple shelf and connect one lamp, but I have little time tomorrow. Can you come on Friday?*

А: Я могу установить стеллаж и подключить лампу, но не могу приехать в пятницу — уже занята. Могу приехать в субботу утром. Это устроит?
*I can install the shelf and connect the lamp, but I cannot come on Friday — I'm already booked. I can come Saturday morning. Does that work?*

Б: Суббота утром подходит. Только уточню: нужен ли вам доступ к коридору на два часа?
*Saturday morning works. Just to confirm: will you need access to the hallway for two hours?*

А: Да, доступ нужен примерно на два часа. Я пришлю сообщение с точным временем в пятницу вечером.
*Yes, access will be needed for about two hours. I'll send a message with the exact time on Friday evening.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the core pattern using "могли бы" instead of "можете" and keep the same meaning.
2. Convert the boundary statement to say the work will take three hours instead of two.
3. Write a brief Russian message (one sentence) confirming the meeting time and asking for contact details.
4. Change the dialogue so the service provider offers an earlier partial visit (30 minutes) to assess but not complete the work; write that line in Russian plus translation.
5. Role-play by writing a short reply that politely declines the offered Saturday time and proposes a weekday evening instead.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional dialogue message exchange (either as one person sending multiple messages or as a short back-and-forth) where you:
- Ask for clarification about a small service task,
- State a boundary or limitation (time, scope, materials),
- Offer an alternative or partial solution,
- Arrange a concrete follow-up (time or promise to message),
- Keep the situation ordinary and non-sensitive.
Each sentence must be in Russian with an adjacent precise English translation.

You are ready to move on if you can: (1) clearly ask for clarification in Russian, (2) state a concrete limitation or boundary without ambiguity, (3) offer a reasonable alternative or partial solution, and (4) schedule or promise a specific follow-up. If any of these feel shaky, repeat the Guided practice and the Independent production exercise until they are comfortable.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model phrase for clarification: "Могли бы Вы уточнить, что именно требуется?" — "Could you clarify exactly what is required?" (Polite, formal.)
2. Boundary converted to three hours: "Я могу сделать это, но это займет около трёх часов." — "I can do that, but it will take about three hours."
3. Sample confirmation message: "Подтверждаю встречу в субботу в 9:00. Пожалуйста, пришлите номер телефона." — "I confirm the meeting on Saturday at 9:00. Please send your phone number."
4. Early partial visit offer: "Я могу зайти на 30 минут в пятницу, чтобы осмотреть и подготовить материалы, а завершить работу в субботу." — "I can come for 30 minutes on Friday to inspect and prepare materials, and finish the work on Saturday."
