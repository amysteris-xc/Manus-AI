## Core pattern
Short teaching: To talk about low-stakes risks and mitigations, use a simple three-part pattern: state the risk (noun or short clause), propose a preventive step (modal or imperative), and acknowledge the remaining limit (short clause with "но" or "всё ещё"). Use everyday objects and verbs you know.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Риск: чашка может опрокинуться. | Risk: the cup can tip over. |
| Предотвращение: надень крышку на чашку. | Prevention: put a lid on the cup. |
| Ограничение: но вода всё ещё может пролиться, если крышка не плотно закрыта. | Limitation: but water can still spill if the lid is not tightly closed. |

## Concise explanation
Form: The pattern typically uses a modal possibility (может) or passive construction for the risk, an imperative or modal verb (надень, нужно) for prevention, and a contrast clause with но or всё ещё for the remaining limit. Meaning: you name what could go wrong, what you will do to reduce that chance, and what cannot be fully prevented. Limitation/possible confusion: Russian uses aspect (perfective vs. imperfective) in verbs; here prefer imperfective for ongoing possibilities (может проливаться) and perfective for completed preventive actions (надеть крышку — completed action). Do not overgeneralize — aspect rules have many exceptions and depend on verb pairs.

## Worked dialogue
А: У нас сегодня кофе, риск, что чашка опрокинется?
*We have coffee today; is there a risk that the cup will tip over?*

Б: Да, чашка может опрокинуться, особенно на краю стола.
*Yes, the cup can tip over, especially on the edge of the table.*

А: Что можно сделать, чтобы уменьшить риск?
*What can be done to reduce the risk?*

Б: Надень крышку на чашку и поставь её подальше от края, но она всё ещё может опрокинуться, если заденут стол.
*Put a lid on the cup and place it farther from the edge, but it can still tip over if someone bumps the table.*

А: Понял. Нужно быть осторожным и всё равно держать рядом салфетки.
*Understood. Need to be careful and still keep napkins nearby.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Translate into Russian: "Risk: the phone can fall." Use простая конструкция with может.
2. Write a one-sentence preventive step in Russian: "Use a case" or "Put it on a stand."
3. Add a one-sentence limitation: begin with но or всё ещё and state a remaining limit.
4. Combine the three parts into a short three-sentence paragraph in Russian describing a low-stakes home risk (e.g., plant, laptop, pan).
5. Read your paragraph out loud, then record yourself and check pronunciation against a native model (use a dictionary audio); note one word that felt difficult and look up its stress.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional note (6–10 sentences) in Russian from the perspective of a flatmate warning others about a small household risk and proposed mitigations. Include:
- One sentence that names the risk.
- One sentence with a preventive imperative or modal suggestion.
- One sentence acknowledging a residual limitation (use но or всё ещё).
- Two sentences describing where to keep the item and when to be especially careful.
- Close with a polite reminder phrase like "пожалуйста" or "не забудьте".
Use vocabulary you know from Russian 001–101 where possible; keep sentences simple and grammatically accurate.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) produce a three-part Russian sequence naming a low-stakes risk, saying a preventive step, and stating a remaining limit without hesitation; (2) use может or нужно appropriately for possibility/necessity; (3) keep verb aspect simple (use perfective for completed preventive actions, imperfective for ongoing possibilities). If you hesitate on aspect or modal usage, review this lesson and retake the guided practice.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Example answer for Guided practice 1–3 combined:
   1. Риск: телефон может упасть. (Risk: the phone can fall.)
   2. Предотвращение: положи телефон в чехол. (Prevention: put the phone in a case.)
   3. Ограничение: но экран всё ещё может треснуть при сильном ударе. (Limitation: but the screen can still crack on a hard impact.)
2. Model short paragraph (Independent production example, 6 sentences):
   1. В кухне стоит кружка с чаем — риск, что она опрокинется.
      (There is a mug with tea in the kitchen — a risk that it will tip over.)
   2. Наденьте крышку и поставьте кружку дальше от края стола.
      (Put a lid on it and place the mug further from the table edge.)
   3. Но горячий чай всё ещё может пролиться, если кто-то заденет стол.
      (But hot tea can still spill if someone bumps the table.)
   4. Лучше держать кружку на столике рядом с креслом, а не на журнальном столе.
      (Better to keep the mug on the side table by the armchair, not on the coffee table.)
   5. Будьте осторожны вечером, когда в комнате мало света.
      (Be careful in the evening when the room is dimly lit.)
   6. Пожалуйста, не ставьте книги на угол стола рядом с кружкой.
      (Please don't put books on the corner of the table next to the mug.)
3. Criteria for correctness in the independent task:
   - The text names a clear risk, gives at least one preventive action, and explicitly states a remaining limit using но or всё ещё.
   - Grammar: verbs agree with subjects; aspect is reasonable (preventive action can be perfective).
   - Lexicon: everyday vocabulary from prerequisites; no invented idioms.
4. Example short practice sentences (for spaced repetition speaking):
   - Риск: сумка может упасть с полки. — Предотвращение: положи её ниже. — Ограничение: но вещи всё ещё могут поцарапаться.
     (Risk: the bag can fall from the shelf. — Prevention: put it lower. — Limitation: but items can still be scratched.)
