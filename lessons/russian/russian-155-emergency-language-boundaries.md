## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Recognize and produce a handful of concise Russian emergency phrases (calling for help, police, ambulance, fire).
- Say basic, clear statements about the nature of an emergency and request that someone contact local emergency services.
- State, in Russian, when you should seek immediate local help (use simple temporal and necessity expressions).
- Understand limits: this lesson trains language for contacting help, not how to perform medical or technical interventions.

## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on short, unambiguous declarative sentences and imperative requests used when contacting help.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Помогите, пожалуйста! | Help, please! |
| Вызовите скорую помощь! | Call an ambulance! |
| Позвоните в полицию! | Call the police! |
| Пожар! Нужна пожарная охрана! | Fire! Fire brigade needed! |
| Это экстренная ситуация. | This is an emergency situation. |
| Я не могу дышать. | I cannot breathe. |

## Concise explanation
Forms: Imperative verbs (e.g., "Помогите", "Вызовите") are used to request immediate actions from others; they are polite but urgent when used with "пожалуйста" optionally omitted. Declarative short sentences (e.g., "Это экстренная ситуация.") state the problem succinctly. Limitation/common confusion: do not rely on nuanced verbs or idiomatic phrases in emergencies — simple, unambiguous words are safer. Also, cultural and procedural variations exist by country; this lesson avoids region-specific instructions and focuses on language only.

## Worked dialogue
А: Помогите! Я упал и не могу встать.
*Help! I fell and cannot get up.*

Б: Спокойно. Кто-то вызовет скорую помощь. Где вы находитесь?
*Calm down. Someone will call an ambulance. Where are you located?*

А: Я рядом с большим магазином на улице Ленина. Это срочно.
*I'm near the big shop on Lenina Street. It's urgent.*

Б: Хорошо. Я сейчас позвоню. Оставайтесь на линии.
*Okay. I will call now. Stay on the line.*

А: Спасибо. Моё имя — Анна. У меня сильная боль в груди.
*Thank you. My name is Anna. I have severe chest pain.*

Б: Понятно. Я сказал(а), что это экстренная ситуация. Ожидайте помощи.
*Understood. I said this is an emergency situation. Expect help.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "Call the fire department!" Use an imperative form.
2. Convert "Это экстренная ситуация." into a short question to ask someone if they understand ("Do you understand this is an emergency?").
3. Write a short Russian sentence saying "I need help now" and another with "Please call 112" but without specifying a number if you prefer (use "службы экстренной помощи" as an alternative).
4. Role-play: write two lines (А and Б) where А says "Fire!" and Б asks "Is everyone out of the building?" in Russian.
5. Create a one-line Russian phrase informing someone you are alone and cannot move, using "я один/я одна" and a phrase meaning "cannot move".

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional message in Russian that you might say to a neighbor asking them to call for help. Include:
- One imperative request to call a service (e.g., ambulance or police).
- One concise declarative description of the problem.
- A sentence indicating your immediate location (keep it fictional and generic).
- Your name or a way to identify yourself.
- A calm instruction about what the neighbor should tell the emergency operator.
- End with a clear short sentence asking them to stay with you until help arrives.

Provide English translations for each Russian sentence.
