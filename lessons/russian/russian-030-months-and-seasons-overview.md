## Core pattern

Seasons answer the question “when?” with whole adverbial forms: **зимой** “in winter”, **весной** “in spring”, **летом** “in summer”, and **осенью** “in autumn”. Use them at the start of a routine sentence.

| Season phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| зимой | in winter |
| весной | in spring |
| летом | in summer |
| осенью | in autumn |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Что ты делаешь летом?
> *What do you do in summer?*
>
> **Б:** Летом я много читаю.
> *In summer I read a lot.*
>
> **А:** А зимой?
> *And in winter?*
>
> **Б:** Зимой я учусь.
> *In winter I study.*
