## Core pattern

For polite or formal requests, Russian adds **-те** to the imperative stem: **читайте** “read”, **пишите** “write”, **слушайте** “listen”. These are useful for following study instructions.

| Formal imperative | Infinitive | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| Читайте медленно. | читать | Read slowly. |
| Пишите аккуратно. | писать | Write carefully. |
| Слушайте внимательно. | слушать | Listen attentively. |
| Повторите, пожалуйста. | повторить | Please repeat. |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Пожалуйста, читайте этот текст.
> *Please read this text.*
>
> **Б:** Хорошо, я читаю.
> *Alright, I am reading.*
>
> **А:** Слушайте внимательно.
> *Listen attentively.*
>
> **Б:** Да, я слушаю.
> *Yes, I am listening.*
