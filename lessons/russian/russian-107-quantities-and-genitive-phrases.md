## What you will be able to do
You will be able to say that there is "a lot" or "a little" of something with `много` and `мало`, and "several" with `несколько`, using the genitive case after these words. You will practice memorized noun chunks (countable and mass nouns) rather than trying to produce full declension tables.

## Core pattern
Use `много`, `мало`, or `несколько` + noun in genitive. For mass nouns (water, time, bread) treat them as uncountable; for countable items the noun appears in genitive plural (or genitive singular for some mass nouns). Focus on memorized chunks.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| много воды | a lot of water |
| мало времени | little time / not much time |
| несколько книг | several books |
| много студентов | many students |
| мало вопросов | few questions |

## Concise explanation
`много`, `мало`, and `несколько` require the genitive form of the noun that follows. For most countable nouns learn the genitive plural chunk (e.g., "книг", "студентов"). For mass nouns that are uncountable in English (вода, хлеб, время) use the genitive singular or the established mass-form chunk (e.g., "воды", "хлеба", "времени"). Limitation: some nouns have irregular genitive plural forms or zero endings — memorize the chunk rather than applying one rule universally.

## Worked dialogue
А: У нас много времени?
*Do we have a lot of time?*

Б: Нет, у нас мало времени.
*No, we have little time.*

А: Может взять несколько бутербродов?
*Maybe take several sandwiches?*

Б: Да, только немного хлеба и много воды.
*Yes, only some bread and a lot of water.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian using a memorized chunk: "a lot of books."
2. Translate into Russian: "few questions."
3. Say in Russian: "several apples" (use the common genitive form).
4. Produce two sentences: one about water ("a little water") and one about students ("many students").
5. Replace the noun in this sentence and keep the correct genitive: "У нас много ______." Try with хлеб, времени, и друзей.

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences describing a short fictional day at a university café. Use at least three different quantity expressions from this lesson (`много`, `мало`, `несколько`) and include at least one mass-noun phrase (e.g., вода, хлеб, время) and one countable-noun phrase (e.g., книги, студенты, яблоки). Keep sentences simple and memorize the noun chunks before writing.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. много книг — model criterion: "книг" is the genitive plural of "книга".
2. мало вопросов — model criterion: "вопросов" is the genitive plural of "вопрос".
3. несколько яблок — model criterion: "яблок" is the genitive plural of "яблоко" (irregular plural stem).
4. Sample short paragraph (6 sentences):
   - Я пришёл в кафе рано.
     *I came to the café early.*
   - Там было много студентов.
     *There were many students there.*
   - Я купил несколько булочек и немного хлеба.
     *I bought several rolls and a little bread.*
   - Пожалуйста, дайте мне много воды.
     *Please give me a lot of water.*
   - У меня было мало времени до лекции.
     *I had little time before the lecture.*
   - Я оставил несколько писем на столе.
     *I left several letters on the table.*
