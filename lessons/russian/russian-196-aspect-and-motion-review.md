## Core pattern
Use imperfective verbs for ongoing or habitual motion, and perfective verbs for completed single arrivals or single completed actions.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я иду в библиотеку. | I am going (on foot, right now) to the library. |
| Я хожу в библиотеку каждый день. | I go to the library (habitually, regularly). |
| Я пришёл в библиотеку. | I arrived at the library (completed arrival). |
| Я читал книгу в пути. | I was reading a book while traveling. |
| Я прочитал статью в поезде. | I finished reading the article on the train. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Motion verbs typically come in imperfective/perfective pairs or imperfective pairs with directional distinctions (идти/ходить; ехать/ездить). Use imperfective (идти/ехать/ходить/ездить) to describe ongoing actions or habitual movement. Use perfective (прийти/прибыть/доехать/приходить in perfective forms like прийти) to describe completed single arrivals or completed actions (прочитать = finish reading). Limitation/common confusion: some verbs of motion have directionality pairs (e.g., идти vs. ходить) that do not have a single perfective counterpart; you must choose appropriate perfective verbs for single completed events (e.g., прийти for arrival). Also, some verbs change meaning when perfective (e.g., вспомнить vs. помнить).

## Worked dialogue
А: Я иду в университет, но задерживаюсь, потому что читаю новую статью.
*I am going to the university, but I am delayed because I am reading a new article.*

Б: Ты всегда ходишь в университет пешком?
*Do you always go to the university on foot?*

А: Обычно я хожу, но сегодня еду на трамвае, потому что мне нужно принести книги.
*Usually I go (habitually), but today I am taking the tram because I need to bring books.*

Б: Когда ты приедешь, напиши мне — я приду в библиотеку.
*When you arrive, write to me — I will come to the library.*

А: Хорошо. Я напишу, когда приеду.
*Okay. I will write when I arrive.*

## Guided practice — five tasks
1. Convert the sentence "I go to the gym every week" into Russian using a habitual motion verb. Write the Russian sentence and its English translation.
2. Translate "She arrived at the station" choosing the correct perfective arrival verb; supply Russian + English.
3. Make two Russian sentences that contrast ongoing travel and completed arrival using "читать" and its perfective counterpart. Provide translations.
4. Write a Russian sentence describing you riding (by transport) habitually to work and another sentence describing you having completed that trip once (perfective). Provide translations.
5. Identify in the sentence "Он шёл в музей и читал путеводитель" which verbs are imperfective and explain why.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph (travel or study theme) that integrates:
- one sentence with ongoing motion (use an imperfective motion verb),
- one sentence with habitual movement (use ходить/ездить),
- one sentence with a completed arrival (use a perfective arrival verb),
- at least one ongoing activity during motion (imperfective verb like читать или слушать),
- and one completed action (perfective verb like прочитать или написать).
Provide the Russian paragraph followed by an accurate English translation under each sentence. Aim for natural, simple vocabulary.
