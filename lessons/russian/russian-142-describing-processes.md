## What you will be able to do
You will be able to describe a simple, familiar process (for example, preparing a study session or organizing notes) in ordered steps using neutral instructional language and common sequence connectors (сначала, затем, потом, в конце). Sentences will be short, clear, and focused on actions.

## Core pattern
We present neutral instructional sentences using sequence connectors and infinitives or imperatives.
| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Сначала подготовьте место для учёбы. | First, prepare a place to study. |
| Затем разложите учебники и тетради по порядку. | Then, arrange your textbooks and notebooks in order. |
| После этого сделайте план занятия на 30 минут. | After that, make a 30-minute study plan. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Use sequence connectors (сначала, затем, после этого, потом, в конце) + imperative (подготовьте) or infinitive phrase with modal constructions (нужно + infinitive). Meaning: these structures order actions clearly and neutrally. Limitation/common confusion: imperatives and infinitives are different styles—imperative адресует слушателя directly (e.g., Подготовьте место), while infinitive constructions with нужно sound more general (e.g., Нужно подготовить место). Do not mix person-specific past-tense narrative markers when intending neutral instructions.

## Worked dialogue
А: Сначала подготовь стол и стул.
*First, prepare the table and the chair.*

Б: Хорошо. Затем что мне разложить?
*Okay. Then what should I lay out?*

А: Разложи учебники и тетради по предметам.
*Lay out the textbooks and notebooks by subject.*

Б: После этого сделать план на 30 минут?
*After that, should I make a 30-minute plan?*

А: Да, потом выключи телефон на половину часа.
*Yes, then turn off your phone for half an hour.*

## Guided practice
1. Rewrite the core pattern sentence "Сначала подготовьте место для учёбы." as an instruction using нужно + infinitive.
2. Translate into Russian: "Then put the notes for math on the left and the notes for Russian on the right."
3. List three sequence connectors in Russian and use each in a short imperative sentence.
4. Convert an imperative sentence into a neutral infinitive instruction (e.g., "Разложи книги" → "Нужно разложить книги").
5. Read aloud the worked dialogue and record yourself; check that your stress and endings match the written forms.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence paragraph in Russian describing how you prepare a 30-minute study session. Use at least three different sequence connectors (сначала, затем, после этого, потом, в конце). Use neutral instructional language (imperative or нужно + infinitive). Each sentence should be simple and focus on one action.
