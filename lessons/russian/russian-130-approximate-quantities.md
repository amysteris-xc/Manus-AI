## Core pattern
This lesson focuses on modifiers that make quantities and times approximate. Use them before numbers or time phrases.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| около пяти | about five |
| примерно двадцати минут | approximately twenty minutes |
| несколько часов | several hours |
| около семи часов | around seven o'clock |

## Concise explanation
Около and примерно are near-synonyms used before numerals or time expressions to mean "about" or "approximately." Они часто стоят перед числительными (около пяти, примерно десяти минут). Несколько means "a few" or "several" and usually takes the genitive plural (несколько минут, несколько человек). Limitation: when you use несколько with precise calendar expressions (e.g., "несколько 1-го числа") it can sound odd — prefer примерно or около for dates and clock times when you need an approximate day or hour.

## Worked dialogue
А: Ты во сколько придёшь на встречу?
*What time will you arrive at the meeting?*
Б: Я приду около семи.
*I will come around seven.*
А: Тебе нужно ждать примерно сколько минут?
*About how many minutes do you need to wait?*
Б: Около пятнадцати минут, не больше.
*About fifteen minutes, no more than that.*
А: У тебя есть ещё дела после?
*Do you have other things after that?*
Б: Да, несколько коротких звонков, примерно полчаса в сумме.
*Yes, a few short calls, roughly half an hour in total.*

## Guided practice
1. Convert the English phrase "about ten people" into Russian. Write the Russian sentence using it in a short context: "At the meeting were about ten people."
2. Say "I'll be there in about twenty minutes" in Russian.
3. Transform "three hours" into an approximate phrase meaning "about three hours" in Russian.
4. Rewrite these precise times to be approximate: "в 8:00" → ? and "в 14:30" → ?.
5. Compose a short announcement: "The lecture will last approximately 90 minutes" in Russian, using примерно or около.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) describing a day when your schedule kept changing. Use at least four approximate expressions (около, примерно, несколько) and include at least one time and one quantity (people, minutes, hours). Keep the paragraph in Russian; provide an English translation below each Russian sentence. Aim for everyday vocabulary and natural word order.

Example requirements:
- 6–10 sentences.
- At least four uses of approximate expressions in total.
- Include at least one clock time (e.g., семь часов) modified by около/примерно and one quantity like несколько человек or примерно тридцати минут.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. "At the meeting were about ten people." — На встрече было около десяти человек.
2. "I'll be there in about twenty minutes." — Я буду через примерно двадцать минут. (More natural: Я буду примерно через двадцать минут.)
3. "About three hours." — примерно три часа / около трёх часов.
4. "в 8:00" → около восьми часов; "в 14:30" → примерно в половине третьего / примерно в четырнадцать тридцать. (Note: "половине третьего" is idiomatic for 14:30.)
