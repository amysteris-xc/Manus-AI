- Ask a simple clarifying question about the exact time or day.
- Confirm or correct a tentative date using polite phrases.

## Core pattern
Short teaching paragraph: To state a deadline, use the date or weekday with phrase markers like "к" (by) or "до" (until) and a time phrase if needed. To ask for clarification, use question words like "когда?" and confirm uncertainty with "Вы уверены?" or "Правильно ли я понимаю...?" Below are practical sentence patterns.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Сдать проект нужно к пятнице. | The project needs to be submitted by Friday. |
| Дедлайн — 15 мая. | The deadline is May 15. |
| Это нужно до трёх часов дня? | Is this needed by three o’clock in the afternoon? |
| Вы уверены в дате? | Are you sure about the date? |
| Можно уточнить время? | May I clarify the time? |

## Concise explanation
Use "к" + weekday or "до" + time to express "by" a certain point (deadline). When giving a calendar date, Russian usually places the day before the month ("15 мая"). For asking clarification, use "Когда?" (When?) or "Во сколько?" (At what time?). Limitation: when using ordinal forms with dates (e.g., "пятнадцатого мая") you must match case for the surrounding grammar — beginners can safely use the nominative cardinal form "15 мая" in many spoken contexts to avoid declension errors.

## Worked dialogue
А: Сдать эссе нужно к понедельнику?
*Is the essay due by Monday?*

Б: Да, к понедельнику вечером.
*Yes, by Monday evening.*

А: Во сколько именно вечером? Точнее: в семь или в десять?
*At what time exactly in the evening? More precisely: at seven or ten?*

Б: В семь. Вы уверены, что успеете?
*At seven. Are you sure you'll make it?*

А: Пока да, но можно уточнить по дате ещё раз? Пятого числа, верно?
*So far yes, but may I confirm the date again? The fifth, right?*

Б: Да, пятое мая — правильно.
*Yes, May fifth — correct.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "The homework is due by Wednesday."
2. Write in Russian: "Is the meeting on June 10 or June 11?"
3. Convert the English date "August 3" into natural Russian spoken form.
4. Role-play: write two sentences (А and Б) where А states a deadline and Б asks "When exactly?" in Russian.
5. Correct this sentence for a learner: "Дедлайн — на 20 марта." If incorrect, rewrite it correctly and explain briefly in one sentence.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional message (6–10 sentences) in Russian to a study partner describing a group assignment deadline and asking one clarifying question about time. Include:
- a calendar date (day + month),
- a weekday name,
- a time expression (hour of day),
- a clarifying question using "Во сколько?" or "Когда?",
- a confirmation phrase such as "Правильно?" or "Вы уверены?".
All sentences must be short and clear.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model answer for Guided practice 1: "Домашнее задание нужно сдать к среде." — Acceptable variations: "Нужно сдать домашнее задание к среде."
2. Model answer for Guided practice 2: "Встреча 10 июня или 11 июня?" or "Встреча 10 июня или 11-го июня?" — Either is conversationally acceptable.
3. Model answer for Guided practice 3: "3 августа" or spoken "третьего августа" (note: the latter requires correct case in a sentence).
4. Role-play model (А and Б): А: "Дедлайн — в пятницу." Б: "Во сколько в пятницу?" — Short, clear confirmation is the goal.
5. Correction for "Дедлайн — на 20 марта.": Correct: "Дедлайн — 20 марта." Explanation: For dates, Russian typically uses the day before month without "на"; use "на" only in certain locative contexts (e.g., "на 20-е число" is colloquial but less standard).
