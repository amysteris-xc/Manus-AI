# Russian 019 — Basic Motion with `в` and `на`

**Estimated study time:** 25–35 minutes
**Prerequisites:** Russian 001–017 and the immediately preceding lessons
**Author:** Manus AI

## What you will be able to do

By the end of this lesson, you should be able to distinguish a few memorized motion phrases using **в** and **на**, and ask a simple question with **куда?**.

## Retrieval practice

1. Translate **Я не учусь дома.**
2. Ask informally, “Are you at the library?”
3. Produce one short future sentence with **буду**.
4. From the previous C lesson, name one checked boundary that makes binary data processing safer.

## Core pattern

Use **куда?** for “where to?” when the sentence focuses on a destination. Treat **в библиотеку** and **в парк** as complete destination phrases today. The location forms learned earlier answer **где?**; this lesson adds a small, controlled set of “where to?” chunks.

| Question or phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|
| Куда? | Where to? |
| Я иду в парк. | I am going to the park. |
| Я иду в библиотеку. | I am going to the library. |
| Ты идёшь домой? | Are you going home? |

## Concise explanation

Start from the complete model phrase. Keep previously learned words in their established forms and change only the new element. This reduces errors and makes later review easier. Russian questions can keep statement word order; use a question mark and question intonation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Куда ты идёшь?
> *Where are you going?*
>
> **Б:** Я иду в парк.
> *I am going to the park.*
>
> **А:** Ты идёшь в библиотеку?
> *Are you going to the library?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, я не иду в библиотеку.
> *No, I am not going to the library.*
