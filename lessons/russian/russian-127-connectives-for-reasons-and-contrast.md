## Core pattern
Use one connective per sentence to keep relationships clear.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я остался дома, потому что мне было холодно. | I stayed home because I was cold. |
| Мне было холодно, поэтому я выпил тёплый чай. | I was cold, therefore I drank warm tea. |
| Он хотел идти в парк, но пошёл дождь. | He wanted to go to the park, but it started to rain. |

## Concise explanation
- потому что introduces a reason (clause giving cause). It connects a statement to its cause and usually introduces a full clause: [result], потому что [reason].
- поэтому introduces a consequence or result (it follows the cause). The usual order is [cause], поэтому [result].
- но is a simple coordinating conjunction meaning "but" used in spoken and written Russian to show contrast between two clauses.
- however-style однако is slightly more formal and often appears at the start of a sentence or clause to contrast with the previous idea.
Limitation/common confusion: потому что requires a full clause after it (not a single noun phrase), and поэтому should follow the clause that provides the cause; do not use both потому что and поэтому in the same short sentence to avoid redundancy.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я хотел пойти в кино, потому что фильм хорошая комедия.
*I wanted to go to the cinema because the movie is a good comedy.*

Б: Но билетов не осталось.
*But there were no tickets left.*

А: Поэтому я купил фильм онлайн.
*Therefore I bought the film online.*

Б: Однако вечером шёл сильный дождь, и мы остались дома.
*However, in the evening it was raining heavily, and we stayed home.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian using потому что: "She is tired because she worked late." Keep one connective per sentence.
2. Translate into Russian using поэтому: "It rained all day; therefore, the streets are wet."
3. Make two Russian sentences linking them with но: first say "I planned a picnic." then contrast with "It was cold."
4. Rewrite the pair in task 3 using однако at the start of the second sentence (use appropriate punctuation).
5. Combine a cause and result in Russian with separate sentences (first cause, second sentence beginning with поэтому): topic — "the bus was late" → "I was late for class."

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences (in Russian, Cyrillic) telling a short fictional sequence that includes:
- At least one sentence with потому что (reason).
- At least one sentence beginning with поэтому (result).
- At least one contrast using но or хотя (note: хотя is allowed but prefer но/однако for this lesson).
- Keep one relationship per sentence (i.e., each sentence should show either a cause → result or a contrast, not both).
Example structure to follow but not copy exactly: introduction sentence, cause sentence with потому что, result sentence with поэтому, contrast sentence with но/однако, concluding sentence.
