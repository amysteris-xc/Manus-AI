## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to propose specific wording in a shared document, suggest revisions, accept or decline changes politely, and ask for clarification — all using concise, consent-focused Russian phrases suitable for collaborative editing.

2. Recall the instrumental singular ending for feminine nouns from Russian 096–101. Provide the form for "правка".
3. Recall the polite imperative form from Russian 100–101. Give the polite request for "Пожалуйста, прочитайте" in Russian.
4. Recall a C concept: identify whether a 32-bit integer alignment requires a byte-boundary of 4 bytes or 8 bytes on a typical 32-bit architecture.

## Core pattern
Use the following short phrases to propose, agree, or ask for clarification.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Предлагаю оставить этот абзац. | I propose we keep this paragraph. |
| Согласен. / Согласна. | I agree. (male/female speaker) |
| Давайте уточним формулировку. | Let's clarify the wording. |
| Можно переформулировать так? | Could we rephrase it like this? |
| Я бы предложил/предложила заменить на... | I would suggest replacing with... |

## Concise explanation
These phrases are short, polite ways to manage edits in collaborative writing. "Предлагаю" is a first-person present-tense verb used to offer a suggestion; it can be followed by an infinitive or a clause. "Согласен" (male) and "согласна" (female) are one-word agreements and must match the speaker's gender. "Давайте уточним" uses the first-person plural imperative to invite joint clarification. Limitation: gender agreement is required for short participles/adjectives like "согласен/согласна"; do not use one form for all speakers.

## Worked dialogue
А: Предлагаю сократить этот абзац и оставить только ключевую мысль.
*I propose to shorten this paragraph and keep only the key idea.*

Б: Согласна, но давайте уточним предложение во втором предложении.
*I agree, but let's clarify the sentence in the second sentence.*

А: Можно переформулировать так: "Документ вводит основные принципы системы."
*Could we rephrase it like this: "The document introduces the system's main principles."*

Б: Хорошо, я бы предложила заменить "системы" на "проекта" для точности.
*Good, I would suggest replacing "system" with "project" for accuracy.*

А: Согласен, оставляем "проекта". Хотите, я внесу правку?
*I agree, we keep "project." Do you want me to make the edit?*

Б: Да, пожалуйста, внесите — и давайте уточним заголовок позже.
*Yes, please make it — and let's clarify the heading later.*

## Guided practice
1. Write three alternative one-line proposals beginning with "Предлагаю..." to change the introduction of a fictional README.
2. Respond to each proposal as a partner using "Согласен/Согласна" or a polite disagreement like "Предпочитаю другой вариант" and give a one-sentence reason.
3. Take one proposal and use "Можно переформулировать так?" then provide an alternate phrasing.
4. Use "Давайте уточним" to ask a specific clarifying question about terminology in the document.
5. Practice a short exchange (4 turns) where one person proposes a change and the other either accepts or proposes a compromise using "я бы предложил/предложила".

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional collaborative edit log (6–10 sentences) for a shared document in Russian. Use the following constraints:
- Start with "Предлагаю" in the first sentence.
- Include at least one instance of "Согласен" or "Согласна" that matches the speaker's gender.
- Include "Можно переформулировать так?" once with an alternative sentence in quotes.
- Use "Давайте уточним" to raise a clarification question.
- End with "Я внесу изменения" or "Я внесу правку" as confirmation of who will edit.

Each Russian sentence must be followed immediately by its precise English translation in italics.
