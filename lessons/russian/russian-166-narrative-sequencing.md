## Core pattern
We use `сначала` to introduce the first action, `потом` for the next action(s), `вдруг` for an unexpected event, and `поэтому` to express a simple cause-effect link.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Сначала он открыл дверь. | First he opened the door. |
| Потом она сказала, что уехала. | Then she said that she left. |
| Вдруг начался дождь. | Suddenly it started to rain. |
| Поэтому они вернулись домой. | Therefore they returned home. |

## Concise explanation
Form: `сначала`, `потом`, and `вдруг` are sentence adverbs placed before or after the subject or clause to mark sequence or interruption. `Поэтому` links a cause clause (before or implicit) to a result clause and is followed by the result. Meaning: `сначала` = first/initially, `потом` = then/after that, `вдруг` = suddenly, unexpectedly, `поэтому` = therefore/so. Limitation/common confusion: `поэтому` always introduces the result and cannot directly attach to a noun phrase; you need a clause that provides the reason (explicit or understood). Also, `вдруг` implies surprise and is less suitable for planned sequences.

## Worked dialogue
А: Сначала я положил книгу на стол.
*First I put the book on the table.*

Б: Потом ты ушёл?
*Then you left?*

А: Вдруг зазвонил телефон, поэтому я не успел закрыть окно.
*Suddenly the phone rang, so I did not have time to close the window.*

Б: Поэтому стало холодно в комнате?
*So it became cold in the room?*

А: Да, поэтому я вернулся и закрыл окно.
*Yes, therefore I returned and closed the window.*

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph (all sentences in Russian). Use `сначала` once, `потом` at least once, `вдруг` once, and `поэтому` once. Make each event easy to trace in time and include one clear causal link with `поэтому`. After your Russian paragraph, provide an adjacent precise English translation for each Russian sentence (one-to-one).

Guidelines:
- Keep verbs in past tense for clarity (e.g., пришёл, увидела, начался).
- Keep events concrete and short (one main verb per sentence).
- Ensure `поэтому` follows a clause that gives the reason.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model independent paragraph (6 sentences):
   Сначала я вышел из квартиры.
   *First I left the apartment.*
   Потом я купил хлеб в магазине.
   *Then I bought bread at the store.*
   Потом я пошёл в парк.
   *Then I went to the park.*
   Вдруг начался сильный дождь.
   *Suddenly a heavy rain began.*
   Поэтому я побежал под навес.
   *Therefore I ran under the awning.*
   Я дождался и вернулся домой.
   *I waited and returned home.*

2. Guided practice sample answers:
   1) Сначала она вошла в дом, потом сняла пальто. — *First she entered the house, then she took off her coat.*
   2) Начался дождь, поэтому мы промокли. — *It started to rain, so we got wet.*
   3) Я шёл по улице, вдруг начался гром. — *I was walking down the street, suddenly thunder began.*
   4) The cause word is "поэтому"; it introduces the result clause.
   5) Сначала он вскипятил воду, потом положил кофе, потом налил чашку. — *First he boiled water, then he put in coffee, then he poured a cup.*

3. Common error to avoid: placing `поэтому` before its reason clause. The reason must come first or be understood from context.

4. Byte-boundary/validation recall: Ensure a UTF-8 validator checks that multi-byte sequences are complete (no truncated continuation bytes) to avoid invalid characters.
