## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will reliably form short phrases and simple sentences that say where someone or something is (using `в` or `на`) and what someone is talking or thinking about (using `о`). You will recognize common stable-location phrases and choose the correct preposition for basic topical statements.

2. Recall a phrase from Russian 096–101 for "at work" and write it in Russian.
3. Recall a phrase from Russian 096–101 for "on the table" and write it in Russian.
4. Recall a basic C byte-boundary or validation concept from your parallel studies (for example: "checksum" or "boundary alignment") and state it in one short English sentence.

## Core pattern
Use `в` + prepositional case for enclosed or general "in" locations; use `на` + prepositional case often for surfaces or certain fixed expressions like workplaces and events; use `о` + prepositional case to mean "about" (a topic of speech or thought).

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| в школе | in (at) school |
| на работе | at work |
| о книге | about the book |
| в комнате | in the room |
| на столе | on the table |

## Concise explanation
The prepositions `в` and `на` with the prepositional case describe location: `в` is used for being inside or in general interior locations (в комнате, в магазине), while `на` is often used for surfaces (на столе) and many fixed expressions such as workplaces and events (на работе, на концерте). The preposition `о` (sometimes written `об/обо` before vowels or certain clusters) with the prepositional case marks the topic of speech or thought: `говорить о фильме` — "to talk about the film." Limitation/common confusion: some places are fixed by usage (e.g., `на работе` not `в работе`); you must learn these as collocations rather than applying a single rule to all nouns.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я сейчас в школе.
*I am at school now.*

Б: О чём ты говоришь на уроке?
*What are you talking about in class?*

А: Мы говорим о книге по истории.
*We are talking about a history book.*

Б: А где ваша группа после урока? Они на столовой или в библиотеке?
*And where is your group after class? Are they at the cafeteria or in the library?*

А: Они в библиотеке, потому что работают над проектом.
*They are in the library because they are working on a project.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Translate into Russian: "She is in the office." Use the correct preposition for workplace.
2. Translate into Russian: "We spoke about the film." Use `о` with the prepositional case.
3. Write a short Russian phrase for "on the balcony" and another for "in the balcony" — if one sounds unnatural, note which is preferred.
4. Choose the correct phrase: "on the lesson" vs "in the lesson" — write both Russian options and mark which is idiomatic for "during class discussion."
5. Replace the noun in this pattern keeping the preposition: change `в комнате` to `в аудитории`, and make a full sentence "They are in the auditorium."

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences in Russian describing where five different people are and what one of them is talking about. Use `в` or `на` consistently for each location and use `о` once to introduce a topic. Example structure to follow (but produce your own content): "Мария в школе. Иван на работе. Ольга в библиотеке. Мы говорим о книге. ...". Provide an English translation line after each Russian sentence.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: a) produce at least five correct short sentences using `в`/`на` for locations, b) form at least one correct sentence using `о` + prepositional for a topic, and c) identify at least two fixed collocations (e.g., `на работе`, `в школе`) without guessing. If you make systematic preposition errors, repeat the Guided practice and Spaced-repetition tasks before advancing.
