## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to produce brief Russian greetings, requests, and closings in three registers—informal (friend), neutral (acquaintance/colleague), and formal (official/elder)—and choose appropriate vocabulary and pronouns for each context.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall a Russian informal greeting you learned in Russian 096–101 and write it with translation.
2. Recall a neutral polite request structure from Russian 096–101 (use the verb in infinitive or present tense).
3. Recall a formal closing phrase from Russian 096–101 (e.g., a polite farewell).
4. Recall a C-language byte-boundary or validation concept briefly in English (for example, "buffer overflow prevention" or "input validation by length").

## Core pattern
Use the same communicative content (greeting → request → closing) in three registers. Change pronouns, verb forms, and set phrases to match register.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Привет! Можно попросить тебя помочь? Спасибо. | Hi! Could I ask you to help? Thanks. |
| Здравствуйте. Можете, пожалуйста, помочь мне? Заранее спасибо. | Hello. Could you please help me? Thanks in advance. |
| Добрый день. Не могли бы Вы мне помочь? Буду признателен(на). | Good afternoon. Could you help me (polite)? I would be grateful. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Informal register uses "ты", simple verbs (можешь, помоги), and casual closings (спасибо, пока). Neutral register often uses "можете" with polite words like пожалуйста and common closings (спасибо, до свидания). Formal register uses the polite pronoun "Вы" with capitalized Вы optionally in writing, conditional forms (не могли бы Вы), modal phrases (буду признателен/признательна), and more formal greetings (Добрый день, Здравствуйте). Limitation: Russian formal speech often adds mitigating phrases and past-tense conditionals for extra politeness; not every situation requires extreme formality—use context and the relationship to decide.

## Worked dialogue
А: Привет! Ты свободен сегодня вечером?
*Hi! Are you free tonight?*

Б: Привет! Да, свободен. Чем могу помочь?
*Hi! Yes, I'm free. How can I help?*

А: Не мог бы ты одолжить мне книгу на неделю? Спасибо.
*Could you lend me a book for a week? Thanks.*

Б: Конечно, без проблем. Увидимся вечером. Пока!
*Of course, no problem. See you tonight. Bye!*

А: Здравствуйте. Не могли бы Вы проверить этот документ?
*Hello. Could you (formal) check this document?*

Б: Добрый день. Да, могу. Отправьте файл, пожалуйста.
*Good afternoon. Yes, I can. Please send the file.*

## Guided practice
1. Convert the informal request "Помоги мне завтра" into a neutral and a formal version; write all three with translations.
2. Rewrite the neutral sentence "Можете прислать отчёт?" as a slightly more polite formal sentence.
3. Choose a single content request (e.g., "Could you confirm our meeting?") and write three full mini-messages (greeting, request, closing) in informal, neutral, and formal registers.
4. Replace the informal closing "Пока" with two neutral closings and one formal closing; provide translations.
5. Role-play: write a short 4-line dialog where a younger person asks an older neighbor for a favor using formal register.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional exchange (6–10 sentences total) in which you:
- Begin with a greeting appropriate to the chosen register.
- Make a single concrete request (about borrowing an item, scheduling, or asking for information).
- Provide a brief reason for the request.
- Close politely.
Produce the same exchange three times: once informal, once neutral, and once formal. For each sentence include an immediate precise English translation. Maintain accurate grammar and consistent register choices.

Estimated length: 6–10 sentences per register set (total 18–30 sentences).

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) produce a greeting, request, and closing in informal, neutral, and formal registers with appropriate pronouns and set phrases; (2) explain why you chose a particular register for a given fictional relationship; and (3) avoid mixing "ты" forms with formal "Вы" within a single message. If you cannot do all three, repeat the Guided practice and Independent production exercises.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model informal three-sentence mini-message: "Привет! Помоги мне, пожалуйста, с компьютером завтра? Спасибо!" — "Hi! Help me, please, with the computer tomorrow? Thanks!"
2. Model neutral three-sentence mini-message: "Здравствуйте. Можете помочь мне завтра с компьютером? Благодарю." — "Hello. Can you help me tomorrow with the computer? Thank you."
3. Model formal three-sentence mini-message: "Добрый день. Не могли бы Вы помочь мне завтра с компьютером? Буду Вам признателен(на)." — "Good afternoon. Could you (formal) help me tomorrow with the computer? I would be grateful to you."
4. Answer criteria for role-play: greet appropriately for the relationship, use consistent pronoun choice (ты or Вы) throughout, include a clear request verb (помочь/одолжить/проверить), and close with an appropriate courtesy phrase (спасибо/благодарю/с уважением or буду признателен(на)).
