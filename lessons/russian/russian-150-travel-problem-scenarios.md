# Russian 150 — Travel Problem Scenarios

## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to describe a simple travel problem (delayed train, wrong platform, missing reservation) in Russian and ask a staff member or fellow traveler for a clear, practical clarification or instruction.

## Retrieval practice — four numbered prompts
1. Recall Russian 096–101: write the Russian verbs for "to arrive", "to leave", and "to wait" in the present tense for "я" and "они".
2. Recall Russian 096–101: give the accusative form of a masculine inanimate noun "билет" and a feminine noun "поезд".
3. Recall Russian 096–101: produce a simple polite request pattern using "Можно...?" or "Не могли бы вы...?" with an example about directions.
4. Recall a C byte-boundary or validation concept: explain briefly what "byte-boundary" means when saving filenames and why you should validate input length before writing to a file (one sentence).

## Core pattern
Use this pattern to explain the problem and ask for a specific clarification:
[Problem statement] + "Что мне делать?" or "Можно ли...?" + specific action.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Поезд задерживается. Что мне делать? | The train is delayed. What should I do? |
| Я на неправильной платформе. Можно ли перейти на платформу три? | I'm on the wrong platform. May I go to platform three? |
| Моя бронь не найдена. Не могли бы вы проверить по номеру? | My reservation is not found. Could you check by the number? |

## Concise explanation
Form: state the situation with a short declarative clause (subject + verb + object/place), then add a direct practical question using "Что мне делать?" (What should I do?) or a polite request "Можно ли...?" (Is it possible to...?) or "Не могли бы вы...?" (Could you...?). Meaning: these constructions let you both describe the problem and request a clear action or permission. Limitation/common confusion: "Можно ли...?" asks for permission or possibility and is not the same as "Можно" as an exclamation; for asking staff for an action, prefer "Не могли бы вы...?" for politeness.

## Worked dialogue
А: Извините, мой поезд задерживается. Что мне делать?
*Excuse me, my train is delayed. What should I do?*

Б: Пожалуйста, подойдите к информационной стойке. Они проверят ситуацию.
*Please go to the information desk. They will check the situation.*

А: Я на платформе два, а по моему билету — платформа три. Можно ли перейти на другую платформу?
*I'm on platform two, but my ticket says platform three. May I change to the other platform?*

Б: Да, можно. Но сначала спросите у проводника, открыт ли переход на платформу три.
*Yes, you may. But first ask the conductor whether the passage to platform three is open.*

А: Моя бронь не найдена в системе. Не могли бы вы проверить по фамилии и номеру брони?
*My reservation is not found in the system. Could you check by last name and booking number?*

Б: Конечно, скажите, пожалуйста, фамилию и номер. Я посмотрю.
*Of course, please tell me your last name and the number. I will look.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Translate into Russian: "The bus is late. What should I do?" Use "Что мне делать?".
2. Compose a short sentence in Russian asking, "Can I change platforms?" using "Можно ли...?".
3. Role-play: write two Russian sentences you would say at the information desk if your reservation is missing (include "Не могли бы вы...?" or "Можно ли...?").
4. Convert this into a polite request: "Check my booking by number" using "Не могли бы вы проверить..." and add "пожалуйста".
5. Listen/Read check: read aloud your sentences and ensure you use stress on key words (platforma, бронь, номер); record or note any unclear syllables.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional exchange (6–10 sentences total, in Russian) in which:
- You are a traveler at a station with one of the three problems (choose delayed transport, wrong platform, or missing reservation).
- You clearly state the problem in one sentence.
- You ask a practical question using "Что мне делать?", "Можно ли...?", or "Не могли бы вы...?".
- The other person (staff or traveler) responds with a brief practical instruction (1–2 sentences).
- Finish with your confirmation or a follow-up question.

Each Russian sentence must be in Cyrillic. Below your Russian text, provide an exact English translation for each sentence on the next line in italics.

Example structure (do not copy exactly; create your own scenario):

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) accurately state a short travel problem in Russian with correct case and verb form; (2) form a polite practical question using "Что мне делать?", "Можно ли...?", or "Не могли бы вы...?" and have it understood by a Russian speaker; (3) respond appropriately to a simple instruction from staff. If any of these are uncertain, repeat the Guided practice and the Independent production exercise.
