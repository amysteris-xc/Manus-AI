## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to open a short meeting in Russian, introduce and move between two brief agenda items, ask for brief clarification, and close the meeting with an explicit next step.

## Retrieval practice
1. Recall Russian 096–101: Translate and say aloud the polite phrase used to get someone's attention in a formal group: "Excuse me, may I speak?" — "Извините, можно выступить?" — (practice pronunciation and intonation).
2. Recall Russian 096–101: Produce the phrase for offering a suggestion: "I suggest we..." — "Я предлагаю..." — (use with infinitive or noun phrase).
3. Recall Russian 096–101: Give the short formal closing: "Спасибо за внимание." — "Thank you for your attention."
4. Recall a C concept (byte-boundary/validation): Explain briefly why aligning a field on a byte boundary matters in TLV encodings — misalignment can cause incorrect parsing or extra padding.

## Core pattern
This pattern shows short facilitation phrases useful for a controlled meeting dialogue.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Доброе утро, всем. | Good morning, everyone. |
| Давайте начнём. | Let's begin / Let's start. |
| Первый пункт — отчёт по проекту. | First item — project report. |
| Переходим ко второму пункту. | We move on to the second item. |
| Есть вопросы по первому пункту? | Any questions about the first item? |
| На этом заканчиваем; следующий шаг — распределить задачи. | We finish here; next step — assign tasks. |

## Concise explanation
The phrases above are short, functional sentences for meeting flow. Use polite forms like "Давайте..." to propose action for the group and "Есть вопросы?" to invite brief questions. Limitation: in very formal meetings you may prefer "Разрешите начать" or full introductions; the casual "Давайте начнём" can feel too informal for some contexts.

## Worked dialogue
А: Доброе утро, всем. Давайте начнём.
*Good morning, everyone. Let's begin.*

Б: Доброе утро. Первый пункт — отчёт по проекту. Кто кратко расскажет?
*Good morning. First item — project report. Who will give a brief update?*

А: Я подготовил слайды. Есть вопросы по первому пункту?
*I prepared slides. Any questions about the first item?*

Б: Нет вопросов. Переходим ко второму пункту — риски и сроки.
*No questions. We move on to the second item — risks and deadlines.*

А: Хорошо. На этом заканчиваем; следующий шаг — распределить задачи по рискам.
*Good. We finish here; next step — assign tasks for the risks.*

## Guided practice
1. Say aloud and record: "Доброе утро, всем. Давайте начнём." Repeat until pronunciation is clear.
2. Write three variations for opening a meeting (one formal, one neutral, one casual). Translate each precisely.
3. Practice asking for clarification: form the question "Можете уточнить, пожалуйста?" and replace "уточнить" with "повторить" and "объяснить".
4. Role-play (with a partner or aloud) a two-topic meeting using the core pattern: open, present item 1, ask questions, transition, present item 2, close with next step.
5. Time a spoken run-through that lasts 1–2 minutes containing an opening, two agenda items, and a closing.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional meeting record (6–10 sentences) in Russian using the core facilitation phrases. Include: an opening line, naming two agenda items, one brief question and answer, and a closing with a concrete next step and who is responsible. Provide an English translation for each sentence immediately after it.

Example constraints: keep sentences simple, use present or past tense, and state responsibility with "ответственный — [name/role]" or "ответственный: [name/role]".

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can: (1) open a meeting naturally with "Доброе утро, всем. Давайте начнём," (2) name two agenda items and transition between them, (3) invite a short question with "Есть вопросы?" or "Можете уточнить, пожалуйста?", and (4) close with a next step that names a responsible person. If you struggle with any of these, repeat the guided practice and spaced-repetition tasks.
