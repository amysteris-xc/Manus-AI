## Core pattern

Colors follow the same gender-agreement patterns as other qualifying adjectives: **белый / белая / белое** “white”, **чёрный / чёрная / чёрное** “black”, and **синий / синяя / синее** “blue”.

| Object | Color phrase | Precise translation |
|---|---|---|
| стол (m) | белый стол | white table |
| книга (f) | чёрная книга | black book |
| окно (n) | синее окно | blue window |
| чай (m) | чёрный чай | black tea |

## Concise explanation

Work from the established models. Build phrases systematically, keeping previously learned words in their stable forms. Russian retains statement word order for simple questions, relying on question intonation and punctuation.

## Worked dialogue

> **А:** Это твоя книга?
> *Is this your book?*
>
> **Б:** Да, это моя чёрная книга.
> *Yes, this is my black book.*
>
> **А:** А стол синий?
> *And is the table blue?*
>
> **Б:** Нет, стол белый.
> *No, the table is white.*
