## What you will be able to do
You will be able to:
- Make a simple tentative hypothesis in Russian (e.g., "Maybe X happened").
- Give a short reason for that hypothesis.
- Add a phrase that limits your confidence (e.g., "probably", "I might be wrong").

## Core pattern
Use the pattern: [Hypothesis] + [Reason] + [Limit of confidence].

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Возможно, он опоздал, потому что был трамвай задержан. | Possibly, he was late because the tram was delayed. |
| Наверное, она забыла, так как не ответила на сообщение. | Probably, she forgot, since she didn't reply to the message. |
| Может быть, книга в сумке, но я не уверен(а). | Maybe the book is in the bag, but I'm not sure. |

## Concise explanation
Form: Start with a hedge word or phrase (Возможно, Наверное, Может быть), then give a short clause with a reason introduced by потому что, так как, or since-like connectors, and finish with a limiting phrase (я не уверен(а), кажется, вряд ли, может оказаться иначе). Meaning: these constructions express a tentative hypothesis plus the speaker's degree of confidence. Limitation/common confusion: "Наверное" and "Возможно" are close in meaning but differ in nuance—"наврéное" often implies a stronger guess based on some evidence, while "возможно" signals more general possibility. Do not treat them as fully interchangeable in all contexts.

## Worked dialogue
А: Возможно, он оставил ключи на столе.
*Possibly, he left the keys on the table.*

Б: Но почему ты так думаешь?
*But why do you think so?*

А: Я видел свет в комнате, и, может быть, он заходил туда.
*I saw light in the room, and maybe he went in there.*

Б: Это правда, но я не уверен — может быть, это был кто-то другой.
*That's true, but I'm not sure — it might have been someone else.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "Maybe she studied because the notes are on the desk, but I'm not certain."
2. Say aloud: "Наверное, окно открыто, потому что слышу шум." Add "I might be wrong." in Russian.
3. Write three different short sentences using Возможно, Наверное, and Может быть to hypothesize about a missing umbrella.
4. Correct this sentence: "Возможно он опоздал потому что трамвай задерживался я не уверен." Add punctuation and a limit phrase.
5. Pair exercise (self-check): ask a simple question in Russian about a hypothetical reason (e.g., "Почему он не пришёл?") and answer with a hypothesis + reason + limit.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a friend who might have missed a class. Include:
- One sentence starting with Возможно or Может быть.
- One sentence giving a reason introduced by потому что or так как.
- One sentence that limits your confidence (я не уверен(а), вряд ли, может быть иначе).
- Use at least one past tense verb and one modal or ability expression learned earlier.

Example constraints: keep the scenario harmless and everyday (e.g., traffic, alarm, wrong classroom).

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can consistently produce a short Russian statement that includes (1) a hypothesis word (возможно/наверное/может быть), (2) a reason clause (потому что/так как), and (3) a limiting phrase expressing uncertainty (я не уверен(а)/вряд ли/может быть иначе). Check that your Russian sentences have correct word order for clarity and that tense/agreement is correct for past or present verbs used. If you still hesitate about which hedge to use, practice distinguishing "возможно" (more neutral) and "наверное" (a bit more confident).
