# Russian 182 — Evidence Qualifiers

Prerequisites: Russian 001–181
Estimated study time: 25–35 minutes

Learning objective: qualify a claim according to whether it is observed, reported, or uncertain.
Teaching focus: Practice `по моему опыту`, `по данным текста`, and `может быть`. Emphasize that language cannot replace verification.

## What you will be able to do
You will be able to label statements as based on your experience, based on a text/source, or uncertain; make modestly cautious claims in conversation; and signal that further verification is needed.

## Retrieval practice — four numbered prompts
1. Recall and write one sentence from Russian 096–101 that uses the past tense (прошедшее время) and translate it into English.
2. Recall and write one sentence from Russian 096–101 that uses the accusative case after a transitive verb and translate it.
3. Recall and write one example from Russian 096–101 using a modal or semi-modal construction (e.g., нужно, можно) and translate it.
4. Recall a C-level concept: what is one byte-boundary or validation principle used when checking data integrity (e.g., checksum, parity, or boundary alignment)? Write it in English and explain in one sentence why it matters for reliable data.

## Core pattern
This lesson practices three common qualifiers:
- по моему опыту — based on my (personal) experience
- по данным текста — according to the text/source
- может быть — maybe / it may be

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| По моему опыту, так получается чаще. | In my experience, that happens more often. |
| По данным текста, температура снизилась на два градуса. | According to the text, the temperature dropped by two degrees. |
| Может быть, это связано с погодой. | Maybe this is related to the weather. |
| По моему опыту, люди чаще используют это слово в устной речи. | Based on my experience, people more often use this word in spoken speech. |

## Concise explanation
Form: each qualifier is a short phrase that precedes a clause. Meaning: "по моему опыту" attributes the statement to the speaker's personal experience; "по данным текста" attributes it to a cited text or source; "может быть" marks uncertainty or possibility. Limitation/common confusion: using these phrases does not make a statement objectively verified — especially "по моему опыту" is subjective and "по данным текста" requires that the text is trustworthy; always check the source if accuracy matters.

## Worked dialogue
А: По моему опыту, в этом районе утром меньше людей.
*In my experience, there are fewer people in this area in the morning.*

Б: По данным текста, магазин открывается в девять.
*According to the text, the shop opens at nine.*

А: Может быть, они открываются по-другому в праздники.
*Maybe they open differently on holidays.*

Б: Тогда лучше позвонить и уточнить.
*Then it's better to call and clarify.*

## Guided practice — five numbered concrete tasks
1. Translate into Russian: "In my experience, the bus is late."
2. Read a short paragraph (fictional or real) and write one sentence beginning with "По данным текста..." summarizing one fact.
3. Convert this firm statement into an uncertain one using "может быть": "Она придёт завтра."
4. Write two short sentences: one starting with "По моему опыту..." and one with "По данным текста..." about the same topic (e.g., coffee shops).
5. Role-play briefly (2–3 sentences) where you advise a friend and use at least one of the three qualifiers.

## Independent production exercise
Write 6–10 sentences in Russian describing a small fictional problem and possible causes, using all three qualifiers at least once. Include:
- One sentence that begins with "По моему опыту..."
- One sentence that begins with "По данным текста..." (you may invent a short source title)
- One sentence that includes "может быть" to express uncertainty
- Finish with one recommendation that includes a note to verify the information (use simple Russian).

Example constraints: keep vocabulary basic, use past or present tense, and avoid complex subordinate clauses beyond beginner–intermediate level.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can: (1) produce a natural sentence using each qualifier without hesitation, (2) correctly attribute one fact to a text and one to your experience in the same paragraph, and (3) convert a confident claim into a cautious one with "может быть" and explain why verification is needed.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Model translation for Guided practice 1: "По моему опыту, автобус опаздывает." — Accept variants with synonyms for "late" (опаздывает).
2. Model for Guided practice 2 (example): "По данным текста, музей закрывается в восемь вечера." — Accept accurate summary sentences that start with "По данным текста...".
3. Model for Guided practice 3: "Может быть, она придёт завтра." — Accept forms that place "может быть" before the clause; punctuation may vary.
4. Model Independent production criteria: 6–10 sentences, all three qualifiers used appropriately; factual-sounding "По данным текста..." should reference a named or implied source (e.g., "по данным отчёта" or "по данным статьи").
