## What you will be able to do
By the end of this lesson you will be able to:
- Compare two concrete study strategies using clear criteria (time investment, repetition frequency, and comfort).
- State your own reasoned preference in Russian and English, making it clear that this is your personal choice.
- Use concise Russian sentences to explain why one method suits you better.

## Core pattern
Teaching paragraph: We will practice comparing two study strategies using short comparative phrases and clear criteria words (время, повторение, комфорт). Use structures like "Мне удобнее..." and "Я предпочитаю..., потому что...".

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Мне удобнее короткие занятия каждый день. | Short sessions every day are more comfortable for me. |
| Я предпочитаю длительные сессии два раза в неделю, потому что у меня больше времени. | I prefer long sessions twice a week because I have more time. |
| Для меня важно повторение через интервалы. | For me, spaced repetition is important. |

## Concise explanation
Form and meaning: Use "Мне удобнее..." to state personal comfort (dative construction with "мне"). Use "Я предпочитаю..." + infinitive or noun to express preference. Use "потому что..." to give a reason. Limitation: do not use these as universal rules — say "Мне удобнее" or "Я предпочитаю" to make it explicitly personal; avoid implying others must do the same.

## Worked dialogue
А: Мне удобнее короткие занятия каждый день.
*It is more comfortable for me to have short sessions every day.*

Б: Почему ты так думаешь?
*Why do you think so?*

А: Потому что я могу уделять по двадцать минут, и повторение помогает запоминать.
*Because I can spend twenty minutes, and repetition helps me remember.*

Б: Я предпочитаю длительные сессии два раза в неделю, потому что мне легче погружаться в тему.
*I prefer long sessions twice a week because it is easier for me to immerse in a topic.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate into Russian: "I prefer short daily sessions because I have a busy schedule."
2. Translate into Russian: "For me, repetition every three days works best."
3. Write two sentences in Russian comparing time and repetition using "Мне удобнее..." and "Я предпочитаю...".
4. Record yourself saying one of those Russian sentences and check pronunciation against a native audio resource.
5. Write a one-paragraph (3–4 sentences) justification in Russian explaining your preference, ending with "Это мой личный выбор." and the precise English translation after it.

## Independent production exercise
Write a 6–10 sentence fictional paragraph in Russian describing which of the two study strategies you would choose for learning vocabulary and why. Use at least three of these criteria words: время, повторение, комфорт. Include one sentence that begins with "Мне кажется..." and end with a concluding sentence that states "Это мой личный выбор." Provide an English translation after the Russian paragraph.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on if you can (1) produce two clear Russian sentences comparing strategies using "Мне удобнее" and "Я предпочитаю", (2) give at least two concrete reasons using время, повторение, or комфорт, and (3) state explicitly in Russian that the preference is personal (e.g., "Это мой личный выбор."). If any of these feel uncertain, repeat the guided practice and spaced repetitions.

Это мой личный выбор." Criteria: 6 sentences, uses время/повторение/комфорт, includes "Мне кажется..." and ends with "Это мой личный выбор."
