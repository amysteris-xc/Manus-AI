## What you will be able to do
- Recognize and produce simple sentences with a small set of masculine animate direct objects using high-frequency verbs (e.g., видеть, знать, ждать).
- Distinguish these animate accusative forms from inanimate accusative forms in the limited modeled examples.
- Use modeled phrases like вижу брата and вижу друга correctly in short statements.

## Core pattern
This lesson models a small, high-frequency pattern: for some masculine animate nouns in the singular, the accusative form equals the genitive. Practice the fixed examples below.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я вижу брата. | I see (my) brother. |
| Я вижу друга. | I see (a) friend (male). |
| Я жду брата. | I am waiting for (my) brother. |
| Я знаю друга. | I know (a) friend (male). |

## Concise explanation
In Russian, certain masculine animate nouns in the singular use an accusative form that matches the genitive form. For this lesson you will learn and use only a handful of high-frequency pairs (e.g., брат → брата, друг → друга) with verbs like видеть, ждать, and знать. Limitation: do not generalize this mini-rule to all masculine nouns or to plural forms—those have additional patterns and exceptions that will be covered later.

## Worked dialogue
А: Я вижу брата.
*I see (my) brother.*

Б: Кто это?
*Who is that?*

А: Это мой друг. Я вижу друга рядом.
*This is my friend. I see a friend nearby.*

Б: Понятно. Ты ждёшь брата или друга?
*I see. Are you waiting for the brother or the friend?*

А: Я жду брата.
*I am waiting for my brother.*

## Guided practice
1. Replace the subject in "Я вижу брата." with "Она" and adjust the verb: write the full Russian sentence and English translation.
2. Change the verb in "Я вижу друга." to "ждать" (present tense) and write the Russian sentence and translation.
3. Make a negative sentence: turn "Я знаю друга." into negative ("I don't know a friend") in Russian and translate.
4. Write a yes/no question from "Я вижу брата." (use intonation or inversion as practiced in prior lessons) and translate.
5. Create a short three-word sentence using "ждать" + "брата" with a different subject (e.g., Мы ждём брата) and translate.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short 6–10 sentence fictional micro-paragraph (in Russian) about a small, harmless scene at a café or park where the speaker sees or waits for one or two male persons. Use at least three sentences that include one of the learned pairs (брат → брата, друг → друга) and verbs from the core pattern (вижу, жду, знаю). Provide an English translation for each Russian sentence. Keep vocabulary simple; avoid introducing new grammatical forms beyond what you have practiced.

## Final self-check
You are ready to move on when you can do the following without looking: produce "Я вижу брата" and "Я вижу друга" with correct spelling and stress placement; change subject and verb in the Guided practice examples while keeping the animate accusative forms correct; and write a short 6–10 sentence paragraph that uses брата or друга at least three times correctly. If you make consistent errors with other masculine nouns or plural forms, review this lesson again before generalizing.

## Spoiler: answers and model response
1. Replace subject with "Она": Она видит брата. — She sees (my) brother. (Verb: видит)
2. Change verb to ждать: Я жду друга. — I am waiting for a friend (male).
3. Negative of "Я знаю друга.": Я не знаю друга. — I do not know a friend (male).
4. Yes/no question: Ты видишь брата? — Do you see (your) brother? (intonation/inversion acceptable)
5. Three-word sentence: Мы ждём брата. — We are waiting for (our) brother.
