## What you will be able to do
You will be able to:
- Report information by attributing a source: use phrases like «я прочитал(а), что», «по словам …», «источник говорит».
- Distinguish between "someone reported this" and "I am certain": contrast attribution phrases with personal certainty (e.g., «я уверен(а)»).
- Practically say where you heard or read a claim and suggest verifying important facts.

## Core pattern
We focus on short attribution phrases. Use them before reported content.

| Russian | Precise English translation |
|---|---|
| Я прочитал(а), что поезд задерживается. | I read that the train is delayed. |
| По словам репортёра, погода улучшится завтра. | According to the reporter, the weather will improve tomorrow. |
| Источник говорит, что встреча перенесена. | The source says that the meeting was moved. |
| Я не уверен(а), но по слухам магазин закроется. | I'm not sure, but according to rumors the store will close. |

## Concise explanation
Forms: use a short attribution phrase + clause. «Я прочитал(а), что …» uses the past tense of читать with a gendered ending: add -а if you are female, omit if male (limitation: in informal writing some speakers drop gender marking, but standard grammar uses it). «По словам + [person/genitive] …» often follows with the person in genitive (e.g., по словам журналиста). «Источник говорит, что …» treats источник as a simple noun subject. Meaning: these phrases report information without committing to its truth. Limitation/common confusion: using an attribution phrase does not equal personal certainty—do not follow it with «я уверен(а)» unless you mean you are personally sure.

## Worked dialogue
А: «Я прочитал, что концерт начнётся в семь часов.»
*I read that the concert will start at seven o'clock.*

Б: «По словам организаторов, время может поменяться.»
*According to the organizers, the time might change.*

А: «Источник говорит, что билеты почти распроданы.»
*The source says that tickets are almost sold out.*

Б: «Я не уверен, давай проверим на сайте.»
*I'm not sure, let's check on the website.*

## Guided practice
1. Translate to Russian: "I read that the museum is closed on Mondays." Use «я прочитал(а), что…».
2. Rewrite this sentence to attribute to a journalist: "The minister resigned" → "According to the journalist, the minister resigned." Use «по словам …».
3. Change this certain sentence into attributed report: "Она уволена." → make it "The source says she was fired."
4. Combine attribution + uncertainty: Create a Russian sentence that says "I heard that the café might close, but I'm not sure."
5. Role-play: write two short lines (А and Б) where А reports a rumor with «по слухам» and Б asks to verify it.

## Independent production exercise
Write a short fictional paragraph (6–10 sentences) about a neighborhood event. Include:
- One sentence starting with «Я прочитал(а), что…»
- One sentence using «по словам…»
- One sentence using «источник говорит…»
- One sentence expressing your personal uncertainty with «я не уверен(а)» or «я сомневаюсь»
- End with a suggestion to verify the information (e.g., «проверим на официальном сайте»).
All sentences must be in Russian (Cyrillic). After each Russian sentence, include its English translation on the next line (italic).

Example requirements: keep content fictional and harmless.
